## MARKET CONTEXT

Venezuela is ELIMFILTERS®' home LATAM market. The commercial team operates from Barquisimeto, Lara — one of Venezuela's most active industrial distribution hubs. This document maps the existing distribution ecosystem so ELIMFILTERS® can identify which companies are already carrying competing filtration brands, which represent open territory, and which should be targeted as distributor candidates.

**Economic context (2026):** Venezuela's economy dollarized informally beginning in 2019 and formally consolidated by 2021. Most commercial transactions in industrial distribution are now conducted in USD. This has materially improved the commercial viability of the distribution sector — companies that survived the transition are operationally resilient and commercially active. The filtration market is recovering alongside the broader industrial economy.

**Competitive landscape:** The following brands are confirmed active in the Venezuelan market through established distributors:
- **Donaldson** — distributed by GEDISA (Caracas) and Filtros LC (Valencia)
- **WIX Filters** — has its own manufacturing plant in Valencia (Zona Industrial Sur II) since 1988
- **Baldwin Filters** — distributed by Filtros LC (Valencia), Distribuidora Miraflores (Portuguesa), Filtrolandia (national)
- **Fleetguard (Cummins)** — distributed by Filtros LC (Valencia)
- **Mann Filters** — distributed by Filtros LC (Valencia)
- **Parker Racor** — distributed by Filtros LC (Valencia)
- **GFC Performance Filters** — distributed by Distribuciones Cagua del Centro (Aragua)
- **Millard Filters (GP by Millard)** — multiple distributors nationally

**ELIMFILTERS® competitive position:** No confirmed competing brand holds exclusive coverage of the full national territory. The market has multiple overlapping distributors per brand. This creates genuine opening for a premium technical entrant.
