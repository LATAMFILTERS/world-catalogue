## HOW A DISTRIBUTOR THINKS

A distributor in Colombia, Chile, or Mexico who receives an outreach message from ELIMFILTERS® and is genuinely interested will conduct their own due diligence before investing $6,000 USD in opening stock, committing floor space, retraining a sales team, and risking their customer relationships on a brand they do not yet know.

Their evaluation follows a predictable sequence:

1. **Web search** — Can I find them? Do they look credible?
2. **Corporate verification** — Who are they legally? Do they exist?
3. **Product validation** — Can I verify the products are real and specified?
4. **Commercial terms review** — Do the numbers work for my business?
5. **Supply chain assessment** — Will I actually receive product on time?
6. **Support expectation** — What happens when something goes wrong?
7. **Reference check** — Who else is already selling this?

This checklist is written from the distributor's chair — not ELIMFILTERS' chair.

## THE HONEST ASSESSMENT

ELIMFILTERS® has built a legitimately impressive brand foundation. The Knowledge System, the Part Search tool, the technical precision of the website copy, and the articulated program structure (opening orders, exclusivity thresholds, Tier 1/2 markets) are all real assets that most brands entering a new market do not have.

The gap is not brand quality. The gap is **operational readiness**.

A distributor with an existing business and customer relationships is being asked to:
- Risk $6,000 USD in opening stock
- Realign their sales team around a new brand
- Stake their customer relationship on product quality they cannot yet verify
- Sign a legal agreement that does not yet exist
- Accept supply chain timelines that cannot be confirmed

The brand is ready to recruit. The program infrastructure is not yet ready to support what recruitment generates.

The recommended sequence: **resolve the 5 blocking actions first, then recruit.**

Recruiting before the blockers are resolved does not save time. It generates interested candidates who will ask questions that cannot be answered, and who will either wait indefinitely or walk away. That is not a pipeline. That is a list of people who chose not to trust.
