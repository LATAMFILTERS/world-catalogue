// EQUIPMENT IDENTIFICATION

Where to Find Your Serial Number

To search for the exact protection system your asset needs, locate the serial number or VIN on your equipment.

🚛

Heavy Trucks & Transport

Located on the driver-side door jamb sticker, the firewall plate, or the dashboard near the windshield. Format: 17-character VIN (e.g. 1HGCM82633A004352).

DOOR JAMB FIREWALL DASHBOARD
🛠️

Excavators & Heavy Equipment

Found on the machine serial plate near the main frame, operator's cab door frame, or on the boom arm. CAT format: CAT0615CT2XG99001.

FRAME PLATE CAB DOOR BOOM ARM
⛵

Marine & Vessels

Hull Identification Number (HIN) is located on the transom (rear) of the vessel, upper right corner. Engine serial is on the block near the oil filter.

TRANSOM (HIN) ENGINE BLOCK
⚡

Generators & Stationary

Serial plate is mounted on the generator housing panel or main frame rail. Engine serial is typically stamped near the valve cover.

HOUSING PANEL FRAME RAIL

// FILTRATION SCIENCE

Why Filter Ratings Matter

Not all protection systems are equal. Understanding the science behind our ratings is the first step to making the right decision.

BETA RATIO

What is the Beta Ratio?

The Beta Ratio (β) is the only scientifically valid metric to measure a filter's actual capture efficiency. A β₁₀ ≥ 1000 means 99.9% of all 10-micron particles are captured before they reach critical engine surfaces.

Read More →
CONTAMINATION

Silica: The Invisible Enemy

Silica (fine dust) is responsible for over 60% of premature engine wear. It abrades cylinder liners at a microscopic level, increasing oil consumption and destroying rings long before the filter shows external signs of failure.

Read More →
WATER & FUEL

Water in Diesel: Silent Injector Destroyer

Even 0.05% water contamination in diesel destroys the lubricity film that protects Common Rail injection pumps operating at 2,000+ bar. HYDROCORE™ centrifugal separation is the only effective defense.

Read More →
TCO

Total Cost of Ownership

A compromised protection system costs $0.20/hour. An unplanned engine rebuild costs $50,000+. The asset protection system is not an expense — it is the least expensive insurance policy available.
