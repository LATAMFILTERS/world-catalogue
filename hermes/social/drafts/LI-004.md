## Published copy

A micron rating by itself does not tell you how efficiently a filter removes contamination.

That is where Beta Ratio becomes useful.

Beta Ratio compares the number of particles upstream of a filter with the number of particles downstream at a specified particle size.

For example:

β10 = 2 → 50% efficiency at 10 μm
β10 = 10 → 90% efficiency at 10 μm
β10 = 200 → 99.5% efficiency at 10 μm

The important point is that two filters can both be described as “10 micron” filters and still perform very differently.

Without knowing the Beta Ratio, the micron number alone gives an incomplete picture of filtration performance.

That is why filtration specifications should be read as performance data, not just as a single micron value.
