# DISTRIBUTOR PROGRAM DECISION WORKBOOK
## ELIMFILTERS® — Version 1 Program Definition
**Date:** 2026-06-04 · **Context:** New filtration brand entering LATAM industrial markets

---

## FRAMING ASSUMPTIONS

Before the 10 decisions, three assumptions govern all recommendations:

**1. ELIMFILTERS® is not a commodity brand.**
The program is designed for distributors who sell on performance and total cost of ownership — not on price. The right distributor already carries premium brands or serves technical buyers. A distributor whose entire model is price competition will not succeed with ELIMFILTERS®.

**2. LATAM market entry is different from North American expansion.**
LATAM industrial distribution operates primarily through direct relationships, WhatsApp communication, trade credit, and in-person technical support. The program must accommodate local commercial culture. Fixed North American program structures (net-60 terms, portal-only communication, rigid SKU minimums) will not translate.

**3. The program must be manageable at small scale.**
ELIMFILTERS® Version 1 will have a limited number of active distributors. Every program element must be executable by a small commercial team. Complex structures that require a large back-office to administer are out of scope for Version 1.

## DECISION 1 — TERRITORY MODEL

### The Decision

How is the distribution territory defined and assigned?

### Recommended Option: Country-Level Territory with Industry Anchor

Assign one primary distributor per country. Each distributor is responsible for their entire national market. In large or geographically complex markets (Brazil, Mexico), subdivide by region rather than the full country.

The "industry anchor" refinement: in the first 12 months, each distributor is expected to show traction in at least one of ELIMFILTERS®' five core industries for their market (Mining, Agriculture, Marine, Oil & Gas, Heavy Transport). This anchors the activation plan without restricting what the distributor can sell.

**Priority markets for initial coverage:** Mexico, Colombia, Chile, Peru. Secondary: Brazil (Southeastern region), Ecuador, Panama, Costa Rica.

### Why This Is Recommended

Country-level exclusivity is the standard expectation of a serious industrial distributor in LATAM. A distributor investing in brand development — product knowledge, customer introductions, warehouse stock — needs the security of knowing a second distributor will not be placed next to them before they have recovered that investment.

For ELIMFILTERS® as a new brand, national exclusivity is also a recruitment tool: it signals that the program rewards committed partners, not just the first company to send an email.

### Risks

- **Underperformance risk.** A single national distributor who fails to activate the market blocks the entire country. Mitigation: performance criteria (see Decision 2) with clear consequences.
- **Coverage gaps in large markets.** A distributor in Bogotá may not effectively cover the Llanos, the coast, or the mining regions. Mitigation: industry anchor requirement ensures focus on the segments with the highest commercial potential, regardless of geography.
- **Relationship dependency.** If a national distributor is lost, the replacement process in a country-level exclusive model takes 6–12 months. Mitigation: 6-month written notice clause before termination, transition inventory protection for the outgoing distributor.

### Alternative Options

**A. Non-exclusive, open market.** Any qualified distributor can sell ELIMFILTERS® in any territory. Maximizes early market coverage. Destroys distributor motivation to invest in brand development and marketing. Not recommended for a brand that requires technical selling.

**B. Industry-vertical exclusivity.** One distributor holds mining rights, another holds agriculture rights, same territory. Complex to administer. Creates conflicts at large accounts that operate across verticals. Not recommended for Version 1.

**C. City or regional level.** Multiple distributors per country, divided by geography. Appropriate for a mature brand with high SKU depth and large market penetration. Premature for a new brand — creates territorial disputes and reduces each distributor's investment motivation.

## DECISION 2 — EXCLUSIVITY POLICY

### The Decision

What conditions govern the granting and retention of exclusive territory rights?

### Recommended Option: Conditional Exclusivity with Annual Performance Review

Exclusivity is granted at contract signing and reviewed annually. Retention requires meeting two of three performance conditions:

**Condition A — Purchase volume.** Minimum annual purchases totaling the equivalent of 6× the initial opening order value. (If the opening order is $6,000 USD, the annual threshold is $36,000 USD.) This scales with market size and does not punish a distributor in a small market.

**Condition B — Market activation.** At least one active account in the distributor's declared anchor industry within the first 6 months. Verified by a formal customer reference or a documented active order from that account.

**Condition C — Program compliance.** Distributor has completed the ELIMFILTERS® training program, maintains minimum inventory levels, and has not materially misrepresented the brand.

If a distributor fails both A and B, exclusivity converts to non-exclusive. They may continue selling but ELIMFILTERS® may appoint a second distributor in the territory.

### Why This Is Recommended

Unconditional exclusivity creates a moral hazard: a distributor locks up a territory and then does nothing, knowing no competitor will be placed. Performance-gated exclusivity maintains the incentive to activate while still offering the security that motivates serious distributors to invest.

The two-of-three structure is forgiving for Year 1 — a distributor who misses the volume threshold but has active accounts and is training diligently does not lose their territory. This is appropriate for a new brand where market development takes time.

### Risks

- **Negotiation friction.** Experienced distributors may push for unconditional exclusivity. Having a clear written policy removes this from individual negotiation.
- **Subjective compliance criteria.** "Materially misrepresented the brand" requires judgment. Mitigation: define specific prohibited behaviors in the distributor agreement (e.g., price-dumping, selling as a private label, misquoting certifications).

### Alternative Options

**A. Unconditional exclusivity.** No performance requirement. Creates passive distributors who hold the territory as an asset rather than develop it. Not recommended.

**B. 12-month grace period then hard revenue targets.** Gives the distributor a year without pressure, then enforces targets. Allows underperformers to idle for a full year before action can be taken. Not recommended.

**C. Non-exclusive from the start with a pathway to exclusivity.** Distributor earns exclusivity after 12 months of performance. Can motivate competitive behavior. Discourages early investment. Better suited for markets where multiple capable distributors exist. Consider for Brazil.

## DECISION 3 — MINIMUM OPENING ORDER

### The Decision

What is the minimum purchase required to open a new distributor account?

### Recommended Option: $6,000 USD — Minimum Opening Order (MOO)

The opening order must include representation across at least four of the five core product families: Air Filtration, Fuel Filtration, Hydraulic Filtration, Lube Oil Filtration, Cabin Filtration. No more than 50% of the opening order value may be concentrated in a single product family.

The geographic adjustment applies to smaller markets: Ecuador, Panama, Costa Rica, and similar markets may open at $3,500 USD with the same distribution across product families.

Payment terms for the opening order: 50% prepayment, 50% net-30 upon confirmed delivery.

### Why This Is Recommended

$6,000 USD is a serious but accessible commitment for an established industrial distributor in LATAM. It is large enough to demonstrate genuine commercial intent and to fund a usable demo inventory. It is small enough that a profitable two-truck distributor or a mid-size industrial parts dealer can fund it without a credit facility.

The product family spread requirement serves two purposes: it prevents a distributor from stocking only air filters (the easiest sell) and ignoring hydraulic or cabin product lines, and it ensures the distributor's customers encounter the full ELIMFILTERS® range from day one.

The 50/50 payment split is standard for new distributor relationships in LATAM where no credit history exists with the supplier.

### Risks

- **Too high for a new brand.** A distributor who has never sold ELIMFILTERS® may resist a $6,000 commitment on an unknown brand. Mitigation: the opening order is paired with the program one-pager, the Part Search demonstration, and the warranty explanation. The commit-to-stock risk is reduced because the product family spread mirrors the distributor's existing customer needs.
- **Under-stocked from day one.** $6,000 does not fund a full inventory for an active territory. Mitigation: this is an opening order, not a stocking policy. The 30-day replenishment target (Decision 4) governs ongoing inventory levels.

### Alternative Options

**A. $3,000 USD flat.** Lower barrier to entry. Risk of distributor who commits $3,000 but has no real commercial intent — the cost of abandoning is low. Appropriate for pilot markets.

**B. $10,000 USD.** Limits recruitment to larger distributors. Appropriate for Mexico City or São Paulo. Too high for smaller markets or newer distributors.

**C. No minimum — stock what you can sell.** Maximum distributor flexibility. Produces distributors with no inventory who cannot service walk-in or emergency demand. Not appropriate for a product category (filtration) where fast delivery is a competitive requirement.

## DECISION 4 — INVENTORY EXPECTATIONS

### The Decision

What ongoing inventory levels are expected of an authorized distributor?

### Recommended Option: 30-Day Rolling Coverage of the Core SKU List

ELIMFILTERS® defines a Core SKU List for each priority market — the top 80 SKUs by estimated demand for the distributor's declared anchor industry. The distributor is expected to maintain 30-day coverage (based on trailing 90-day sales) for every SKU on the Core List.

The Core SKU List is provided by ELIMFILTERS® at onboarding and updated annually based on sales data and cross-reference trends.

A distributor who drops below 30-day coverage on more than 20% of Core SKUs for two consecutive months is contacted proactively by the account manager — not penalized — to identify the cause (cash flow, demand, supplier delay) and develop a restock plan.

### Why This Is Recommended

30-day rolling coverage is the standard expectation in LATAM industrial distribution. It allows the distributor to fulfill same-day or next-day requests from their customers without special ordering, which is the primary competitive advantage of a local distributor over direct-from-supplier purchasing.

Defining coverage against a Core SKU List (rather than the full catalog) is realistic for a new distributor who is still learning which SKUs move in their market. The 80-SKU core represents the high-velocity items across the anchor industry — a manageable stocking commitment.

### Risks

- **Core SKU List accuracy.** If the initial list is wrong for a specific market, the distributor stocks the wrong items. Mitigation: the list is advisory in Year 1, with the distributor free to substitute based on observed demand, provided they report the substitution pattern so the list can be refined.
- **Cash flow pressure.** Maintaining 30-day coverage requires capital. Mitigation: ELIMFILTERS® offers net-30 restock terms after the opening order credit history is established (typically after 2 restocking cycles).

### Alternative Options

**A. No inventory requirement.** Distributor orders as needed from ELIMFILTERS®. Zero stock risk for the distributor. Means the distributor has nothing to show customers and cannot quote delivery without checking with the supplier. Eliminates the primary value proposition of local distribution.

**B. 60-day coverage.** More buffer, less cash pressure. Appropriate for markets with long lead times from ELIMFILTERS®' supply point. Consider for Brazil and Southern Cone markets where freight times are longer.

**C. Consignment inventory.** ELIMFILTERS® places stock at the distributor's warehouse; the distributor pays upon sale. Eliminates inventory risk for the distributor. Significant cash flow and administrative burden on ELIMFILTERS® Version 1. Not feasible at small program scale.

## DECISION 5 — TRAINING PROGRAM

### The Decision

What training is required of a new distributor, and how is it delivered?

### Recommended Option: Three-Module Online Program with Optional On-Site Visit

**Module 1 — Product Knowledge (4 hours)**
Covers the 9 protection architectures, the contamination-to-product-family logic (what problem connects to what technology connects to what product family), and the Core SKU List. Delivered via the ELIMFILTERS® Knowledge System pages and a live or recorded video session. Tested by a short qualification quiz.

**Module 2 — Sales Tools (2 hours)**
Covers the Part Search tool, the cross-reference workflow, the warranty program, and the standard objection responses (OEM equivalence, premium pricing justification, competitor comparison). Delivered as a walkthrough of the digital tools with practice exercises.

**Module 3 — Applications Workshop (2 hours)**
Industry-specific case studies relevant to the distributor's anchor industry. Uses the Knowledge System contamination pages as source material. Covers how to qualify an account, how to conduct a filter audit, and how to present the TCO argument. Delivered as a live session with the ELIMFILTERS® technical team.

**On-Site Visit.** For distributors in Tier 1 markets (Mexico, Colombia, Chile, Peru) who have completed all three modules and placed a restock order, ELIMFILTERS® offers one on-site technical presentation to the distributor's top three prospects within the first year. This is a strong recruitment incentive that costs ELIMFILTERS® one travel event per market activation.

### Why This Is Recommended

ELIMFILTERS®' competitive advantage is technical. A distributor who cannot explain contamination control, ISO standards, and TCO will sell ELIMFILTERS® on price — and lose. Training is not a compliance formality; it is what converts an average distributor into a differentiated one.

The three-module structure is manageable: 8 hours total is a realistic ask for a committed distributor. Splitting into three sessions allows completion over 2–3 weeks without disrupting the distributor's business.

Using the Knowledge System as training curriculum is efficient — the content already exists, is accurate, and can be updated without rebuilding a separate training program.

### Risks

- **Distributor sales team turnover.** If the distributor trains one person who then leaves, the knowledge is gone. Mitigation: encourage the distributor to train at minimum two people. The online modules can be retaken at no cost.
- **Quality of live sessions.** Module 3 requires a live technical team member. If that resource is unavailable, training is delayed. Mitigation: record a reference version of Module 3 so it can be consumed asynchronously when scheduling prevents a live session.
- **Language.** Technical terms in English are barriers in Spanish-speaking markets. Mitigation: use the Spanish version of the Knowledge System for training materials; deliver live sessions in Spanish.

### Alternative Options

**A. In-person only training.** Higher quality, stronger relationship. Not scalable in Year 1 with multiple simultaneous market activations and a small commercial team.

**B. No formal training requirement.** Distributor self-learns. Produces inconsistent selling behavior and misrepresentation risk. Not appropriate for a technically complex brand.

**C. Annual distributor conference.** Brings all distributors together once a year. Excellent for Year 2+ once there are multiple active partners. Premature for Version 1.

## DECISION 6 — TECHNICAL SUPPORT MODEL

### The Decision

How does ELIMFILTERS® support distributors with technical questions, cross-reference requests, and application engineering?

### Recommended Option: Tiered Technical Support via WhatsApp + Email

**Tier 1 — Self-Service (immediate).**
Part Search tool handles the majority of cross-reference requests. The Knowledge System handles standards, contamination mode, and application questions. Distributors are expected to use these tools as their first step.

**Tier 2 — Distributor Support Channel (response within 24 hours, business days).**
A dedicated WhatsApp number (or group) for authorized distributors. Used for: cross-reference requests not found in Part Search, unusual application questions, warranty claims, competitive displacement questions. Staffed by the ELIMFILTERS® technical team.

WhatsApp is the dominant business communication channel in LATAM. A support channel that requires email-only will be used significantly less and will be perceived as less responsive regardless of actual response times.

**Tier 3 — Engineering Escalation (response within 48 hours).**
For complex application questions (custom fluid systems, unusual equipment platforms, off-standard operating conditions). Handled via email with structured intake: equipment make/model, operating hours, current filter part number, observed problem, and expected outcome.

**Response Time Commitments:**
- Tier 2 (WhatsApp): within 24 business hours
- Tier 3 (Email escalation): within 48 business hours

### Why This Is Recommended

The greatest source of friction in LATAM distributor relationships is slow or inaccessible technical support. A distributor who cannot get a fast answer on a cross-reference while a customer is waiting at the counter will eventually stop asking and default to a competitor's product.

WhatsApp support resolves this. It matches the communication preference of the market, reduces response latency compared to email, and allows photo/document sharing (distributor photographs an unknown filter housing; ELIMFILTERS® identifies the cross-reference in minutes).

### Risks

- **WhatsApp channel boundary management.** A support WhatsApp without defined hours becomes a 24/7 expectation. Mitigation: set clear office hours (e.g., 8:00–18:00 Venezuela time) and communicate them at onboarding. Use WhatsApp Business with automated after-hours messages.
- **Volume growth.** One support channel becomes unmanageable beyond 15–20 active distributors. Mitigation: transition to a CRM-based support ticket system (HubSpot, Freshdesk, Zoho) when distributor count reaches that threshold.

### Alternative Options

**A. Email only.** LATAM commercial culture does not prioritize email. Response latency will feel slow even if it is fast by North American standards. Not recommended as the primary channel.

**B. Dedicated support portal.** Login-required ticket system from day one. Creates friction for distributors accustomed to WhatsApp. Appropriate for Year 2+.

**C. Phone support.** High-touch, effective. Not scalable with a small technical team across multiple time zones. Consider for Tier 3 escalations for the most critical accounts.

## DECISION 7 — MARKETING SUPPORT MODEL

### The Decision

What marketing support does ELIMFILTERS® provide to authorized distributors?

### Recommended Option: Digital-First Support Kit with Co-Branding and On-Site Activation Option

**Distributed to all authorized distributors at onboarding:**

1. **Digital product brochure (PDF)** — Brand overview, product family descriptions, performance claims, ISO references. Available in Spanish and Portuguese. Distributor can co-brand with their company logo and contact information.

2. **Technology one-pagers (PDF, one per protection architecture)** — Technical selling sheets explaining each technology's contamination target, ISO standard, and application. Used by the distributor's sales team in customer conversations.

3. **Social media content kit** — 12–24 pre-made posts in Spanish/Portuguese (images + captions) suitable for LinkedIn and Instagram. Covers contamination education, product launches, application tips. Quarterly refresh.

4. **Customer presentation template** — A co-brandable slide deck for customer presentations. Covers contamination, TCO argument, and product recommendation framework.

5. **Part Search tool access and promotional asset** — "Find Your Filter" graphic and link. Shared with distributor customers to drive direct cross-reference lookups.

**On-site activation support (Tier 1 markets only):**
ELIMFILTERS® will co-present with the distributor at the distributor's top 3 prospects in Year 1 (combined with the on-site training visit from Decision 5). This is the highest-value marketing support activity: a technical presentation delivered jointly by ELIMFILTERS® and the distributor builds credibility and shortens the sales cycle.

**What is NOT in the Version 1 support kit:**
- Printed product catalogs (cost, logistics, and updating burden are not justified at early scale)
- Trade show participation support (deferred to Year 2)
- Co-op marketing budget (deferred to Year 2 after volume establishes the financial basis)

### Why This Is Recommended

Digital-first is cost-effective for ELIMFILTERS® at this stage and practical for distributors who primarily sell through direct sales visits, not retail channels. PDFs are distributable, updatable, and do not require print-run commitments.

The co-branding option is a low-cost but high-perceived-value support element. A distributor who can put their logo on ELIMFILTERS® technical materials gets materials they would otherwise have to create themselves.

The on-site activation visit is the highest-ROI marketing investment ELIMFILTERS® can make per market. One person traveling to meet 3 accounts in Bogotá converts faster than any digital campaign at early scale.

### Risks

- **Content maintenance.** PDFs become outdated. A distributor sharing a year-old brochure with wrong specs damages credibility. Mitigation: version-date every document, distribute updates via the distributor WhatsApp channel when materials are refreshed.
- **Co-brand misuse.** Distributors may alter co-branded materials to make unsupported claims. Mitigation: provide editable files (PowerPoint, Canva templates) with defined editable zones only, and include a clause in the distributor agreement about materials modification.

### Alternative Options

**A. Full print catalog.** High distributor preference. Expensive, requires annual reprinting, cannot be easily updated. Defer to Year 2 when the SKU range and pricing are stable enough to print at scale.

**B. No marketing support.** Distributor handles their own marketing. Produces inconsistent brand presentation. Appropriate only for established markets where the brand speaks for itself. Not for new entry.

**C. Co-op budget from day one.** ELIMFILTERS® contributes a percentage of purchases to a distributor marketing fund. Administratively complex at small scale. Consider at Year 2 when purchase volumes are sufficient to justify the fund management overhead.

## DECISION 8 — DISTRIBUTOR QUALIFICATION CRITERIA

### The Decision

What minimum profile must a company meet to be considered for authorized distributor status?

### Recommended Option: Five-Criterion Minimum Qualification

A company must meet all five criteria to be approved as an authorized distributor:

**Criterion 1 — Industrial Customer Base.**
The applicant must demonstrate an existing customer base in at least one of ELIMFILTERS®' 12 industries (Mining, Agriculture, Marine, Construction, Oil & Gas, Power Generation, Transportation & Fleets, Automotive, Bus & Coach, Railway, Waste Management, Manufacturing). This is verified through company description, customer references, or current product lines sold. A retail auto parts shop with no industrial customers does not qualify.

**Criterion 2 — Technical Sales Capability.**
The applicant must have at least one person on their sales team who currently sells technical industrial products (filters, lubricants, hydraulic components, pneumatic systems, or similar) to industrial accounts. This does not require a professional engineer — it requires someone who can have a technical conversation with a fleet manager or a maintenance supervisor.

**Criterion 3 — Minimum Operational History.**
The company must have been operating for at least 2 years. Startups without an established commercial track record are not appropriate for Version 1. This is not about company size — it is about demonstrating that the organization exists, pays its obligations, and has survived early-stage risk.

**Criterion 4 — Geographic Coverage.**
The applicant must be able to demonstrate commercial activity in the declared territory. A company claiming national coverage in Colombia must have at minimum two active cities or a logistics infrastructure that serves their declared market.

**Criterion 5 — Financial Capacity for Opening Order.**
The applicant must be able to fund the opening order without a credit facility from ELIMFILTERS®. No credit check is performed, but the 50% prepayment requirement filters applicants who cannot fund even a modest initial commitment.

### Why This Is Recommended

These five criteria filter out two types of unqualified applicants that are common in LATAM industrial distribution outreach: (1) retail distributors who buy on impulse because the brand looks interesting but have no industrial customers, and (2) brokers or agents who want the exclusivity without the inventory commitment.

Neither of these applicant types will develop the ELIMFILTERS® brand. A thorough upfront qualification saves both parties from a failed 12-month experiment.

None of the criteria require large company size, existing filtration experience, or financial documentation. This keeps the program accessible to the right mid-size distributors who often outperform larger competitors because they are more invested in each brand they carry.

### Risks

- **Overly restricting entry.** In some markets, the ideal distributor may be slightly newer or cover a narrower territory. Mitigation: the criteria are guidelines, not hard rules. The commercial team evaluates borderline cases on total profile rather than individual criterion failure.
- **Subjective verification.** "Technical sales capability" and "geographic coverage" are not easily verified from an application form. Mitigation: the discovery call (part of the onboarding process) is where these are validated in conversation, not on paper.

### Alternative Options

**A. Lower the bar significantly.** Accept any applicant with a company registration and a bank account. Maximizes volume of applicants. Produces high volume of non-performing relationships. Not appropriate for a brand that requires technical selling and active market development.

**B. Require proof of revenue above a threshold.** Filters to larger companies only. Misses the mid-size distributors who are often the best brand builders for new market entries. Not recommended.

**C. Open application to agents / brokers (no inventory).** Agents represent the brand without carrying stock. Appropriate for markets where establishing a stocking distributor quickly is impossible. Consider as a bridge strategy in markets where no qualified stocking distributor exists (e.g., Paraguay, Bolivia).

## DECISION 9 — ONBOARDING PROCESS

### The Decision

What is the defined sequence from application approval to first sale?

### Recommended Option: 30-Day Activation Sequence

**Day 1–3 — Welcome and Account Setup**

- Distributor agreement signed
- Opening order payment (50% prepayment) received
- Welcome email with: account manager contact, WhatsApp support number, Knowledge System access, Part Search link, training schedule
- Digital materials kit delivered (brochures, presentation templates, social content)

**Day 4–10 — Training (Module 1 + 2)**

- Distributor completes Product Knowledge module (online, self-paced, 4 hours)
- Distributor completes Sales Tools module (online, walkthrough, 2 hours)
- Account manager confirms completion and answers first-round questions via WhatsApp

**Day 11–20 — First Order Ships + Applications Workshop**

- Opening order shipped from supply point
- Module 3 (Applications Workshop) delivered as live or recorded session with ELIMFILTERS® technical team
- Distributor receives Core SKU List and 30-day inventory coverage guidance

**Day 21–25 — First Inventory Received**

- Distributor confirms receipt and product condition
- Any packaging or documentation issues resolved within 48 hours
- Distributor begins placing initial stock in their system

**Day 26–30 — Commercial Activation**

- Distributor identifies first 3–5 target accounts in their anchor industry
- Account manager reviews target list and provides any relevant cross-reference support
- First customer visits or calls initiated by distributor

**Day 30 — 30-Day Check-In Call**

- Account manager and distributor review: training completion, inventory status, first target accounts, immediate support needs
- Establish 90-day sales target (first milestone, not a performance gate)

### Why This Is Recommended

The first 30 days determine whether a distributor relationship succeeds. A distributor who receives inventory but no training will sell the wrong products or sell them poorly. A distributor who completes training but does not receive inventory cannot demonstrate the product. The sequence above ensures both arrive together.

The 30-day check-in call is the most important element. It is the account manager's opportunity to identify early problems (the distributor's team hasn't been trained yet, the inventory is sitting in a back room, the target accounts are not in the right industry) before they compound.

### Risks

- **Distributor delays.** The distributor completes the application but takes 3 weeks to sign the agreement. This extends the timeline. Mitigation: set a 10-business-day deadline for agreement execution after approval; unapproved slots do not hold territory exclusivity.
- **Shipping delays (especially within LATAM).** The opening order may take longer than expected to arrive. Mitigation: maintain realistic transit time expectations by country at the time of opening order placement. Do not start Module 3 (inventory-dependent training) until the shipment is confirmed in transit.

### Alternative Options

**A. Accelerated 2-week onboarding.** Compresses training and delivery into 14 days. Appropriate only if logistics allow and the distributor has dedicated resources. Risky if rushed.

**B. No formal onboarding structure.** Distributor receives the agreement, places an order, and self-starts. Common in commodity distribution. Inappropriate for a technically-sold brand where the distributor's quality of engagement determines commercial outcomes.

## DECISION 10 — FIRST 90-DAY EXPECTATIONS

### The Decision

What is a realistic definition of success for a new ELIMFILTERS® distributor in the first 90 days?

### Recommended Option: Activity-Based Milestones, Not Revenue Targets

For a new brand in a new market, the 90-day window is market development time, not revenue harvest time. The following milestones define a successful first 90 days:

**Training milestone (Day 30 target):**
All three training modules completed by at least two members of the distributor's team.

**Inventory milestone (Day 30 target):**
Opening order received and stocked. At least 60% of the Core SKU List represented in the distributor's inventory system.

**Account activation milestone (Day 60 target):**
Minimum 5 active prospect accounts identified in the anchor industry. "Active" means the distributor has made direct contact (visit, call, or message) and has documented a filtration need or current product being used.

**First conversion milestone (Day 90 target):**
Minimum 1 customer has purchased ELIMFILTERS® product. This can be a sample order, a trial of one product family, or a first replacement order. The first conversion establishes proof of concept in the territory.

**Communication milestone (ongoing):**
Distributor responds to account manager check-ins within 48 hours and completes the 30-day and 60-day review calls.

### What is NOT expected in the first 90 days

- Full territory market penetration
- Consistent monthly reorder volumes
- Large fleet or key account conversions (these take 3–6 months of relationship building)
- Profitability from the ELIMFILTERS® line (the investment phase)

### Why This Is Recommended

Revenue targets in the first 90 days for a new brand in a new market create the wrong behavior. A distributor under revenue pressure will discount to close quickly, damage the premium positioning, and attract price-sensitive customers who will switch at the first competitive price. Activity milestones create the behaviors that produce durable commercial relationships: training, account identification, and first conversions at the right price.

One genuine first conversion — a maintenance supervisor who buys an air filter, installs it, and calls back to say the equipment is running better — is worth more commercially than $10,000 of one-time sales to a price buyer.

### Risks

- **Distributor inactivity hidden by activity.** A distributor who reports 5 accounts "contacted" but has no real engagement can technically meet the milestone without doing meaningful work. Mitigation: the 60-day review call explores the quality of account engagement, not just the count. Account manager asks: "What did you learn about their current filter brand? What is the equipment? What is the purchase decision timeline?"
- **First conversion takes longer than 90 days.** In some markets or industries (large mining operations, government fleets), the decision cycle is genuinely longer than 90 days. Mitigation: the 90-day conversion target is a milestone, not a performance gate. Missing it triggers a conversation, not a penalty.

### Alternative Options

**A. Revenue-based 90-day targets.** "Sell $X in the first 90 days or we review the relationship." Creates discounting pressure. Not appropriate for a new brand.

**B. No formal 90-day expectations.** Distributor operates without accountability for the first quarter. Some will do nothing for three months and then blame the brand. Not appropriate.

30-day activation sequence from agreement signing to first commercial call. Key milestones: training completion by Day 10, inventory received by Day 21, first account visits by Day 30.

Activity-based, not revenue-based: training complete (Day 30), Core SKU list 60% stocked (Day 30), 5 active prospects identified in anchor industry (Day 60), 1 first customer conversion (Day 90).

### DECISIONS STILL REQUIRED (Not answered by this document)

The following decisions cannot be made from the outside and must be resolved internally before the program is communicated to distributor candidates:

1. **Pricing structure.** What are the wholesale price tiers? What margin does a standard distributor earn at the opening order volume versus at annual volume threshold? This determines whether the program is commercially attractive before any candidate applies.

2. **Supply chain logistics.** What is the cost, transit time, and reliability of delivery from the supply point to each Tier 1 market? This determines whether the 30-day inventory coverage requirement is realistic. A distributor in Santiago cannot hold 30-day coverage if replenishment takes 45 days.

3. **Kleo Technologies / ELIMFILTERS® LLC relationship.** As identified in the commercial audit, the legal entity that will sign the distributor agreement must be clearly identified, and the relationship to Kleo Technologies must be articulated to candidates doing due diligence.

4. **Warranty claim process.** The warranty terms are documented. The claim submission process — how does a distributor file a warranty claim, what documentation is required, and what is the replacement timeline — is not documented and must be defined before distributors are active.

5. **Initial commercial team capacity.** The onboarding process and technical support model assume an account manager and a technical support person are available. The program cannot be activated without confirming who fills these roles and what their capacity is.
