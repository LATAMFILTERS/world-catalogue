# ELIMFILTERS Business Platform — Project Manifesto

## Mission

The ELIMFILTERS Business Platform (EBP) preserves and operationalizes ELIMFILTERS product engineering, manufacturer intelligence, costs, distributor access, quality history, and business decisions.

It is a private business platform. It is not part of the public corporate website experience.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. Documentation is the source of truth; code implements the approved documentation.
2. No module is implemented without documented scope, business rules, data model, permissions, validations, tests, and acceptance criteria.
3. Existing canonical product and SKU records remain the single source of truth.
4. ELIMFILTERS owns product requirements, technology assignments, engineering targets, packaging policy, and approvals.
5. Manufacturers may submit offered values, evidence, prices, and deviations but may never overwrite ELIMFILTERS requirements.
6. Every manufacturer has a permanent confidential ELIMFILTERS manufacturer code.
7. Supplier identity, FOB cost, internal engineering requirements, margins, and other distributors' data are never exposed to distributors or public routes.
8. Every significant decision is auditable and versioned.
9. Changes are developed in isolated branches and must pass documented tests and audits before merge.
10. No module is considered complete until documentation and implementation match.

## Definition of done

A phase is complete only when its documentation, implementation, migrations, security controls, tests, audit report, and implementation status are aligned and approved.
