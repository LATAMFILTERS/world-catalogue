# ELIMFILTERS® Axial Seal Air Filters — Product Identity & Printing Standard

**Standard ID:** ELIM-AIR-AXIAL-SEAL-PRINT-001  
**Status:** APPROVED  
**Revision:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 2026-09-10  
**Owner:** ELIMFILTERS® / Kleo Technology LLC  

## 1. Purpose

This document controls the visual identity, printed identification, and manufacturing rules for ELIMFILTERS® cylindrical radial air filters using an axial sealing system.

It applies separately to:

- **Primary Air Filter** — outer radial element.
- **Secondary Air Filter** — inner / safety radial element.

This standard is specific to axial-seal cylindrical air filters. Panel air filters are a separate product family and must use a separate identity and printing standard.

## 2. Controlled master assets

All official visual assets must be taken from the controlled repository:

`frontend/public/assets/`

Required assets:

- ELIMFILTERS® master identity: `frontend/public/assets/Logotipo Elimfilters® con eslogan corporativo.png`
- MACROCORE™ technology identity: `frontend/public/assets/MACROCORE_final.avif`

Official logos must not be recreated, retyped, approximated, stretched, recolored arbitrarily, or substituted.

## 3. Product construction

The family covered by this standard is a **cylindrical radial air-filter element with axial seal**.

The construction may include:

- pleated filter media;
- external support mesh or liner when required;
- upper and lower molded end caps;
- central airflow opening;
- integrated axial sealing surface / gasket;
- primary outer element or secondary inner/safety element.

The artwork must adapt to the real production geometry. The physical filter must never be altered merely to make a generic artwork template fit.

## 4. Printing location

Printing is permitted only on the approved end-cap surface.

The following are prohibited:

- printing directly on the pleated filter media;
- adhesive labels on the filter media;
- printing on the axial sealing/gasket surface;
- graphics crossing the airflow opening;
- graphics entering protected sealing areas.

The useful print surface is the annular area between the center airflow opening and the outside end-cap edge, excluding all engineering no-print areas.

## 5. ELIMFILTERS® logo treatment — curved / half-moon layout

The approved ELIMFILTERS® presentation on axial-seal air filters follows the circular geometry of the end cap.

The logo is positioned as a **curved half-moon / arc-following composition** across the upper section of the end cap.

This curved appearance belongs to the logo placement itself. It must not be simulated with an additional colored crescent, decorative stripe, background mark, or painted arc.

`TOTAL ASSETS PROTECTION` accompanies the master identity and follows the approved circular hierarchy.

The logo must remain optically centered to the airflow opening axis.

## 6. Yellow-color prohibition on product

**Yellow is not permitted as a product print color or decorative surface color under this standard.**

This specifically prohibits:

- yellow crescent graphics;
- yellow bands;
- yellow decorative lines;
- yellow logo fills;
- yellow end-cap paint;
- yellow identification marks added for aesthetic purposes.

Yellow may continue to exist in corporate/digital documentation where governed separately, but it is not to be painted or printed on these axial-seal filter products.

## 7. Primary and secondary identification

Primary and secondary elements must be visually distinguished by printed Product Type.

### Primary element

- SKU format: `EA` + 5 digits.
- Example: `EA32567`.
- Printed Product Type: **PRIMARY AIR FILTER**.
- Technology identity: **MACROCORE™**.

### Secondary / safety element

- SKU format: `EA` + 5 digits + `S`.
- Example: `EA32567S`.
- Printed Product Type: **SECONDARY AIR FILTER**.
- Technology identity: **MACROCORE™**.

The Primary/Secondary designation is mandatory and must not be inferred only from physical size or appearance.

## 8. Approved circular hierarchy

The approved end-cap hierarchy is:

1. ELIMFILTERS® master identity — upper curved / half-moon zone.
2. `TOTAL ASSETS PROTECTION` — directly associated with the master identity.
3. SKU — lateral radial zone.
4. Product Type — lower radial zone.
5. MACROCORE™ — opposing lateral / lower radial zone according to available geometry.

All elements must be centered to the cap axis and kept within the usable annular print area.

No element may overlap the center airflow opening or axial-seal geometry.

## 9. Technology identity

Technology for this product family is:

**MACROCORE™**

The official technology asset must be taken from:

`frontend/public/assets/MACROCORE_final.avif`

The technology identity must not be recreated as generic text when a production-ready controlled asset is available.

## 10. Print color

**Internal ink code:** `EF-INK-002`  
**Name:** ELIMFILTERS Neutral Grey  
**Pantone communication reference:** Pantone Cool Gray 11 C

Pantone is a production communication reference only. Final approval must be made against the physical Golden Sample and released supplier ink formulation.

No yellow ink is permitted on the product under this release.

## 11. Printing process

Acceptable processes may include:

- pad printing;
- silk-screen printing;
- another factory-qualified process compatible with the end-cap material and service environment.

The final production process must meet released requirements for:

- adhesion;
- abrasion resistance;
- chemical resistance;
- temperature resistance;
- cure;
- legibility;
- registration;
- durability throughout normal handling and installation.

## 12. Dimensional release data required per SKU

The factory release sheet must record actual engineering dimensions for each SKU, including:

- Overall Height — `H`
- Outside Diameter — `OD`
- Inside Diameter / Airflow Opening — `ID`
- Axial Seal Outer Diameter — `AS-OD`
- Axial Seal Inner Diameter — `AS-ID`
- End Cap Diameter
- Usable Annular Print Area Outer Diameter
- Usable Annular Print Area Inner Diameter
- End Cap Material
- Axial Seal Material
- Printed-Art Orientation
- SKU
- Primary / Secondary designation
- Technology
- Ink manufacturer and code
- Print process
- Cure conditions
- Golden Sample ID
- Revision and approval date

No generic millimeter dimensions are frozen in this brand standard. Each production SKU must use its released engineering drawing and actual end-cap geometry.

## 13. End-cap artwork adaptation

Artwork may scale to different filter diameters, but the hierarchy must remain intact.

Do not scale all components blindly as one group. When print area is restricted, preserve in this order:

1. ELIMFILTERS® identity legibility.
2. SKU legibility.
3. Primary / Secondary Product Type.
4. MACROCORE™ identity.
5. Supporting tagline at the minimum approved readable size.

Any layout that cannot fit these mandatory elements without entering sealing or airflow zones requires an SKU-specific artwork review before release.

## 14. Both-end printing

When both end caps are geometrically identical and both are approved printable surfaces, the same artwork may be repeated on both ends.

If one end incorporates a sealing surface, closed end, structural feature, valve feature, installation feature, or other incompatible geometry, that end must follow the SKU-specific engineering release and must not be printed merely for visual symmetry.

## 15. Golden Sample and production release

Mass production must not begin until:

- artwork has been generated from controlled assets;
- dimensions have been verified against the real filter;
- ink and printing process have been qualified;
- no-print areas have been checked;
- Primary / Secondary designation has been verified;
- MACROCORE™ identity has been verified;
- a physical Golden Sample has been approved.

The Golden Sample becomes the visual manufacturing reference but does not replace the engineering drawing or released dimensional specification.

## 16. Prohibited uses

The following are explicitly prohibited:

- yellow decoration on the product;
- straight logo placement when the approved circular end-cap format requires the curved half-moon layout;
- invented crescent symbols around the logo;
- rectangular labels on filter media;
- printing on filter media;
- printing on the axial sealing surface;
- incorrect Primary / Secondary identification;
- omission or substitution of MACROCORE™ where required;
- uncontrolled or recreated logo files;
- distortion of filter geometry in production artwork.
