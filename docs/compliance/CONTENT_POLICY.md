\# Content Policy



Effective Date: June 2026



\## Purpose



This policy defines the content standards applicable to ELIMFILTERS digital platforms, AI Labs, Part Search and future intelligence systems.



\## Permitted Content



The following content may be published, processed or displayed:



\* Product information

\* Technical specifications

\* OEM references

\* Cross-reference data

\* Industrial maintenance information

\* Asset protection guidance

\* Educational content

\* Customer-generated content authorized by its owner



\## Restricted Content



The following content may not be uploaded, published or distributed:



\* Copyright-infringing material

\* Confidential information

\* Trade secrets belonging to third parties

\* Fraudulent content

\* Malicious software

\* Illegal content

\* False or misleading technical claims



\## Intellectual Property



Users may only submit content for which they possess the necessary rights, licenses or permissions.



\## AI Generated Content



AI-generated content must be treated as informational assistance and not as certified engineering, legal or regulatory advice.



\## Content Removal



ELIMFILTERS reserves the right to remove content that violates company policies, legal requirements or intellectual property rights.



\## Reporting Violations



Suspected violations may be reported to ELIMFILTERS for review.



\## Enforcement



Violations may result in:



\* Content removal

\* Account restrictions

\* Suspension of access

\* Legal action when appropriate
