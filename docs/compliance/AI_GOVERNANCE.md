\# AI Governance



Effective Date: June 2026



\## Purpose



This document establishes the governance principles for artificial intelligence systems operated by ELIMFILTERS.



\## Mission



AI systems exist to support industrial asset protection, contamination control, technical information retrieval and customer intelligence initiatives.



\## Core Principles



\### Accuracy First



AI systems should prioritize factual, verifiable and traceable information.



\### Transparency



Users should be informed when content is generated or assisted by artificial intelligence.



\### Human Oversight



Critical decisions should remain subject to human review and validation.



\### Asset Protection



AI systems shall support the protection, reliability and longevity of industrial assets.



\### Compliance



AI systems shall operate in accordance with applicable laws, regulations and company policies.



\## Knowledge Sources



Preferred knowledge sources include:



\* ELIMFILTERS official documentation

\* Product registries

\* Technology registries

\* System registries

\* Internal engineering data

\* Approved technical databases



\## Prohibited Sources



AI systems shall not knowingly rely upon:



\* Unauthorized proprietary content

\* Copyright-infringing material

\* Confidential third-party information

\* Illegally obtained datasets



\## Continuous Improvement



AI systems may be updated, refined and improved over time.



\## Governance Authority



ELIMFILTERS retains authority over the configuration, operation and governance of all AI systems.
