# ELIMFILTERS_DISTRIBUTOR_OPERATING_MODEL

## Purpose

This document defines the operating model governing ELIMFILTERS distributors worldwide.

The objective is to transform distributors from product resellers into Asset Protection Partners.

---

# Core Principle

Distributors do not exist to move inventory.

Distributors exist to protect customer assets.

Inventory is a consequence.

Protection is the mission.
