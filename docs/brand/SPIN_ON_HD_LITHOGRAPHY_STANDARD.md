# ELIMFILTERS® — SPIN-ON HD LITHOGRAPHY GRID SYSTEM

**Document ID:** EF-BRAND-SPINON-07.1  
**Status:** Draft controlled standard — pending physical Golden Sample approval  
**Owner:** KLEO Technology LLC / ELIMFILTERS®  
**Applies to:** All ELIMFILTERS® Spin-On HD filters  

## 1. Master reference

EL82100 is the **anchor/master reference** used to define visual hierarchy and proportional behavior for the entire Spin-On HD family. It is not a SKU-specific standard.

The physical geometry of each SKU controls the final artwork adaptation. Artwork must never be stretched or reduced by eye.

## 2. Approved visual hierarchy

The front-face sequence is fixed:

1. Tightening instructions / arrows, when required by the engineering specification.
2. Official ELIMFILTERS® logo.
3. `TOTAL ASSETS PROTECTION`.
4. SKU.
5. Product type.
6. Approved technology identity.

No `GERMAN QUALITY` wording is permitted.

## 3. Two-face repetition rule

The cylindrical wrap uses **two visually identical principal faces**.

- There is **no separate rear marketing lithography**.
- The opposite side repeats the same approved front-face hierarchy.
- Both principal faces must present the same logo, SKU, product type and technology identity.
- A seam / process no-print zone may remain where required by the lithography process.
- Horizontal product presentations used for advertising/rendering remain unprinted unless specifically released otherwise.

## 4. Body coating

**Internal color code:** `EF-COL-001`  
**Name:** `ELIMFILTERS TITANIUM GREY`  
**Finish:** dark neutral-warm titanium grey, satin / low semi-gloss  
**Powder system:** polyester powder coating  
**Target gloss:** 25–35 GU @ 60°  
**Target dry film thickness:** 60–80 μm

The final manufacturing reference is the approved physical powder-coated master panel plus measured CIE L*a*b* values and the released supplier powder code/formulation.

Pantone / HEX references are communication aids only and do not replace the physical master panel.

## 5. Lithography color

**Internal ink code:** `EF-INK-002`  
**Name:** `ELIMFILTERS WARM TECHNICAL GREY`  
**Pantone reference:** `PANTONE Warm Gray 4 C`  
**Use:** all primary Spin-On HD lithography on EF-COL-001 body color.

This replaces the previously proposed all-black print treatment for the current Spin-On HD visual system.

The selected grey must remain visibly distinct from the Titanium Grey housing while preserving a restrained industrial appearance. Before mass production, the plant must submit a physical ink drawdown / printed sample on the released EF-COL-001 powder-coated panel for ELIMFILTERS approval.

No decorative yellow or white lithography is part of this Spin-On HD canister system unless a future controlled revision explicitly authorizes it.

## 6. Dimensional input required per SKU

Each factory release sheet must record:

- Overall height — `H`
- Outside diameter — `OD`
- Printable height — `PH`
- Printable circumference — `PC`
- Top dead zone — `TDZ`
- Bottom dead zone — `BDZ`
- Seam / no-print zone — `SNP`

Printable circumference is derived from actual body OD and the process-specific no-print/seam allowance. No fixed millimeter layout may be assumed across the family.

## 7. Proportional grid

Use `PH` as the vertical coordinate system and the usable circumferential width as the horizontal coordinate system.

Baseline proportional zones from the EL82100 anchor must be calibrated to actual physical measurements before final factory release:

- **Zone A — Installation:** upper tightening instruction area.
- **Zone B — Brand:** ELIMFILTERS® + TOTAL ASSETS PROTECTION.
- **Zone C — SKU:** primary part-number identification.
- **Zone D — Product type:** Lube Filter / Hydraulic Filter / Fuel Filter / etc.
- **Zone E — Technology:** approved technology identity.

The hierarchy remains constant across Small, Medium, Large and Tall/Narrow body classes; spacing adapts to the real printable geometry.

## 8. Technology mapping

Current controlled mapping for Spin-On applications:

- Lube / Oil → `SYNTRAX™`
- Hydraulic → `NANOFORCE™`
- Fuel particulate → `SYNTAPORE™`
- Fuel / water separation → `HYDROCORE™` where applicable
- Coolant → `THERMACORE™`

Technology artwork must always come from the official master asset repository:

`world-catalogue/frontend/public/assets/`

No logo redraw, approximate font recreation, or unofficial technology mark is permitted.

## 9. Tightening instructions

Tightening arrows/instructions are mandatory when required by the product engineering specification.

The exact wording, number of turns and installation instruction are SKU-specific engineering data and must not be invented from visual references.

## 10. Factory release gate

Mass production is not authorized until all of the following are complete:

- Final dimensional data entered for the SKU.
- Released artwork placed against the real printable geometry.
- EF-COL-001 body coating physically approved.
- EF-INK-002 print sample physically approved on the real coating.
- Print position and legibility approved.
- Adhesion and cure approved.
- Abrasion resistance approved.
- Golden Sample approved and identified.

The first fully approved physical product becomes the **Golden Sample** for visual manufacturing control. It does not replace dimensional or engineering specifications.

## 11. Asset and revision governance

Manufacturing authority follows this hierarchy:

1. Released ELIMFILTERS Brand / Product Identity Manual.
2. Released SKU manufacturing specification.
3. Approved artwork from the official repository.
4. Approved EF-COL-001 physical master panel and EF-INK-002 print sample.
5. Approved physical Golden Sample.
6. Supplier production and QC records.

Any change to body color, lithography color, logo, technology identity, hierarchy, spacing or print geometry requires a controlled revision.
