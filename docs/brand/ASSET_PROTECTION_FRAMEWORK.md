# ASSET_PROTECTION_FRAMEWORK

## Purpose

This document defines the foundational philosophy of ELIMFILTERS.

ELIMFILTERS does not exist to sell filters.

ELIMFILTERS exists to protect industrial assets from contamination-driven failure.

This framework governs:

- Product Development
- Technology Development
- Knowledge Systems
- Customer Intelligence
- Marketing
- Sales
- Engineering
- AI Systems

---

## Core Principle

Assets fail because contamination destroys systems.

Contamination is the root cause.

Filtration is the control mechanism.

Asset Protection is the outcome.
