# KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH_GOVERNANCE

## Purpose

This document defines the governance structure of the ELIMFILTERS Knowledge Graph.

The objective is to create a universal engineering intelligence model capable of connecting contamination threats, industrial assets, technologies, products, industries, and economic outcomes.

This framework governs:

- AI Engine
- Knowledge System
- Customer Intelligence Platform
- Product Intelligence
- Industry Intelligence
- Future AI Agents

---

## Core Principle

Knowledge must be connected.

Isolated information has limited value.

Connected information creates intelligence.

The Knowledge Graph transforms data into decision-making capability.
