# PRODUCT_LIFECYCLE_GOVERNANCE

## Purpose

This document defines the official lifecycle governance model for ELIMFILTERS products.

The objective is to ensure every SKU follows a controlled lifecycle from introduction to retirement while maintaining catalog integrity, cross-reference continuity, and asset protection consistency.

This framework governs:

- Product Development
- Catalog Management
- AI Engine
- Part Search
- Customer Intelligence Platform
- Global Catalog

---

## Core Principle

Products evolve.

Protection objectives remain.

Lifecycle governance exists to preserve protection continuity.

---

# Lifecycle States

## New Product

Status:

Development or pre-launch.

Characteristics:

- Not publicly released
- Internal testing
- Engineering validation

---

## Active Product

Status:

Commercially available.

Characteristics:

- Searchable
- Orderable
- Recommended

---

## Alternative Product

Status:

Compatible replacement option.

Characteristics:

- Similar application
- Different technology and/or strategy possible

Rule:

Must never automatically be treated as equivalent.

---

## Technology Upgrade

Status:

Higher protection capability.

Examples:

- Improved efficiency
- Improved dirt-holding capacity
- Improved water separation

Rule:

Must be clearly identified.

---

## Technology Downgrade

Status:

Lower protection capability.

Rule:

Requires explicit justification.

---

## Superseded Product

Status:

Official replacement exists.

Rule:

Catalog must redirect toward successor SKU.

Old SKU remains searchable.

---

## Obsolete Product

Status:

No longer recommended.

Rule:

Remains searchable for historical purposes.

---

## Discontinued Product

Status:

No longer produced.

Rule:

Must identify replacement path when available.

---

## Archived Product

Status:

Historical record only.

Rule:

Cannot be recommended automatically.

# Search Governance

When searching an inactive SKU, the system should return:

- Original SKU
- Lifecycle Status
- Recommended Replacement
- Technology Relationship
- Protection Impact

---

# AI Governance

AI must communicate lifecycle state explicitly.

Examples:

Correct:

- Superseded by
- Recommended replacement
- Technology upgrade
- Archived product

Incorrect:

- Same product
- Identical replacement

unless validated.

---

# Catalog Governance

Every SKU must contain:

- Lifecycle State
- Technology
- System
- Product Family
- Effective Date
- Replacement Path

---

# Governance Rule

No SKU may disappear from the catalog.

Products may retire.

Knowledge must remain accessible.
