# PROBLEM_REGISTRY

Status: Active

ELIMFILTERS engineering content is organized around contamination and reliability problems, not around commodity filter descriptions.

## Canonical problem domains

- Airborne particulate ingestion
- Fuel particulate contamination
- Fuel water contamination
- Lubricant particulate contamination
- Hydraulic-fluid contamination
- Cooling-system contamination
- Compressed-air moisture
- Cabin airborne contamination
- Seal and bypass integrity
- Service-interval and maintenance execution risk

## Technology relationships

- Air Intake & Airflow Protection: MACROCORE™, MICROKAPPA™, DRYCORE™, INTEKCORE™
- Fuel Cleanliness Protection: SYNTAPORE™, HYDROCORE™
- Lubrication Protection: SYNTRAX™
- Hydraulic Protection: NANOFORCE™
- Cooling System Protection: THERMACORE™
- Marine applications: MARINECLEAN™
- Integrated maintenance kits: DURACTECH™

Rule: a technology may be associated only with its approved protection function.
