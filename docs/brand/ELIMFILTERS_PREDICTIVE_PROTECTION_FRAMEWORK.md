# ELIMFILTERS_PREDICTIVE_PROTECTION_FRAMEWORK

## Purpose

This document defines the predictive protection model of ELIMFILTERS.

The objective is to move industrial maintenance from reactive behavior to predictive asset protection.

---

# Core Principle

Traditional maintenance reacts to failures.

Asset Protection predicts failures.

The future belongs to prediction.

# Reactive Maintenance

Characteristics:

- Failure occurs first
- Repair occurs second

Result:

Highest cost

Highest risk

Highest downtime

---

# Preventive Maintenance

Characteristics:

- Fixed intervals
- Scheduled replacements

Result:

Improved reliability

Potential over-maintenance

---

# Condition-Based Maintenance

Characteristics:

- Decisions based on inspections
- Decisions based on measurements

Result:

Improved asset visibility

---

# Predictive Protection

Characteristics:

- Failure prediction
- Risk forecasting
- Asset intelligence

Result:

Maximum asset value preservation
