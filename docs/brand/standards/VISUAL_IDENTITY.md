\# VISUAL\_IDENTITY



Version: 1.0 Draft



\---



\# PURPOSE



This document defines the official visual identity standards of ELIMFILTERS.



All marketing materials, websites, packaging, presentations, social media content, distributor materials and AI-generated assets must follow these standards.



\---



\# BRAND PERSONALITY



ELIMFILTERS represents:



\* Protection

\* Reliability

\* Engineering

\* Technology

\* Industrial Performance

\* Operational Continuity



The visual identity must communicate confidence, professionalism and industrial expertise.



\---



\# VISUAL POSITIONING



ELIMFILTERS is an Asset Protection Technology Company.



The brand must never be presented as a low-cost filter supplier.



The brand must communicate industrial protection, reliability and technology.



\---



\# OFFICIAL COLOR PALETTE



Primary Colors:



\* ELIMFILTERS Yellow

\* Black



Support Colors:



\* White

\* Industrial Gray



Color codes will be defined in COLOR\_STANDARD.md.



\---



\# OFFICIAL TYPOGRAPHY



Primary Typeface:



\* Montserrat



Secondary Typeface:



\* Inter



Typography standards will be defined in TYPOGRAPHY\_STANDARD.md.



\---



\# PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE



Approved Style:



\* Hyperrealistic

\* Industrial

\* Professional

\* Real equipment

\* Real environments

\* Real applications



Preferred Environments:



\* Mining

\* Construction

\* Agriculture

\* Oil \& Gas

\* Manufacturing

\* Marine

\* Power Generation

\* Transportation



\---



\# PROHIBITED VISUAL STYLES



The following visual styles are prohibited:



\* Cyberpunk

\* Science fiction environments

\* Holographic interfaces

\* Unrealistic machinery

\* Fantasy environments

\* Cartoon styles

\* Low-quality AI imagery



\---



\# AI GENERATED CONTENT



Artificial intelligence may be used to create or enhance visual content.



However, generated content must remain consistent with real industrial applications and realistic operating environments.



AI content must reinforce the ELIMFILTERS brand and its Asset Protection philosophy.



\---



\# VISUAL PRINCIPLE



Every visual asset should answer a single question:



What industrial asset is being protected?
