\# ELIMFILTERS BRAND MANUAL



Version: 1.0 Draft



\---



\# 1. BRAND IDENTITY



\## Who We Are



ELIMFILTERS® is an Asset Protection Technology Company.



We develop technologies, systems and solutions designed to protect industrial assets through contamination control.



Our purpose is to improve reliability, extend equipment life and reduce operational downtime.



\---



\## What We Do



We protect:



\* Engines

\* Hydraulic Systems

\* Fuel Systems

\* Lubrication Systems

\* Cooling Systems

\* Airflow Systems



through advanced contamination control technologies.



\---



\## What We Do Not Do



ELIMFILTERS does not define itself as a filter manufacturer.



ELIMFILTERS does not compete as a commodity filter supplier.



ELIMFILTERS does not compete on price.



\---



\## What We Deliver



We deliver:



\* Reliability

\* Asset Protection

\* Operational Continuity

\* Extended Equipment Life

\* Contamination Control



\---



\# 2. CORPORATE POSITIONING



Primary Positioning Statement:



Protecting Industrial Assets Through Contamination Control.



\---



Corporate Definition:



We do not define ourselves by the products we sell.



We define ourselves by the assets we protect.
