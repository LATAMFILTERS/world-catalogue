# ELIMFILTERS_CUSTOMER_INTELLIGENCE_PLATFORM

## Purpose

This document defines the architecture of the ELIMFILTERS Customer Intelligence Platform.

The objective is to transform every product interaction into actionable intelligence for ELIMFILTERS and its distribution network.

The platform must generate value for:

- ELIMFILTERS
- Distributors
- Fleet Operators
- Industrial Customers
- Asset Owners

---

# Core Principle

Every filter sold generates intelligence.

Every intelligence event improves future decisions.

Every decision improves asset protection.
