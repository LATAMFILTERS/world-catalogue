\# PURPOSE



This document defines the primary industrial assets protected by ELIMFILTERS systems, technologies and products.



Assets are the central object of the ELIMFILTERS Asset Protection methodology.

\# ELIMFILTERS PRINCIPLE



Assets generate value.



Systems support assets.



Technologies protect systems.



Products deliver technologies.



Therefore:



Assets remain the primary focus of ELIMFILTERS.
