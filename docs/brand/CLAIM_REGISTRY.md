# CORE PHILOSOPHY

## ELIMFILTERS Core Philosophy

We do not define ourselves by the products we sell.

We define ourselves by the assets we protect.

Status:
Approved

Priority:
Highest

Classification:
Corporate Philosophy

Purpose:

This statement defines the fundamental identity of ELIMFILTERS.

It establishes the transition from a traditional filtration supplier model to an Industrial Filtration Engineering / Asset Protection Systems model.

All corporate communications, brand positioning, distributor programs, marketing materials, website content, artificial intelligence systems and future business initiatives should reinforce this philosophy.

This statement supersedes product-centric positioning.

Spanish Version

No nos definimos por los productos que vendemos.

Nos definimos por los activos que protegemos.

# ENGINEERING PRINCIPLE

## The filter is the means. Asset protection is the objective.

Status:
Approved

Priority:
Highest

Classification:
Strategic Engineering Principle

Purpose:

This principle establishes that filters and filtration components are the engineered means used to achieve contamination control, system protection, equipment reliability and asset protection.

Canonical value chain:

Filter / Filtration Component → Contamination Control → System Protection → Asset Protection → Reliability and Uptime

Spanish Version

El filtro es el medio. La protección del activo es el objetivo.

Governance:

This principle must guide website architecture, product positioning, technology descriptions, Knowledge Center content, sales language, AI retrieval, structured data and distributor communications.

# DURATECH™ ON-ROAD CLAIM

## 15,000 km On-Road Protection Interval

Status:
Approved — Governed Commercial Claim

Priority:
High

Classification:
Product / Commercial Differentiation

Applies to:

- DURATECH™ integrated maintenance and asset-protection kits
- On-Road applications
- Trucks
- Commercial vehicles

Approved claim concept:

DURATECH™ provides an integrated 15,000 km On-Road protection interval for applicable trucks and commercial vehicles.

Strategic interpretation:

DURATECH™ is not positioned as a bundle of filters. It represents an integrated asset-protection interval in which the required filtration components work together within a defined maintenance cycle.

Public-use governance:

- Use only for approved DURATECH™ On-Road applications.
- Warranty and coverage wording must remain consistent with ELIMFILTERS-approved application, installation and maintenance conditions.
- Do not generalize the 15,000 km claim to off-road, marine, industrial, severe-duty or unapproved applications.
- Do not convert the interval into an unsupported universal performance or durability claim for individual filters.

Spanish Version

DURATECH™ ofrece un intervalo integrado de protección On-Road de 15.000 km para camiones y vehículos comerciales aplicables, sujeto a las condiciones de aplicación, instalación y mantenimiento aprobadas por ELIMFILTERS.
