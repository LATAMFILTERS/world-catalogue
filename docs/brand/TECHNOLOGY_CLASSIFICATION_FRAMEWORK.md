# TECHNOLOGY_CLASSIFICATION_FRAMEWORK

## Purpose

This document defines the official classification model governing all ELIMFILTERS® technologies.

The objective is to establish a consistent relationship between:

- Contamination threats
- Protection objectives
- Technologies
- Systems
- Products
- SKUs

This framework serves as the engineering foundation for the catalog, AI engine, customer intelligence platform, knowledge system, and future product development.

## Core Principle

Technologies are not products.

Technologies are engineered contamination-control platforms.

Products are physical implementations of technologies.

SKUs are commercial implementations of products.
