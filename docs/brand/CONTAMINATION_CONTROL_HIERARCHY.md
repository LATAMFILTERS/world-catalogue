# CONTAMINATION_CONTROL_HIERARCHY

## Purpose

This document defines the official contamination-control hierarchy of ELIMFILTERS.

The objective is to establish a universal framework connecting contamination threats, failure mechanisms, protection systems, technologies, and asset protection outcomes.

This hierarchy serves as the engineering foundation for:

- Product Development
- Technology Development
- AI Systems
- Knowledge Systems
- Customer Intelligence
- Asset Protection Strategy

---

## Core Principle

Contamination is the root cause.

Failure is the consequence.

Protection is the intervention.

Asset reliability is the outcome.
