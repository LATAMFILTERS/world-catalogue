# AI_ENGINE_ARCHITECTURE

## Purpose

This document defines the operational architecture of the ELIMFILTERS AI Engine.

The objective is to transform structured catalog data, knowledge graph intelligence, customer intelligence, and asset intelligence into explainable recommendations.

---

# Core Principle

The AI Engine does not invent knowledge.

The AI Engine discovers, connects, prioritizes, and explains knowledge.
