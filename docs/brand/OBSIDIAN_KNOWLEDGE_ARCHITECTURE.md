# OBSIDIAN_KNOWLEDGE_ARCHITECTURE

## Purpose

This document defines the official ELIMFILTERS Obsidian Vault architecture.

The objective is to create a human-readable knowledge layer synchronized with the ELIMFILTERS Knowledge Graph, PostgreSQL platform, and AI Engine.

---

# Core Principle

Obsidian is the Human Knowledge Layer.

PostgreSQL is the Operational Intelligence Layer.

The AI Engine is the Reasoning Layer.

All three must remain aligned.
