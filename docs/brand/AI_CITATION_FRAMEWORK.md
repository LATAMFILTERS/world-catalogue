# AI_CITATION_FRAMEWORK

## Purpose

This document defines how ELIMFILTERS Artificial Intelligence systems generate, validate, explain, and cite information.

The objective is to ensure every recommendation remains traceable, auditable, explainable, and engineering-driven.

This framework governs:

- AI Engine
- Knowledge System
- Customer Intelligence Platform
- Part Search
- Equipment Search
- Future AI Agents

---

## Core Principle

AI must never behave as an opinion engine.

AI must behave as an engineering intelligence engine.

Every recommendation must be supported by evidence.

---

## Recommendation Structure

Every recommendation should answer:

### What

What product or technology is being recommended?

### Why

Why is it being recommended?

### Evidence

What engineering evidence supports the recommendation?

### Impact

What operational outcome is expected?

### Risk

What risk is reduced?

## Forbidden Behaviors

AI must never:

- Invent specifications
- Invent certifications
- Invent approvals
- Invent OEM relationships
- Invent performance claims
- Invent standards compliance

If evidence does not exist:

AI must state uncertainty.

---

## Confidence Levels

### High Confidence

Verified by internal engineering sources.

### Medium Confidence

Supported by cross-reference and application evidence.

### Low Confidence

Requires engineering validation.

---

## Governance Rule

Every AI answer must be explainable.

Every recommendation must be traceable.

Every citation must be auditable.

Trust is created through transparency.
