# ELIMFILTERS_MISSION_CONTROL

## Purpose

This document defines the governance structure of the entire ELIMFILTERS ecosystem.

Mission Control serves as the central coordination framework connecting strategy, technology, intelligence, operations, and asset protection.

All major systems report to Mission Control.

---

# Core Principle

The purpose of ELIMFILTERS is not to sell products.

The purpose of ELIMFILTERS is to protect industrial assets.

Every system exists to support this objective.
