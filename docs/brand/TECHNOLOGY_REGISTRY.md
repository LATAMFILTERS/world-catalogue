# TECHNOLOGY_REGISTRY

Status: Active

# BRAND ENGINEERING PRINCIPLE

ELIMFILTERS technologies represent protection functions within the Asset Protection Systems architecture.

The filter is the means. Asset protection is the objective.

Filters and filtration components are not positioned as the final value proposition. They are engineered media used to control contamination, protect critical systems, support equipment reliability and protect the asset.
