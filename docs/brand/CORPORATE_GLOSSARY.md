\# CORPORATE\_GLOSSARY



Version: 1.0 Draft



\---



\# Asset Protection



The process of protecting industrial assets against contamination, wear, degradation and premature failure.



\---



\# Contamination Control



The identification, reduction and management of contaminants that negatively impact industrial systems and equipment reliability.



\---



\# Asset Protection Technology



Technologies designed to improve equipment reliability, extend asset life and reduce contamination-related failures.



\---



\# Reliability



The ability of an asset or system to perform its intended function consistently over time.



\---



\# Operational Continuity



The ability to maintain production and operations without unplanned interruptions.



\---



\# Downtime



Any period during which an asset, machine or operation is unavailable for productive work.



\---



\# Uptime



The percentage of time an asset remains operational and available.



\---



\# Asset Life



The useful operating life of equipment, components and systems.



\---



\# Fuel Cleanliness



The condition of fuel being free of contaminants that may damage pumps, injectors and fuel system components.



\---



\# Hydraulic Cleanliness



The condition of hydraulic fluid being maintained within acceptable contamination limits.



\---



\# Lubrication Protection



The protection of engines and rotating components through contamination-controlled lubrication systems.



\---



\# Cooling System Protection



The protection of cooling systems against contamination, corrosion and performance degradation.



\---



\# Airflow Protection



The protection of engines and systems through controlled intake air quality.



\---



\# Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)



The total lifecycle cost associated with acquiring, operating, maintaining and replacing an asset.



\---
