# COMPETITIVE_POSITIONING

ELIMFILTERS is not a filter company.

ELIMFILTERS is an Asset Protection Technology company.

The company exists to reduce contamination-driven failures across industrial assets.

The market does not buy filters.

The market buys:

- Reliability
- Uptime
- Protection
- Risk Reduction
- Asset Life Extension

Therefore ELIMFILTERS competes in:

Asset Protection

not

Filter Manufacturing.

## Positioning Layers

Level 1

Commodity Suppliers

Compete on price.

Level 2

Filtration Manufacturers

Compete on filter performance.

Level 3

Asset Protection Companies

Compete on operational outcomes.

ELIMFILTERS operates at Level 3.

## Strategic Mission

Protect critical industrial assets through contamination control.

## Strategic Vision

Become the world's most intelligent contamination-control ecosystem.

## Competitive Rule

Never position ELIMFILTERS as a filter supplier.

Always position ELIMFILTERS as an Asset Protection Technology company.
