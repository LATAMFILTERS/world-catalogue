# OEM_RELATIONSHIP_FRAMEWORK

## Purpose

This document defines the relationship between ELIMFILTERS and Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs).

The objective is to establish a clear governance model for OEM references, applications, equipment intelligence, and asset protection strategies.

---

# Core Principle

OEMs design assets.

ELIMFILTERS protects assets.

The relationship is complementary.

---

# OEM Definition

OEM stands for Original Equipment Manufacturer.

Examples:

- Caterpillar
- Cummins
- Volvo
- Komatsu
- Detroit Diesel
- John Deere
- Scania
- Mercedes-Benz

OEMs define:

- Equipment
- Engines
- Systems
- Original specifications

---

# ELIMFILTERS Position

ELIMFILTERS does not replace OEM engineering.

ELIMFILTERS supports OEM asset protection objectives through contamination control technologies.
