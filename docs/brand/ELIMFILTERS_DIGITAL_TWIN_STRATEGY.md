# ELIMFILTERS_DIGITAL_TWIN_STRATEGY

## Purpose

This document defines the Digital Twin strategy of ELIMFILTERS.

The objective is to create a digital representation of every protected asset within the ELIMFILTERS ecosystem.

The Digital Twin becomes the intelligence layer connecting products, technologies, maintenance history, contamination events, and protection recommendations.

---

# Core Principle

Every physical asset should have a digital counterpart.

The physical asset generates activity.

The digital asset generates intelligence.

---

# Digital Twin Definition

A Digital Twin is a continuously evolving representation of a real industrial asset.

Examples:

- Truck
- Excavator
- Generator
- Compressor
- Marine Engine
- Hydraulic System

The twin stores knowledge about the asset throughout its lifecycle.
