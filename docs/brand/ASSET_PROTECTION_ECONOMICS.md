# ASSET_PROTECTION_ECONOMICS

## Purpose

This document defines the economic model behind contamination control and asset protection.

The objective is to demonstrate that the value of ELIMFILTERS is measured through avoided operational losses rather than filter acquisition cost.

---

## Core Principle

Customers do not lose money because filters are expensive.

Customers lose money because failures are expensive.

The economic objective is therefore:

Reduce failure cost.

Not reduce filter cost.
