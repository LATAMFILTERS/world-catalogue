# PRIVACY_AND_DATA_PROTECTION_POLICY

## Purpose

This document defines the privacy and data protection principles governing ELIMFILTERS digital platforms.

The objective is to protect customer data, distributor data, asset intelligence, AI interactions, and future customer intelligence systems.

---

# Core Principle

Data must be collected with purpose.

Data must be protected with discipline.

Data must be used responsibly.

---

# Data Categories

ELIMFILTERS may collect:

- Contact information
- Company information
- Distributor information
- Search activity
- Product registrations
- Equipment information
- Asset information
- Technical inquiries
- AI interactions

---

# Data Usage

Data may be used to:

- Support customers
- Improve product recommendations
- Improve search accuracy
- Build customer intelligence
- Improve asset protection strategies
- Support distributors
- Improve platform performance

---

# Data Protection

Requirements:

- Secure storage
- Secure transmission
- Access control
- Auditability
- Backup protection
- Responsible retention

---

# Customer Rights

Where applicable, users may request:

- Access to data
- Correction of data
- Deletion of data
- Export of data
- Restriction of processing

---

# Consent

Data collection should be transparent.

Users should understand why information is requested.

---

# AI Data Use

AI systems must not expose private customer data.

AI interactions must be handled according to privacy and security rules.

---

# Distributor Data

Distributor data must be protected as confidential business information.

---

# Asset Intelligence

Asset intelligence must be used to improve protection outcomes and must be governed according to customer agreements.

---

# Governance Rule

ELIMFILTERS must use data to improve protection, not exploit users.
