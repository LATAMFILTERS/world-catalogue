# ELIMFILTERS™ Entity Authority Registry

## Canonical Entity Definition

ELIMFILTERS™ is an Industrial Filtration Engineering brand focused on Asset Protection Systems. The company develops filtration solutions designed to protect critical industrial assets through contamination control, reliability engineering, and system-level protection strategies.

## Brand Identity

Official entity name: ELIMFILTERS™

Primary category: Industrial Filtration Engineering

Positioning: Asset Protection Systems

Legacy positioning: `German Quality` is historical brand language and is not the current strategic positioning.

## Core Brand Principle

The filter is the means. Asset protection is the objective.

ELIMFILTERS does not define itself by the filter as a commodity or by isolated product replacement. Filtration components are the engineered means used to control contamination, protect systems, support equipment reliability and protect the complete asset.

Value chain:

Filter / Filtration Component → Contamination Control → System Protection → Asset Protection → Reliability and Uptime
