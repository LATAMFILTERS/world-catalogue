# TECHNOLOGY_GOVERNANCE_CHARTER

## Purpose

This document serves as the constitutional framework governing all ELIMFILTERS technologies.

The objective is to ensure long-term consistency across engineering, product development, catalog management, AI systems, customer intelligence, and future innovation initiatives.

This charter governs:

- Technology Development
- Product Development
- AI Systems
- Knowledge Systems
- Customer Intelligence Platform
- Global Catalog

---

## Core Principle

Technologies are strategic assets.

Products may change.

SKUs may change.

Applications may change.

Technologies remain the foundational protection platforms of ELIMFILTERS.

---

# Technology Mission

Every technology must exist to solve a contamination-control problem.

Technology is not branding.

Technology is not marketing.

Technology is engineered protection.

# Technology Governance Rules

## Rule 1

Every technology must have:

- Defined contamination threat
- Defined protection objective
- Defined engineering purpose

---

## Rule 2

Every technology must support at least one protection system.

---

## Rule 3

Every technology must support measurable asset-protection outcomes.

---

## Rule 4

Technologies cannot be created solely for marketing purposes.

---

## Rule 5

Technology definitions may only be modified through engineering governance.
