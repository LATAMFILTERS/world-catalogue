# AI_ENGINE_GOVERNANCE

## Purpose

This document defines the governance model of the ELIMFILTERS Artificial Intelligence Engine.

The objective is to ensure that every AI-generated recommendation remains explainable, auditable, traceable, and aligned with asset protection principles.

This framework governs:

- AI Engine
- Part Search
- Equipment Search
- Knowledge System
- Customer Intelligence Platform
- Future AI Agents

---

## Core Principle

AI exists to support engineering decisions.

AI does not replace engineering.

AI accelerates engineering.

AI must never operate as an opinion system.

AI must operate as an evidence-based intelligence system.

# Confidence Levels

## Level 1

High Confidence

Supported by:

- Internal registries
- Engineering rules
- Verified mappings

---

## Level 2

Moderate Confidence

Supported by:

- Cross references
- Application data
- Historical intelligence

---

## Level 3

Low Confidence

Requires:

- Engineering validation
- Customer confirmation
- Additional evidence

---

# Prohibited Behaviors

The AI must never:

- Invent specifications
- Invent certifications
- Invent OEM approvals
- Invent testing results
- Invent performance claims
- Invent technical standards

If evidence is unavailable:

The AI must explicitly state uncertainty.

# Asset Protection Rule

The objective of the AI is not to recommend filters.

The objective of the AI is to protect assets.

Products are tools.

Protection is the mission.

# Governance Rule

Every AI output must be:

- Explainable
- Auditable
- Traceable
- Defensible
- Engineering-Based

Trust is earned through evidence.
