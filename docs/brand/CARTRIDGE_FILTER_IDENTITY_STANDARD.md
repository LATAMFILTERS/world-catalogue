# ELIMFILTERS® Cartridge Filter Identity Standard

Status: Controlled draft
Scope: ELIMFILTERS cartridge-style filter elements across applicable product families.

## 1. Purpose

Define the visual, dimensional and manufacturing identity rules for ELIMFILTERS cartridge filters. This standard controls how product identification is applied to the physical element and how the family scales across different cartridge geometries.

This document does not replace engineering drawings, media specifications, dimensional tolerances or application-specific technical data.

## 2. Core Identity Hierarchy

The default product hierarchy is:

1. ELIMFILTERS®
2. SKU / Part Number
3. Product Type
4. Approved Technology Identity

The physical cartridge must remain visually clean. Extended technical, legal and commercial information belongs primarily on the individual package and master carton unless engineering or regulatory requirements require direct marking on the element.

## 3. Technology Mapping

Technology assignment must follow the current governed ELIMFILTERS technology architecture. Typical mappings include:

- Lube / Oil cartridge → SYNTRAX™
- Hydraulic cartridge → NANOFORCE™
- Fuel particulate cartridge → SYNTAPORE™
- Fuel / Water Separation cartridge → HYDROCORE™ when applicable
- Coolant cartridge → THERMACORE™ when applicable

No technology name may be assigned solely for visual purposes. Engineering/product classification controls the final assignment.

## 4. Geometry Classes

Cartridge artwork must be derived from the actual element geometry. Do not scale artwork blindly from one SKU to another.

Controlled dimensional inputs per SKU:

- Overall height
- Maximum outside diameter
- Minimum outside diameter where geometry varies
- Inside diameter / center opening, top
- Inside diameter / center opening, bottom
- End-cap height and profile
- Media exposed height
- Available identification height
- Available identification width / circumference where applicable
- Structural cage or mesh interference zones
- Non-print / no-label zones

Cartridges may be grouped into size/geometry classes only after the actual dimensional range has been validated.

## 5. Identification Methods

The approved marking method must be selected according to cartridge construction and manufacturing capability. Permitted methods may include:

- Direct ink printing on an approved end-cap or band
- Laser marking on an approved component
- Permanent label on a validated non-media surface
- Molded or embossed identification where applicable

Direct printing on exposed filtration media is prohibited unless specifically validated by Product & Technology and Production for media compatibility, adhesion, chemical resistance and filtration integrity.

## 6. Visual Placement Rules

Where the cartridge provides a stable cylindrical or planar identification surface, the following hierarchy applies:

### Zone A — Master Brand
Official ELIMFILTERS® logo or approved wordmark asset.

### Zone B — Product Identification
SKU / Part Number. This is the primary operational identifier and must remain clearly readable after installation handling.

### Zone C — Product Type
Examples: Lube Filter, Hydraulic Filter, Fuel Filter, Coolant Filter, as technically applicable.

### Zone D — Technology
Official technology identity and only the approved descriptor where space permits.

If available marking space becomes restricted, secondary descriptors are removed before reducing the master brand or SKU below the approved minimum size.

## 7. Color Rules

Color of printed identification must be selected for maximum durable contrast against the actual component surface.

Do not assume the Spin-On paint/ink combination applies to cartridges. Cartridge color is controlled by component material, end-cap finish, media construction and approved manufacturing process.

Any new ink/color combination requires a controlled production sample before release.

## 8. End Caps and Structural Components

For each SKU, the factory release must identify:

- End-cap material
- End-cap color/finish
- Upper/lower end-cap geometry
- Center tube or core material
- Inner/outer support mesh if applicable
- Adhesive/potting visual acceptance where exposed

Brand color must never compromise component material, bonding performance, dimensional fit or chemical compatibility.

## 9. Media Presentation

The media itself is a functional component and must not be altered for aesthetic reasons unless the change is part of an approved technical specification.

Controlled visual characteristics may include:

- Media base color
- Pleat uniformity
- Pleat spacing
- Media edge cleanliness
- Visible support layer / mesh
- Bonding consistency

No competitor media imagery or visual pattern may be used as an ELIMFILTERS manufacturing reference.

## 10. Packaging Identity

Cartridge filters should use individual packaging when required by product protection, product family or commercial presentation.

The individual package is the primary location for extended identification, including as applicable:

- ELIMFILTERS®
- SKU
- Product Type
- Technology
- Barcode
- QR code
- Country of origin
- Lot / traceability
- Kleo Technology LLC identification
- Website / controlled digital reference
- Handling information

Master carton quantities are SKU-specific and must be defined by weight, volume and product protection requirements.

## 11. Golden Sample

The first completely approved production sample for each representative cartridge geometry becomes the physical Golden Sample for visual manufacturing control after approval.

The Golden Sample does not replace engineering drawings or dimensional tolerances.

## 12. Release Rule

No cartridge artwork, marking method, color, packaging or component finish is released for mass production until:

1. Actual dimensions are recorded.
2. Manufacturing method is confirmed.
3. Artwork is generated from official repository assets.
4. Physical sample is produced.
5. Readability, adhesion and placement are approved.
6. Golden Sample / release record is signed off.

## 13. Asset Governance

Official ELIMFILTERS and technology identities must come from the controlled assets in the `world-catalogue/frontend/public/assets/` repository path. Recreated or approximate logos are not permitted.

Revision changes must be recorded in Git and reflected in the corresponding factory release sheet.
