# DECISION_GOVERNANCE_FRAMEWORK

## Purpose

Defines how ELIMFILTERS generates, evaluates, approves, and records decisions.

---

# Core Principle

Every important decision must be traceable.

---

# Decision Hierarchy

Level 1

Informational

Examples:

- Product lookup
- Cross reference lookup

---

Level 2

Recommendation

Examples:

- Technology recommendation
- Protection recommendation

---

Level 3

Operational

Examples:

- Inventory recommendation
- Distributor recommendation

---

Level 4

Strategic

Examples:

- New technology
- New territory
- New OEM mapping

---

# Decision Record

Every decision should capture:

- Decision Type
- Inputs
- Evidence
- Confidence
- Recommendation
- Outcome

---

# Governance Rule

No strategic decision should exist without evidence.
