# LEAD GENERATION STRATEGY
## ELIMFILTERS Engineering Intelligence Platform — Phase 4

---

## 1. Principle

Every lead capture opportunity must feel like the logical next engineering step.

A CTA that appears before trust is earned is rejected as interruption.
A CTA that appears after conviction is earned is welcomed as assistance.

**The engineering conversation earns the right to ask.**

No lead form appears without at least Trust Signal T-4 (Technology Architecture)
having been presented to the visitor. Ideally T-6 (Traceable Recommendation)
is complete before any lead capture is attempted.

---

## 2. Lead Categories

### L-1: Engineering Consultation Request
**Value:** Highest. Qualified technical prospect.
**Trigger:** After a complete protection system recommendation (Journey 2, Step 5–6).
**Information earned before CTA:** Full engineering conversation. Contamination identified,
failure mode understood, technology recommended, confidence shown.
**Ask:** "Would you like an ELIMFILTERS engineer to review this recommendation for your specific application?"
**Information requested:** Name, company, equipment type, operating environment, email, phone.
**Routing:** Engineering team directly. High-priority follow-up.

### L-2: Protection System Quote
**Value:** High. Commercial intent confirmed.
**Trigger:** After Journey 2 or Journey 3 completion, visitor has a recommended protection system.
**Information earned before CTA:** Complete journey. Problem → technology → products identified.
**Ask:** "Request a quote for your recommended protection system."
**Information requested:** Name, company, fleet size, equipment list, email, phone.
**Routing:** Sales team + engineering review.

### L-3: Distributor Contact Referral
**Value:** High. Immediate purchase intent.
**Trigger:** Journey 1 (part identified) or Journey 3 (urgent problem).
**Information earned before CTA:** Part confirmed or solution identified.
**Ask:** "Find your nearest ELIMFILTERS distributor."
**Information requested:** Country / region (for distributor matching). Optional: name + email.
**Routing:** Distributor network.

### L-4: Technical Document Download
**Value:** Medium. Research intent, longer cycle.
**Trigger:** Journey 4 (learning). After reading an engineering article or technology page.
**Information earned before CTA:** Engineering article read, technology understood.
**Ask:** "Download the technical specification for [Technology Name]."
**Information requested:** Email (required), name (optional), company (optional).
**Routing:** Marketing automation nurture sequence.

### L-5: Engineering Newsletter
**Value:** Medium-low. Awareness and long-term relationship.
**Trigger:** Journey 4 after multiple article reads. Technology Research intent detected.
**Information earned before CTA:** Demonstrated interest in engineering content.
**Ask:** "Receive engineering intelligence updates: failure mode analyses, standards updates, field observations."
**Information requested:** Email only.
**Routing:** Newsletter platform. Engineering content, not marketing content.

### L-6: Failure Analysis Request
**Value:** High. Reactive intent, pain present.
**Trigger:** Journey 3 (Problem Diagnosis). After failure mode identified but before solution clear.
**Information earned before CTA:** Problem recognized, customer understands the engineering.
**Ask:** "Request a failure analysis for your specific equipment and operating conditions."
**Information requested:** Name, company, equipment description, observed symptoms, email, phone.
**Routing:** Engineering team. High-urgency follow-up.

### L-7: Training / Workshop Interest
**Value:** Medium. Institutional relationship, multiple contacts.
**Trigger:** Journey 4. Supplier Evaluation intent detected.
**Information earned before CTA:** Multiple knowledge articles read, technology comparison done.
**Ask:** "Interested in an engineering workshop for your maintenance team?"
**Information requested:** Name, company, team size, topic of interest, email.
**Routing:** Training / partnerships team.

## 3. CTA Placement Matrix

This table defines where each CTA type is placed and what must have been presented first.

| Lead Type | Placed In | Minimum Trust Level | Minimum Journey State |
|---|---|---|---|
| L-1 Engineering Consultation | Journey 2, Step 5–6 | T-6 (Traceable Recommendation) | STATE 3 |
| L-2 Protection System Quote | Journey 2–3 final step | T-6 | STATE 3 |
| L-3 Distributor Contact | Journey 1–3 product step | T-4 | STATE 2–3 |
| L-4 Technical Download | Technology pages, Journey 4 | T-4 | STATE 2 |
| L-5 Engineering Newsletter | Knowledge System hub, article footer | T-2 | STATE 1 |
| L-6 Failure Analysis | Journey 3, Step 2–3 | T-2 | STATE 2 |
| L-7 Training Interest | Knowledge System hub, Journey 4 exit | T-7 | STATE 3–4 |

**Rules:**
- L-1 and L-2 never appear on the homepage.
- L-5 is the only CTA that can appear at low trust levels (STATE 1).
- L-3 appears only after a specific product or technology has been identified.
- All CTAs use engineering language, not sales language (see Section 5).

## 4. Journey-to-Lead Mapping

### Journey 1 (Part Number)
```
Step 1: Search → no CTA
Step 2: Cross-reference → no CTA
Step 3: Engineering explanation → [L-4 Download tech spec] (subtle, optional)
Step 4: Technology → [L-4 Download] (secondary option)
Step 5: Product confirmed → [L-3 Distributor Contact] (primary CTA)
```

### Journey 2 (Asset Protection)
```
Step 1: Asset selection → no CTA
Step 2: Risk assessment → [L-5 Newsletter] (subtle, sidebar)
Step 3: Failure modes → [L-6 Failure Analysis] (if customer has current problem)
Step 4: Contamination → no CTA (engaged in analysis)
Step 5: Technology recommendation → [L-4 Download tech spec]
Step 6: Protection system → [L-1 Engineering Consultation] (primary) + [L-2 Quote] (secondary)
Step 7: Products → [L-3 Distributor Contact] (if ready to purchase)
```

### Journey 3 (Problem Diagnosis)
```
Step 1: Problem description → no CTA
Step 2: Failure mode identified → [L-6 Failure Analysis Request] (primary)
Step 3: Root cause → no CTA (critical engagement moment)
Step 4: Engineering principle → no CTA
Step 5: Technology recommendation → [L-1 Engineering Consultation]
Step 6: Protection media → no CTA
Step 7: Products → [L-3 Distributor Contact] (primary) + [L-2 Quote] (secondary)
```

### Journey 4 (Learning)
```
Domain selection → no CTA
Article read (first) → [L-5 Newsletter] (subtle footer)
Article read (second, same session) → [L-5 Newsletter] (banner offer)
Technology page → [L-4 Download tech spec]
Multiple technology pages → [L-7 Training Interest]
Standards page → [L-4 Technical documentation]
Journey 4 exit → [L-1 Engineering Consultation] (intent-aware, if research pattern detected)
```

## 5. CTA Language Rules

All CTAs must use engineering language, not sales language.

**Banned CTA language:**
- "Buy now"
- "Get a discount"
- "Limited time offer"
- "Contact our sales team"
- "Request a callback"
- "Sign up"

**Required CTA language:**
- "Request an engineering assessment"
- "Get your protection system recommendation"
- "Find your ELIMFILTERS distributor"
- "Download the technical specification"
- "Request failure analysis for your equipment"
- "Schedule an engineering consultation"
- "Receive engineering intelligence updates"

The distinction is not cosmetic. Sales CTAs feel like interruptions.
Engineering CTAs feel like the next logical step in a technical conversation.

## 6. Post-Lead Follow-Up Engineering Package

When a lead is captured (L-1 through L-7), the platform generates and delivers
an Engineering Package to the customer automatically.

The Engineering Package contains:

```
ENGINEERING SUMMARY FOR [COMPANY] — [DATE]

1. ASSETS IDENTIFIED
   Equipment type, industry, operating environment

2. RISKS IDENTIFIED
   Contamination classes, failure modes, estimated operational impact

3. RECOMMENDED TECHNOLOGY ARCHITECTURE
   [Technology name + canonicalDefinition]
   Implementing engineering principle: [principle + definition]
   Validated by standards: [list of applicable standards]

4. RECOMMENDATION TRACE
   Step-by-step path: [EngineeringReasoningChain as text]
   Confidence: [HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW] — [reason]

5. FIELD ENGINEERING OBSERVATIONS
   [Engineering Memory notes if available]

6. CITATIONS
   [formatted citations for all engineering claims]

7. RECOMMENDED PRODUCTS
   [SKU list with engineering lineage]

8. NEXT STEPS
   Your ELIMFILTERS engineering contact: [routing]
```

This package is generated from the Knowledge Graph via `buildAIContext()`.
It is not written manually. It is the customer's engineering record of the journey.

The package serves two purposes:
1. It confirms to the customer that the recommendation is documented and defensible.
2. It gives the ELIMFILTERS engineer who follows up a complete picture of the customer's situation.

## 7. Lead Qualification Scoring

Leads are scored automatically based on journey data.

| Signal | Score Weight |
|---|---|
| Journey 2 or 3 completed | +30 |
| Engineering consultation CTA clicked | +25 |
| Protection system recommendation accepted | +20 |
| Technical document downloaded | +15 |
| Failure analysis requested | +20 |
| Multiple sessions (return visitor) | +15 |
| Multiple technology pages visited | +10 |
| Journey 4 completed (learning) | +10 |
| Newsletter subscribed | +5 |
| Journey 1 only (part number) | +5 |

Score bands:
- 0–20: Awareness (nurture with engineering content)
- 21–40: Engaged (newsletter, technical content follow-up)
- 41–60: Research (proactive outreach with relevant engineering material)
- 61–80: Qualified (engineering consultation follow-up, 24-hour response)
- 81–100: Hot (engineering consultation follow-up, 4-hour response)
