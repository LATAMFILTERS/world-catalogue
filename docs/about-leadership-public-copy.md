# Public copy boundary

The public Leadership page must present ELIMFILTERS as an industrial filtration organization with an AI-native operating model, not as a novelty AI company.

Core public message:

Human leadership. AI-native execution.

ELIMFILTERS remains human-governed. Executive AI Agents operate specialized domains under defined authority, controls, escalation, traceability, and human intervention. Artificial representations are disclosed as AI agents and are never presented as natural persons or undisclosed human employees.

Capability is demonstrated through technical precision, operational consistency, responsiveness, traceability, customer continuity, and disciplined execution rather than apparent organizational headcount.
