\# AI Use Policy



Effective Date: June 2026



\## Purpose



ELIMFILTERS AI Labs utilizes artificial intelligence technologies to assist users with industrial filtration, contamination control, asset protection and technical information retrieval.



\## AI-Assisted Services



AI systems may be used to:



\* Search product information

\* Identify cross references

\* Retrieve technical data

\* Analyze maintenance scenarios

\* Generate recommendations

\* Support industrial decision-making



\## User Responsibility



Users remain responsible for independently verifying all information before making operational, engineering, safety, regulatory or purchasing decisions.



\## Accuracy



AI-generated responses may contain errors, omissions or outdated information.



ELIMFILTERS does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness or suitability of AI-generated outputs.



\## Prohibited Uses



Users may not use AI services to:



\* Violate laws

\* Infringe intellectual property rights

\* Generate harmful content

\* Misrepresent AI-generated content as official engineering certification



\## No Professional Advice



AI-generated content does not constitute:



\* Engineering certification

\* Legal advice

\* Regulatory approval

\* Professional consulting services



\## Continuous Improvement



ELIMFILTERS may improve, modify or update AI systems at any time.



\## Availability



AI services are provided on an "as available" basis.



Availability, functionality and performance may change without notice.
