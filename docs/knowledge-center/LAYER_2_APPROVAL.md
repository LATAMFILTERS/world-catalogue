# Knowledge Center — Layer 2 Approval

Status: **APPROVED AND FROZEN**

Layer 2 defines the governed authoring contracts and technical taxonomy used to create ELIMFILTERS diagnostic knowledge.

## Approved protection systems

1. Air Intake & Airflow Protection
2. Fuel Cleanliness Protection
3. Lube/Oil Protection
4. Hydraulic Protection
5. Cooling System Protection

## Approved classification model

Every diagnostic case is classified through this hierarchy:

`Protection System → Technical Family → Product Family → Product/Part Relationships`

Diagnostic knowledge belongs to the technical family. It is not duplicated for each part number.

## Air scope

Air includes:

- Primary, secondary/safety, radial-seal, and axial-seal engine air filters.
- Complete air-filter housings, housing components, precleaners, and dust-ejection components under INTEKCORE™.
- Cabin air filters under MICROKAPPA™.
- Air-dryer and coalescing cartridges and related service components under DRYCORE™.

## Fuel scope

Fuel maintains distinct technical families for:

- Primary and secondary engine fuel filters.
- Fuel/water separators and separator elements under HYDROCORE™.
- FH and FG turbine elements, complete turbine assemblies, bowls, heaters, and service components under HYDROCORE/SERIES™.

## Lube/Oil scope

Lube/Oil is restricted to lubrication filtration, including spin-on, cartridge, full-flow, bypass, combination, and lubrication filtration systems under SYNTRAX™.

## Hydraulic scope

Hydraulic includes pressure, return, suction, tank-breather, in-tank, spin-on, and cartridge filtration under NANOFORCE™.

## Cooling scope

Cooling includes blank, precharged, controlled-release, and conditioner coolant filters under THERMACORE™.

## Mandatory diagnostic fields

Every case must contain:

- Protection system
- Technical family
- Product family
- Technology
- Symptom
- Probable causes
- Diagnostic procedure
- Required evidence
- Stop rules
- Corrective actions
- Verification and closure criteria
- Approval metadata

## Change control

Layer 2 is frozen. New families or structural changes require technical review, documented rationale, registry revision, and approval. Layer 3 may add diagnostic content but may not silently alter this taxonomy.
