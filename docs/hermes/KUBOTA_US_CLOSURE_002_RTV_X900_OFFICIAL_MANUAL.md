# Kubota RTV-X900 closeout — official operator manual verification
Date 2026-10-17
Scope: RTV-X900 vs RTV-X1120D. Evidence from Kubota original operator manual:
https://media.kubota.io/uploads/RTV-X900-X1120D01-Utility-Vehicle-Manual.pdf

## Verified official maintenance positions
The original Kubota manual has distinct instructions for engine oil filter, hydraulic transmission / HST filters, fuel filter and air intake. These are service positions, not original part numbers. Do not treat position names as direct OEM codes.
The manual explicitly distinguishes engine oil capacity:
- RTV-X900: 3.1 L / 3.3 US qt when filter changed, 2.7 L / 2.9 US qt without.
- RTV-X1120D: 4.1 L / 4.3 US qt when filter changed, 3.8 L / 4.0 US qt without.
This corrects a misleading 4.3 qt single-model statement on a reseller's RTV-X900W maintenance page. Never inherit X1120D capacity into X900.
