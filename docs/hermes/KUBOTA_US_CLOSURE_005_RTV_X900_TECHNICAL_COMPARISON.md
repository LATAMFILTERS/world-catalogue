## Decision gate
1. Same nominal thread and close OD are positive evidence but NOT homologation.
2. Axial height differs 4 mm. Verify gasket seat, mounting clearance, mechanical envelope.
3. Need official Kubota HHK70-14073 pressure circuit requirements, HIFI SH60416 pressure/efficiency/bypass specification and Donaldson written suitability for HST application.
4. Compare filter anti-drainback behavior and bypass delta-P at viscosity and HST duty cycle.
5. If no matching Donaldson verified on bounded direct OEM check, investigate Fleetguard LF17543 / HF29120 with original maker technical spec; do not assume LF = HST hydraulics without evidence.
6. HIFI manufacturer's own HHK70-14073 cross remains valid HIFI evidence, not proof of Donaldson compatibility.
7. Remain PARTIAL, not complete. Do not mint EL80711 or EH60711 or modify canonical data.
