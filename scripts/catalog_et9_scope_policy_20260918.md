# ET9 / TURBOCORE™ catalog scope policy

- Include complete filtration/separation units, even when the manufacturer uses terms such as housing or assembly.
- Include replacement filter elements.
- Exclude standalone metal/hardware pieces such as a bare filter head/base/bowl/support when they do not constitute a complete filtration unit.
- Do not classify a product as excluded from the word “housing” or “assembly” alone; verify functional completeness from source evidence.
- ET92002 is retained as a complete filtration unit. ET93202 remains the replacement element.
