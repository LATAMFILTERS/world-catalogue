# MARINECLEAN™ — Marine Asset Protection Line

## Product Line Overview

MARINECLEAN™ is a salt-resistant filtration line for commercial marine, offshore, and coastal operations. Standard industrial filtration degrades rapidly in marine environments — salt aerosol penetrates seals, corrodes housings, and compromises element integrity within months.

MARINECLEAN™ is engineered specifically for wet-dry cycling in harbor, offshore, and deep-sea operating environments.

## Engineering Construction

| Feature | Specification |
|---|---|
| Coating | Marine-grade epoxy barrier |
| Geometry | Brine rejection at element interface |
| Internals | Corrosion-resistant alloys |
| Certification | IMO (International Maritime Organization) |
| Systems | Fuel · Hydraulic · Lube oil |

## Operational Contexts

### Commercial Vessels
Main engine fuel filtration, hydraulic steering and deck machinery, lube oil circuits aboard cargo ships, ferries, and workboats.

### Offshore Platforms
Diesel generator fuel systems, hydraulic BOP and wellhead control circuits, crane hydraulics in permanent and semi-submersible platforms.

### Coastal Infrastructure
Port machinery, coastal construction equipment, and shore-based industrial operations subject to continuous salt aerosol exposure.

## Contamination Targets

- Saltwater aerosol ingress through housing seals
- Brine penetration at element interface
- Accelerated corrosion from wet-dry cycling
- Seawater intrusion into fuel and hydraulic circuits

## Classification Note

MARINECLEAN™ is a **commercial product line**, not a filtration technology. It integrates multiple ELIMFILTERS® protection technologies (fuel, hydraulic, lube) into a marine-rated housing and element system. Listed under `/commercial-lines/`, not `/technologies/`.
