MICROKAPPA is a cabin air filtration technology certified to ISO 11155-1 and ISO 11155-2 that removes PM10 and PM2.5 particulate contamination from operator cab air supply in agricultural, construction, mining, railway, road transport, and municipal equipment. PM10 particles (≤10 µm aerodynamic diameter) penetrate the human respiratory system into the bronchial region; PM2.5 particles (≤2.5 µm) penetrate to the alveolar region of the lung, where gas exchange occurs, causing both acute and chronic respiratory effects. ISO 11155-1 governs the test methodology for measuring particulate filtration efficiency of cabin air filters; ISO 11155-2 governs the complementary test for gaseous contaminant (chemical vapor) filtration using activated carbon stages. MICROKAPPA elements are certified against both parts of ISO 11155, providing a documented efficiency baseline for operator health protection planning.

Operator exposure to agricultural and construction dust containing respirable crystalline silica (RCS), limestone dust, coal dust, and diesel particulate matter (DPM) in equipment cab environments without compliant cabin filtration is regulated under occupational health frameworks including EU Directive 2004/37/EC (carcinogens and mutagens at work) and similar national standards. The MICROKAPPA PM10 capture efficiency of >95% reduces operator in-cab RCS exposure to below regulated threshold limits for equipment operating in high-dust environments such as open-cut mining, grain harvesting, tunnel construction, and rail ballast maintenance. DIN 71220 establishes the German automotive standard for vehicle interior air filtration, providing a secondary design requirement framework that MICROKAPPA elements are engineered to satisfy. The activated carbon option extends MICROKAPPA capability to chemical vapor removal — relevant for agricultural applications involving pesticide and herbicide spray drift, and for underground mining environments where diesel exhaust gases and blasting fume byproducts may penetrate cab pressurisation seals.
