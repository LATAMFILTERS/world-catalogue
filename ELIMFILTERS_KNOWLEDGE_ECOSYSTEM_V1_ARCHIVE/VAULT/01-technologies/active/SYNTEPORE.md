SYNTEPORE is an all-synthetic filter media technology developed for air intake filtration in high-humidity and marine environments where cellulose-based media fail through moisture absorption, structural degradation, and reduced particle capture efficiency. Cellulose media absorb atmospheric moisture — particularly under salt-spray, condensation, and tropical operating conditions — increasing pressure differential, reducing air throughput, and risking structural collapse that allows unfiltered air bypass. SYNTEPORE eliminates this failure mode by using fully synthetic fiber construction that does not absorb water, maintaining dimensional stability and rated filtration efficiency across the full operating humidity range from desert-dry to fully saturated marine air.

The synthetic media architecture achieves filtration performance equivalent to or exceeding cellulose media under standard dry-air test conditions (ISO 5011, SAE J1539), while maintaining that performance under humidity conditions where cellulose elements would require early replacement or exhibit reduced efficiency. In marine and offshore applications where air contains both particulate contamination and salt spray, SYNTEPORE's hydrophobic fiber construction prevents salt crystal deposition within the media matrix — a failure mode that can create conductive bridges in sensitive intake systems and accelerate corrosion of housing components.
