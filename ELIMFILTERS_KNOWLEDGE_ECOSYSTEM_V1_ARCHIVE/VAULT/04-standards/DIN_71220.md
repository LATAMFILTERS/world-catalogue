DIN 71220 is the German standard for road vehicle cabin air filter requirements and testing, establishing performance criteria and test procedures for particle filtration efficiency, odour removal, and activated carbon filter performance for road vehicle passenger compartments. Published by DIN (Deutsches Institut für Normung), the standard was developed within the German automotive industry context and preceded the development of the international [[ISO_11155|ISO 11155]] standard, which harmonised and largely superseded DIN 71220 for new product development and international market certification. However, DIN 71220 remains actively referenced in European OEM technical specifications — particularly German OEM supplier qualification documents — where legacy requirements and long-standing OEM-specific test protocols continue to cite DIN 71220 alongside or in place of ISO 11155.

The test methodology of DIN 71220 covers particle filtration efficiency using representative dust challenge, airflow resistance measurement, and for combination filters, activated carbon layer performance against reference odour compounds. The performance thresholds established in DIN 71220 for particle filtration efficiency — greater than 80% at rated test conditions — were adopted into ISO 11155-1 with refinements to the test dust specification and flow condition standardisation. In practice, a cabin filter product that passes ISO 11155-1 will generally satisfy DIN 71220 particle efficiency requirements, though specific test condition differences mean formal DIN 71220 testing may be required separately for OEM qualification in German automotive supply chains where the DIN reference has not been replaced by an ISO equivalent in specifications.

DIN 71220 applies to road vehicles including passenger cars, light commercial vehicles, and buses — the same scope as ISO 11155 — making it relevant to [[AUTOMOTIVE|automotive]] applications including German OEM supply chains, [[BUS_COACH|bus and coach]] operator cab and passenger compartment filtration in European markets, and [[TRUCKS_FLEETS|truck fleet]] cab filtration where German OEM specifications govern filter qualification. [[MICROKAPPA|MICROKAPPA]] cabin air filtration systems developed for European market applications are qualified against both ISO 11155 and DIN 71220 where OEM supply chain requirements specify dual-standard compliance.
