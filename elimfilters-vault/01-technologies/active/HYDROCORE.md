# HYDROCORE™

HYDROCORE™ is the ELIMFILTERS fuel/water separation architecture for standard spin-on and cartridge separators within Fuel Cleanliness Protection -- covering separator applications outside the approved Turbine Series FH and FG architecture reserved for TURBOCORE™.

Its engineering role is to coordinate coalescing and separation media around the required flow, water-holding capacity, element service interval, installation and maintenance access of each approved standard (non-turbine) separator application.
