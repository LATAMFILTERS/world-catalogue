AQUAGUARD is the ELIMFILTERS fuel/water separation technology engineered to protect diesel injection systems from free water, emulsified water, condensation, microbial growth, and water-driven injector erosion. It applies a coalescing-then-barrier separation architecture: small water droplets are collected into larger droplets, separated from the fuel stream, and blocked from downstream delivery to high-pressure injection components.

Modern common-rail diesel injection systems operate at extreme pressure and very tight clearances. Water entering the injector circuit can cause nozzle erosion, needle-seat wear, corrosion, microbial contamination, fuel starvation, and premature filter plugging. AQUAGUARD is positioned as the primary engineering control for fuel cleanliness environments where stored diesel, humid operating conditions, poor fuel handling, or marine exposure make water contamination unavoidable.

AQUAGUARD applies across fuel/water separator assemblies, spin-on separator elements, cartridge separator systems, and turbine-style fuel separation configurations used in industrial, marine, off-highway, power generation, railway, truck fleet, and municipal equipment.
