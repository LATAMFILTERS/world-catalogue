# TURBOCORE™

TURBOCORE™ is the ELIMFILTERS dedicated fuel-separation architecture for approved turbine-style FH and FG applications within Fuel Cleanliness Protection.

Its engineering role is to coordinate staged fuel conditioning and separation around the required flow, separation duty, element micron rating, installation and maintenance access of each approved FH or FG application.
