TURBOCORE™ is the canonical ELIMFILTERS technology for FH/FG turbine-style fuel filtration and fuel/water separation.

Its application scope includes 1000FH, 900FH and 500FG assemblies and the corresponding 2010, 2040 and 2020 element families in 30, 10 and 2 micron grades where applicable. Flow, separation efficiency, restriction and service limits remain model- and element-specific and require validated product data.
