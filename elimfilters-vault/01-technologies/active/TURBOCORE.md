# TURBOCORE™

TURBOCORE™ is the ELIMFILTERS turbine-style fuel/water separation architecture reserved exclusively for approved FH and FG series systems. It is distinct from HYDROCORE™, which governs standard non-turbine separator filters with drain or transparent-bowl configurations.
