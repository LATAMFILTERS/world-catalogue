# MACROCORE™

MACROCORE™ is the ELIMFILTERS engine-air filtration architecture assigned to primary and secondary intake protection within Air Intake & Airflow Protection.

Its engineering role is to control airborne contamination before it reaches the engine by coordinating filtration media, sealing integrity, airflow management and service conditions.
