MACROCORE™ is ELIMFILTERS®' primary air intake protection technology. It applies a Progressive Density Gradient (PDG) architecture: each layer of the filter media increases in density from outside to inside, so large particles are captured first and fine particles are captured progressively deeper. This maximises dust-holding capacity without sacrificing filtration efficiency or flow restriction.

The technology is designed specifically for the contamination profile of heavy-duty off-highway equipment — mining haul trucks, drill rigs, agricultural harvesters, construction excavators — where ambient dust concentrations routinely exceed 5,000 mg/m³ and filter service life is a direct operational cost driver.

MACROCORE™ is ISO 5011 certified. The 62 PSI anti-collapse rating ensures the element maintains structural integrity during cold starts and sudden engine load increases.
