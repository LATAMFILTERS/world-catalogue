# NANOFORCE™

NANOFORCE™ is the ELIMFILTERS hydraulic filtration architecture for contamination control in fluid-power systems within Hydraulic Protection.

Its engineering role is to match media and element construction to flow, pressure, particle size, temperature and duty cycle. Cleanliness targets are established around the most contamination-sensitive hydraulic component and filter selection is validated against the approved application requirements.
