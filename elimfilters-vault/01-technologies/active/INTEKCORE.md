# INTEKCORE™

INTEKCORE™ is the ELIMFILTERS air-cleaner housing and sealing architecture for controlled airflow and bypass prevention within Air Intake & Airflow Protection.

Its engineering role is to preserve the protected intake boundary through housing geometry, structural integrity, element retention and seal loading. Housing sizing, inlet routing, restriction, element fit and sealing must be validated for the intended duty cycle.
