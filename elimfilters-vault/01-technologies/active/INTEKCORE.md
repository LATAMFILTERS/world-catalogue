INTEKCORE is a zero-bypass radial seal housing technology for air intake filtration systems in heavy-duty off-highway and on-highway diesel equipment. Conventional air filter housings with axial seal designs are susceptible to unfiltered air bypass when installation torque is inconsistent, housing deformation occurs under vibration, or filter element seating is incomplete — particularly on equipment operating under high-vibration mining and construction environments. INTEKCORE addresses this failure mode through a radial seal geometry that creates a continuous, self-centering interference fit between the filter element end-cap and the housing bore, independent of axial clamping force. This design eliminates the single most common source of unaccounted particle ingestion: installation-induced bypass.

The housing is manufactured from corrosion-resistant alloy to withstand the caustic wash cycles, mud exposure, and mechanical impact common to construction and mining equipment. The radial seal design also provides a consistent and audible confirmation of correct seating during filter element installation — the element seats positively when the radial seal engages — reducing maintenance error risk in field service environments. Under ISO 5011 test conditions, INTEKCORE housing designs achieve zero measurable bypass, meaning filter element efficiency testing under ISO 5011 protocols reflects actual installed performance without the bypass penalty common in axial-seal housing designs.
