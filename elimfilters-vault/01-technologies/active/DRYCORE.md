# DRYCORE™

DRYCORE™ is the ELIMFILTERS air-dryer filtration architecture for moisture control in pneumatic brake systems within Air Intake & Airflow Protection.

Its engineering role is to remove moisture from compressed air before condensation can affect pneumatic components. Capacity, purge behavior, airflow and replacement interval must be matched to compressor duty and ambient moisture exposure.
