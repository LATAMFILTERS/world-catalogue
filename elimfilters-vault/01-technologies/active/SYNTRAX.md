SYNTRAX is a full-flow lube oil filtration technology engineered to maintain engine and hydraulic oil cleanliness at ISO 4406 target codes of 16/14/11 or better throughout normal service intervals. Full-flow lube filtration means that the entire oil volume passes through the filter element on each circuit cycle — unlike bypass filtration which treats only a fraction of total flow per cycle. This architecture ensures that all oil reaching crankshaft bearings, connecting rod journals, camshaft lobes, and turbocharger bearings has passed through the filtration element, removing particles above the rated Beta efficiency threshold as classified under ISO 16889 multi-pass test methodology.

SYNTRAX media is formulated with synthetic fiber construction to achieve two simultaneous functions: high-efficiency particulate capture (multi-micron and sub-micron particle classes) and soot particle agglomeration. Diesel engine combustion generates soot particles in the 0.1–1 µm range that, at concentrations above 2% by weight in oil, form abrasive soot-oil paste that accelerates camshaft and bearing wear beyond the rate predicted by particle size alone. SYNTRAX media design captures soot agglomerates at concentrations above 2% by weight, preventing soot-induced viscosity shift and maintaining oil film integrity at journal bearing clearances. The combined particulate and soot capture performance, tested against ISO 16889 and DIN 51524 specifications, underpins the 3–5× bearing life extension associated with maintaining ISO 4406 16/14/11 cleanliness versus commodity filter performance at 19/17/14 or worse.
