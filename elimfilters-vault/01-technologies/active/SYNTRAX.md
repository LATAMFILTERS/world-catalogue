# SYNTRAX™

SYNTRAX™ is the ELIMFILTERS lubrication filtration architecture for controlling wear debris and lubricant contamination within Lubrication Protection.

Its engineering role is to balance filtration performance, contaminant capacity, pressure drop and valve integrity across the lubricant service interval. Selection must be matched to engine duty and lubricant condition.
