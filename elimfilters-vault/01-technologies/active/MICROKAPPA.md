# MICROKAPPA™

MICROKAPPA™ is the ELIMFILTERS cabin-air protection architecture for particulate and selected gaseous-contaminant control within Air Intake & Airflow Protection.

Its engineering role is to match cabin filtration media to operator exposure, airflow demand and HVAC pressure-drop limits for the intended operating environment.
