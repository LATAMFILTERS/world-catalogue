Mining is the highest contamination-exposure operating environment in the ELIMFILTERS® industry classification. Open-cut operations expose haul trucks and drill rigs to ambient silica and hardrock dust concentrations of 5,000–15,000 mg/m³ — compared to the ISO 5011 test standard's baseline of 30–300 mg/m³. This 50× gap between test standard and real-world exposure means filtration selection and change intervals are critical operational decisions, not routine maintenance.

The primary contamination threat is particle wear: silica and hardrock dust ingested through air intake systems reaches engine cylinders, turbocharger bearings, and lube oil circuits, accelerating abrasive wear at rates that compress engine overhaul intervals from 25,000 hours to as low as 3,000 hours in unmanaged installations.

Secondary threats are hydraulic contamination (high-precision proportional valve circuits in haul trucks and excavators) and diesel water contamination (bulk fuel storage exposed to condensation cycles in extreme temperature ranges).
