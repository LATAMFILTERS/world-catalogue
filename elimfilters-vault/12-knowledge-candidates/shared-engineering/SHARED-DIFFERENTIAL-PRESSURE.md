## Technical Relationships — Awaiting Validation

- Excessive pressure difference across the filter can trigger bypass operation so lubricant flow can continue around the restricted media. | status: awaiting_validation
- During bypass operation, lubrication flow may continue while filtration through the main media is temporarily reduced or absent. | status: awaiting_validation
- A bypass setting must be treated as application-specific rather than as a universal filter value. | status: awaiting_validation
- A restricted or incorrectly specified filter can be one contributor to abnormal lubrication pressure, but low oil pressure requires system-level diagnosis. | status: awaiting_validation
- Diagnosis should separate filter restriction from lubricant, pump, engine-clearance and other lubrication-system causes. | status: awaiting_validation
- Lower lubricant temperature generally increases viscosity, which can increase resistance to flow during cold-start conditions. | status: awaiting_validation
- Cold-start flow demand can increase pressure differential across the filter and make correct application-specific flow and bypass characteristics important. | status: awaiting_validation
