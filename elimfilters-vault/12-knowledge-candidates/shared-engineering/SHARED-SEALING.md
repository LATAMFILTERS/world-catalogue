## Technical Relationships — Awaiting Validation

- Physical similarity does not establish functional interchangeability between oil filters. | status: awaiting_validation
- Correct fitment requires compatible sealing, attachment geometry and application-specific flow and pressure-control requirements. | status: awaiting_validation
- Oil-filter architecture combines the filtration element with structural, sealing and flow-control components whose functions depend on the application. | status: awaiting_validation
- Spin-on and cartridge designs package these functions differently even when both perform engine-lubricant filtration. | status: awaiting_validation
- Engine-air-filter designs must provide particle capture while maintaining acceptable airflow and sealing within the intake housing. | status: awaiting_validation
- Media construction and filter geometry affect contaminant capacity, restriction behavior and fitment. | status: awaiting_validation
