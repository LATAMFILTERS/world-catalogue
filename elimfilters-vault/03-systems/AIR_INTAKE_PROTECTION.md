Air Intake & Airflow Protection Systems protect combustion engines, turbochargers, cylinders, operators, and intake assemblies from airborne particle exposure, dust ingestion, seal bypass, humidity-driven media degradation, and airflow restriction. This system domain includes primary air filters, secondary/safety elements, radial seal elements, air cleaner housings, intake sealing architecture, and airflow management components.
