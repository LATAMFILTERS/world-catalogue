# HYDRAULIC — Hydraulic Filtration Domain

The hydraulic domain addresses particle contamination in closed hydraulic circuits where component clearances of 1–25 µm require strict ISO 4406 cleanliness maintenance to prevent accelerated wear and valve malfunction.

## Contamination Targets

- Hard particles (silica, metallic wear debris) above 4 µm
- Silt-sized particles (1–4 µm) causing silting in proportional valve spools
- Water ingress causing hydraulic fluid oxidation and additive depletion

## Applicable Standards

- **ISO 16889** — Multi-pass filter efficiency test methodology (Beta ratio)
- **ISO 4406** — Particle cleanliness code classification (target: 17/15/12 to 16/14/11)
- **NFPA T2.14** — Hydraulic fluid power contamination control

## Primary Technology

NANOFORCE — high-Beta hydraulic filter elements for inline and return-line applications, maintaining ISO 4406 16/14/11 to 17/15/12 cleanliness targets for proportional valve and piston pump protection.

## Failure Mechanism

Particle contamination above cleanliness target → spool valve silting → erratic actuator response → proportional valve replacement. Hard particles → piston pump barrel wear → internal leakage → system pressure loss → equipment downtime. Average hydraulic system contamination accounts for 70–80% of premature component failures in mobile equipment.
