# FUEL — Fuel Filtration Domain

The fuel domain addresses particle and water contamination in diesel fuel circuits, with particular focus on HPCR injection systems operating at pressures above 1,800 bar where injector clearances are 2–4 µm.

## Contamination Targets

- Free water and emulsified water (causes injector corrosion and microbial growth)
- Particulate matter above 4 µm (causes injector stiction and nozzle wear)
- Microbial contamination (Hormoconis resinae fungal growth at diesel-water interface)

## Applicable Standards

- **ASTM D6304** — Water content measurement in petroleum products (Karl Fischer method)
- **ISO 12937** — Water determination in petroleum products

## Primary Technology

SYNTAPORE — diesel-fuel particulate filtration elements for primary, secondary and cartridge fuel-filter applications. TURBOCORE — coalescing water separator elements for free and emulsified water removal.

## Failure Mechanism

Water contamination → injector corrosion and microbial growth → injector stiction → misfires and power loss. Particle contamination above 4 µm → injector nozzle erosion → fuel spray pattern degradation → incomplete combustion → increased particulate emissions and engine damage.
