# CABIN — Cabin Air Filtration Domain

The cabin domain addresses airborne particulate and chemical contamination inside operator cabs of mobile equipment operating in dusty, chemically active, or biologically hazardous environments.

## Contamination Targets

- PM10 and PM2.5 mineral dust particles (silica, coal dust, grain dust)
- Chemical vapors from pesticides, fertilizers, and fuel combustion
- Biological contaminants in agricultural environments
- Diesel exhaust particulate from surrounding equipment

## Applicable Standards

- **ISO 11155** — Road vehicle cabin air filtration test method
- **DIN 71220** — Cabin air filter classification and performance

## Primary Technology

MICROKAPPA — cabin air filter elements with multi-layer synthetic media for PM10/PM2.5 capture, with activated carbon variants for chemical vapor environments.

## Failure Mechanism

Inadequate cabin filtration → operator exposure to respirable dust → occupational lung disease (silicosis, coal workers' pneumoconiosis) and chemical toxicity. Regulatory impact: exceeding OSHA/NIOSH PEL thresholds creates occupational health liability.
