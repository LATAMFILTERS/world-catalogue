# OIL — Lube Oil Filtration Domain

The lube oil domain addresses particle and oxidation contamination in engine crankcase circuits, transmission fluid circuits, and bearing lubrication systems across diesel and gasoline engines.

## Contamination Targets

- Metallic wear particles (iron, copper, aluminum) generated by engine break-in and normal wear
- Silica ingestion from degraded air intake filtration
- Soot accumulation from combustion blow-by in diesel engines
- Oxidation byproducts that increase oil viscosity and reduce film strength

## Applicable Standards

- **ISO 4406** — Particle cleanliness code classification (target: 16/14/11 for engine bearings)
- **ISO 16889** — Multi-pass filter efficiency test (Beta ratio methodology)
- **DIN 51524** — German specification for hydraulic and lube oil compatibility

## Primary Technology

SYNTRAX — full-flow lube oil filter elements with synthetic media targeting ISO 4406 16/14/11 cleanliness for engine bearing protection.

## Failure Mechanism

Contamination particles in oil → abrasive wear of bearing surfaces → bearing clearance increase → oil pressure drop → lubrication film breakdown → bearing seizure. Measured impact: bearing life reduction from 15,000+ hours to under 3,000 hours with uncontrolled contamination.
