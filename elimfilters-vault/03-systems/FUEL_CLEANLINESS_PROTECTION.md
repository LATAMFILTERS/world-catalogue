Fuel Cleanliness Protection Systems protect diesel injection equipment, high-pressure common-rail injectors, fuel pumps, separators, and stored fuel programs from water, particulate contamination, microbial growth, filter plugging, and fuel starvation.

Fuel Cleanliness Protection Systems are the ELIMFILTERS system domain controlling water, particles, microbial contamination, and fuel restriction before diesel fuel reaches pumps, injectors, and high-pressure common-rail components.

SYSTEMS
Fuel filters, fuel/water separators, turbine-style separation assemblies, spin-on fuel separators, cartridge fuel filters, bulk fuel protection, and fuel cleanliness programs for industrial diesel assets.

FAILURE_IMPACT
Diesel water and particulate contamination → injector erosion, needle-seat wear, pump scoring, corrosion, microbial growth, filter plugging, fuel starvation, poor combustion, emergency start failure, and unplanned downtime. In high-pressure common-rail systems, small contamination events can create expensive injector and pump failures.
