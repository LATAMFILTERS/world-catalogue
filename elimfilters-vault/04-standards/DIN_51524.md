# DIN 51524 — Hydraulic and Lube Oil Specification

DIN 51524 defines the performance classifications for hydraulic and lube oils used in industrial and mobile equipment. It specifies fluid properties that determine lubricant compatibility with filtration media, seals, and metal surfaces.

## Parts

- **DIN 51524-1** — Minimum hydraulic oil (HL category): rust and oxidation inhibited
- **DIN 51524-2** — Hydraulic oil (HLP category): anti-wear additives included
- **DIN 51524-3** — High-viscosity index hydraulic oil (HVLP category): viscosity stability across temperature ranges

## Relevance to Filtration

Filtration media must be chemically compatible with DIN 51524 fluids. SYNTRAX filter elements are tested for compatibility with DIN 51524 HLP and HVLP class fluids, ensuring no media degradation or additive stripping under normal operating conditions.

## Relationship to ISO Standards

DIN 51524 defines fluid quality; ISO 4406 defines contamination cleanliness codes. Both apply simultaneously in lube oil systems — the fluid must meet DIN 51524 composition requirements while particle contamination must meet ISO 4406 cleanliness targets.
