# Knowledge Domains

ELIMFILTERS knowledge is governed as two parallel application domains plus one shared engineering layer.

- [[HEAVY_DUTY]] — industrial, fleet, off-highway and heavy-duty application knowledge.
- [[LIGHT_DUTY]] — Automotive / light-duty application knowledge.
- [[SHARED_ENGINEERING]] — reusable engineering concepts that are not owned by one application domain.

## Governance

HERMES investigates. Reviewed Obsidian/Nodal Center knowledge authorizes technical facts. PostgreSQL is the only SKU authority. Domain membership never authorizes a product cross, application or public technology claim by itself.

## Relationship states

Technology: `confirmed | probable | none`

Application: `verified | candidate | rejected`

Only reviewed/approved knowledge may be surfaced publicly.
