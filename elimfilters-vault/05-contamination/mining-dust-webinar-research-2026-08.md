## Generic engineering concepts that may be independently expressed by ELIMFILTERS

- Abrasive particulate can accelerate wear at precision mechanical interfaces.
- Dust accumulation can increase restriction or obstruct flow paths.
- Dust accumulation can impair heat rejection and contribute to thermal burden.
- Particulate can interfere with sensors, optical surfaces and control components.
- Contamination introduced during assembly, commissioning, operation or maintenance can affect reliability behavior across the asset lifecycle.
- Filtration economics should consider the value, sensitivity and operating consequence of the protected component rather than filter purchase price alone.
- Condition-aware maintenance can use restriction trend, fluid cleanliness, operating hours, service history, environment and inspection evidence.
- Contamination control should be treated as part of an overall asset-protection strategy, not as the sole determinant of component life.

## Claims requiring independent validation before any public use

Do not publish the following values as ELIMFILTERS claims without primary-source verification, scope definition and technical review:

- “75–80% of hydraulic and pneumatic failures are caused exclusively by contamination.”
- “70% of manufacturing losses are linked to human handling or maintenance error.”
- 60,000–72,000 operating-hour design-life claims for long-life mining components.
- Any universal statement that major long-life components are never stocked and are always made to order.
- Any claimed reduction of 50% in filtration-system footprint.
- Any claimed maintenance-time reduction from 24 hours to 30 minutes.
- Any claimed annual maintenance-cost reduction greater than 70%.
- Any claimed 8,000-hour or 16-month field result, zero restriction events, or 100% elimination of dust caking.
