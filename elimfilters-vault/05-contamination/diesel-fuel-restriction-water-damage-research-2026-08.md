# Diesel fuel restriction and water-contamination research

Internal evidence only. Competitor brands, proprietary claims and source branding must not appear in ELIMFILTERS public-facing content.

## Validated neutral engineering principles

- Excessive fuel-filter or separator restriction can reduce available fuel supply and contribute to reduced power, surging or fuel-starvation symptoms, especially under load.
- Water contamination can contribute to corrosion, wear and damage in pumps, injectors and other precision diesel-fuel components.
- Bulk-fuel storage can introduce water, sediment, rust, dust and microbial contamination.
- Microbial growth commonly develops where water is present in stored diesel and can contribute to premature filter plugging.
- Repeated early plugging should trigger a review of storage, transfer, fuel condition and maintenance rather than repeated element replacement alone.
- Product-level efficiency, pressure drop, flow, water-holding capacity and service intervals must remain tied to validated product data.

## HYDROCORE scope guardrail

HYDROCORE™ governs approved standard non-turbine fuel/water separator configurations, including drain and transparent-bowl applications. It must not be used for FH/FG turbine systems (TURBOCORE™) or plain particulate-only diesel filtration (SYNTAPORE™).

## Public claim restrictions

Do not publish universal pressure figures, repair-cost figures, micron claims, efficiency percentages, service-life multipliers or statements that a clogged separator will necessarily destroy a pump or injector. Public wording should describe risk mechanisms and symptoms conservatively and tie numeric performance to validated product/application data.
