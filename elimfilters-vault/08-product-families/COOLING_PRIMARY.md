The Cooling System Protection family contains all THERMACORE™-technology coolant filtration elements for heavy-duty cooling circuits. This family covers 76 ELIMFILTERS® SKUs (verified directly against the production catalog), spanning cellulose and synthetic media configurations across commercial transport and industrial equipment.

Coolant filtration supports cleanliness of passages, seals and heat-transfer surfaces, and where applicable maintains supplemental coolant additive (SCA) concentration within the approved cooling-system maintenance strategy.
