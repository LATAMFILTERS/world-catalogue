The Air Cleaner Housings family contains all INTEKCORE™-technology components for the engine air-intake system -- housing bodies, element-retention hardware, seals and bypass-prevention architecture. This family covers 493 ELIMFILTERS® SKUs (verified directly against the production catalog).

Housings are the structural boundary that determines whether unfiltered air can bypass the MACROCORE™ filtration element they contain. Housing sizing, inlet routing, restriction, element fit and sealing must be validated for the intended duty cycle.
