## Technical Relationships — Validated

- Correct heavy-duty fuel filtration depends on sealing geometry and housing interface compatibility, not merely on external dimensions. | status: approved
- Center-tube, gasket-seat, thread-ring and bowl interfaces can affect whether a filter seals and routes fuel correctly. | status: approved
- A component that appears physically similar may still be incompatible if a sealing or engagement feature differs. | status: approved
- Housing and interface configuration must be treated as application-specific evidence during fitment validation. | status: approved

## Diagnostic Methods

- Verify gasket location and contact surface.
- Verify center-tube/housing engagement and thread or bowl compatibility where present.
- Confirm that the intended flow path is preserved after installation.
