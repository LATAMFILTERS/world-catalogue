## Technical Relationships — Validated

- HE-CRIVA™ addresses fine airborne particulate where the required cleanliness level is more demanding than general ventilation duty. | status: approved

- Staged prefiltration may be used to protect final high-efficiency stages from premature loading. | status: approved

## Problems

- Unexpected cleanliness loss
- Premature final-stage loading
- Seal or frame leakage
- Pressure drop outside expected trend

## Failure Modes

- Filter-to-housing bypass
- Damaged or improperly seated final-stage filter
- Premature loading caused by inadequate upstream particulate control

## Diagnostic Methods

- Inspect final-stage sealing surfaces, frame seating and housing condition.
- Trend differential pressure rather than relying only on elapsed service time.
- Verify that upstream stages are controlling loading as intended.
- Use the project-defined integrity or cleanliness verification method when required.

## Operating Conditions

- Defined cleanliness requirement
- Fine-particle challenge
- Strict bypass control
- Final-stage pressure-drop sensitivity
- Controlled production or critical ventilation environment
