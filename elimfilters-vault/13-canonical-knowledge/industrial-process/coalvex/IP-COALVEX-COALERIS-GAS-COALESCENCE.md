## Technical Relationships — Validated

- COALERIS™ addresses fine liquid aerosols and small entrained droplets carried in natural-gas and industrial process-gas streams. | status: approved
- Coalescing media captures fine liquid droplets and promotes their growth into larger droplets that can drain away from the gas stream. | status: approved
- Reliable drainage is part of coalescing performance because retained liquid, flooding or re-entrainment can increase carryover and differential pressure. | status: approved
- Gas composition, pressure, temperature, flow turndown, aerosol loading and liquid properties affect coalescing duty and service behavior. | status: approved
- Element sealing and the intended flow path must be preserved to prevent bypass around the coalescing media. | status: approved
- COALERIS™ identifies the filter-element family and does not by itself represent the pressure vessel, separator, drains, instrumentation or piping as ELIMFILTERS products. | status: approved
- COALERIS™ maps to TC-NG-01 within the COALVEX™ platform. | status: approved

## Failure Modes

- Coalescing-media saturation or overload
- Poor liquid drainage or flooding
- Re-entrainment of separated liquid
- Element-to-housing bypass
- Media or element mismatch to gas and liquid conditions

## Symptoms

- Liquid appears downstream of the coalescing stage
- Differential pressure rises outside the expected duty trend
- Drainage becomes unstable or liquid accumulates in the stage
- Element service interval shortens after gas or liquid conditions change
- Downstream equipment shows evidence of liquid exposure

## Root Causes

- Fine-aerosol loading exceeds the validated element duty
- Liquid properties or gas conditions differ from the selection basis
- Drain path is restricted or unable to remove separated liquid
- Gas velocity or turndown behavior promotes re-entrainment
- Element sealing or seating allows bypass

## Diagnostic Methods

- Confirm gas composition, pressure, temperature, flow and turndown.
- Characterize aerosol size, liquid loading and liquid physical properties.
- Inspect element seating, seals and intended flow path.
- Inspect drainage behavior and evidence of flooding or re-entrainment.
- Distinguish fine-aerosol coalescing problems from upstream bulk-liquid carryover.

## Corrective Actions

- Re-evaluate element and media selection when gas or liquid conditions differ from the selection basis.
- Correct sealing or seating defects before attributing carryover solely to media performance.
- Restore reliable drainage and remove causes of flooding or liquid accumulation.
- Add or requalify upstream bulk separation when incoming free liquid or large-droplet loading overwhelms the coalescing stage.
