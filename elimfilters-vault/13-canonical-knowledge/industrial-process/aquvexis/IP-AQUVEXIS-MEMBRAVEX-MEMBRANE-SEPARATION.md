# MEMBRAVEX Membrane Separation

## Technical Relationships — Validated

- MEMBRAVEX™ covers reverse-osmosis, ultrafiltration and nanofiltration treatment paths. | status: approved
- Membrane selection follows feed-water analysis, target permeate condition, pressure, recovery, fouling and scaling potential. | status: approved
- Pretreatment is part of membrane protection and cannot be separated from membrane performance assessment. | status: approved
- RO/NF element test methods must not be represented as universal ultrafiltration standards. | status: approved

## Components

- Membrane element
- Pressure-vessel or module interface
- Pretreatment filtration
- Feed, permeate and concentrate sampling points
- Pressure and flow monitoring

## Problems

- Permeate-quality deterioration
- Membrane fouling
- Scaling
- Loss of normalized flow or rejection

## Failure Modes

- Pretreatment inadequate
- Membrane chemistry incompatible
- Scaling potential uncontrolled
- Element integrity or sealing defect

## Symptoms

- Permeate conductivity rises
- Pressure demand rises
- Recovery declines
- Cleaning frequency increases

## Root Causes

- Feed-water condition changed
- Fouling load increased
- Scaling control insufficient
- Oxidant or chemical exposure exceeded compatibility

## Diagnostic Methods

- Characterize feed, permeate and concentrate.
- Normalize flow and quality against operating conditions where applicable.
- Review pressure, recovery and fouling trends.
- Inspect sealing and element integrity.

## Corrective Actions

- Correct pretreatment or scaling control.
- Requalify membrane chemistry and operating envelope.
- Correct sealing or integrity defects.
- Verify post-service permeate performance.

## Maintenance Procedures

- Trend normalized membrane performance.
- Maintain pretreatment.
- Monitor pressure, recovery and permeate quality.
- Apply validated cleaning and replacement criteria.

## Operating Conditions

- Feed-water chemistry
- Temperature

- Recovery

- Scaling potential
- Required permeate quality
- Membrane material compatibility

## Standards

- ISO 20468-5:2021 — membrane-filtration performance evaluation for water-reuse systems.
- ISO 25175:2026 — air-tightness, permeation and salt-rejection test methods for RO and NF membrane elements within its marine-technology scope; it does not cover UF.
- ISO 23044:2020 — industrial-wastewater softening/desalination guidance including NF and RO where reuse is the project context.
- ASTM D6908-25 — integrity testing of water-filtration membrane systems where that system test applies.

## Governance

- MEMBRAVEX™ does not assign one universal rejection, recovery, pressure or service-life value across RO, UF and NF.
- Standard scope follows the membrane process and project; RO/NF methods are not transferred automatically to UF.
- MEMBRAVEX™ describes membrane-element treatment paths; complete membrane trains, pressure vessels, pumps and controls remain application equipment unless separately approved.
