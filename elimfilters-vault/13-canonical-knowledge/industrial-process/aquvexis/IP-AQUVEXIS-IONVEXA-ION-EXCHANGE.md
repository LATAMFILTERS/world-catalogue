## Failure Modes

- Capacity exhausted
- Regeneration incomplete
- Competing ions consume capacity
- Resin fouled or degraded

## Symptoms

- Target ion appears downstream
- Service cycle shortens
- Regeneration recovery declines
- Outlet quality varies

## Root Causes

- Feed ionic composition changed
- Resin selection mismatched
- Regeneration conditions incorrect
- Fouling load increased

## Diagnostic Methods

- Analyze feed and outlet ionic composition.
- Trend service capacity and breakthrough.
- Review regenerant concentration and dose where used.
- Inspect for fouling or media degradation.

## Corrective Actions

- Requalify resin against feed chemistry.
- Correct regeneration conditions.
- Address fouling source.
- Replace exhausted or degraded media.

## Maintenance Procedures

- Trend outlet quality and capacity.
- Track regeneration performance.
- Preserve representative sampling.
- Inspect media condition on service.

## Operating Conditions

- Feed ionic composition

- pH
- Regeneration strategy
- Required outlet quality

## Standards

- ISO 20468-6:2021 — performance evaluation of ion exchange and electrodialysis in water-reuse systems, including ion-exchange resin and membrane.
- ISO 23044:2020 — industrial-wastewater softening/desalination guidance including ion exchange where reuse is the project context.
- ASTM D1782-26 — operating performance of particulate cation-exchange materials.
- Additional anion- or mixed-bed test methods are selected according to the actual resin configuration rather than assigned universally across IONVEXA™.
