# AQUVEXIS Electrodeionization

> **ELIMFILTERS CANONICAL KNOWLEDGE.** Approved industrial electrodeionization treatment knowledge.

- Domain: INDUSTRIAL_PROCESS_KNOWLEDGE_DOMAIN

- Systems: Industrial & Process
- Platforms: AQUVEXIS™

## Technical Relationships — Validated

- Electrodeionization is an electrically assisted ionic-polishing treatment used after suitable upstream treatment. | status: approved
- Continuous electrodeionization performance depends on feed conductivity, ionic loading, pretreatment quality, flow and electrical operating conditions. | status: approved
- Conductivity or resistivity verification is part of performance assessment but does not alone diagnose all fouling or chemistry problems. | status: approved
- Electrodeionization remains a descriptive AQUVEXIS™ treatment family and is not an independent ELIMFILTERS technology mark. | status: approved

## Components

- Electrodeionization module
- Ion-exchange media
- Ion-selective membranes
- Electrical power interface
- Feed and product-water monitoring points

## Problems

- Product-water conductivity drift
- Ionic leakage
- Scaling or fouling
- Unstable electrical demand

## Failure Modes

- Pretreatment insufficient
- Feed conductivity outside qualified range
- Scaling or fouling develops
- Electrical operating condition outside qualified duty

## Symptoms

- Product resistivity declines
- Current demand changes
- Pressure drop rises
- Product quality becomes unstable

## Root Causes

- RO or upstream pretreatment deterioration
- Feed chemistry changed
- Scaling control insufficient
- Module contamination or degradation

## Diagnostic Methods

- Trend feed and product conductivity/resistivity.
- Review flow, pressure and electrical conditions.
- Analyze feed chemistry and scaling potential.
- Confirm upstream RO or pretreatment performance.

## Corrective Actions

- Correct upstream pretreatment.
- Requalify feed and operating window.
- Correct scaling or fouling source.
- Service or replace the module according to validated condition evidence.

## Maintenance Procedures

- Trend conductivity/resistivity and operating current.
- Monitor pressure and flow.
- Maintain upstream membrane and pretreatment stages.
- Verify product-water quality after service.

## Operating Conditions

- Feed conductivity
- Ionic composition
- Flow
- Pressure
- Temperature
- Electrical operating window
- Scaling potential
- Required product-water resistivity

## Standards

- ISO 23044:2020 — guidance for industrial-wastewater softening/desalination for reuse and explicitly includes electrodeionization among the covered processes.
- ASTM D6807-17(2026) — operating-performance test method for continuous electrodeionization devices on qualifying reverse-osmosis permeate.
- ASTM D1125-23 — electrical conductivity and resistivity measurement of water for applicable feed/product verification.
- Standard scope and feed-water limits must be respected; these methods do not create a universal EDI performance claim.
