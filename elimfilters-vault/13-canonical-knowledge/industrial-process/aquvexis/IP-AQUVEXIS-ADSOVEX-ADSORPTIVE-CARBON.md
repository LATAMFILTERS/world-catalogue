# ADSOVEX Adsorptive Carbon Treatment

## Technical Relationships — Validated

- ADSOVEX™ uses application-qualified adsorptive carbon media for selected dissolved constituents and residual oxidants. | status: approved
- Carbon media properties such as particle size, ash and iodine number characterize the media but do not by themselves prove capacity for every target contaminant. | status: approved
- Breakthrough depends on target chemistry, concentration, competing constituents, contact conditions and media loading. | status: approved
- Pretreatment and solids control can materially affect carbon-bed or carbon-element service behavior. | status: approved

## Components

- Activated-carbon media or element
- Housing or media-retention interface

## Problems

- Selected dissolved-organic challenge
- Residual oxidant challenge
- Premature breakthrough
- Excessive media pressure drop

## Failure Modes

- Media not matched to target chemistry
- Capacity exhausted
- Competing constituents consume capacity
- Particulate loading blinds the media

## Symptoms

- Target constituent appears downstream
- Service interval shortens
- Pressure drop rises
- Outlet quality becomes unstable

## Root Causes

- Feed chemistry changed
- Media capacity undersized
- Contact conditions insufficient
- Pretreatment ineffective

## Diagnostic Methods

- Identify target constituent and inlet concentration.
- Review competing species and contact conditions.
- Trend target breakthrough with media duty.
- Inspect particulate loading and pressure drop.

## Corrective Actions

- Correct upstream contamination where practical.
- Requalify carbon media against the target chemistry.
- Requalify media quantity and contact duty.
- Improve particulate pretreatment where required.

## Maintenance Procedures

- Trend inlet/outlet target constituent.
- Track media duty and breakthrough.
- Inspect loading and flow distribution.
- Replace media using validated service criteria.

## Operating Conditions

- Target constituent
- Inlet concentration
- Water chemistry
- Competing constituents
- Flow and contact conditions
- Temperature and pH
- Particulate loading

## Standards

- ASTM D4607-14 — iodine number of activated carbon as a relative porosity indicator; it is not a universal measure of adsorption capacity for other species.
- ASTM D2862-16(2022) — particle-size distribution of granular activated carbon.
- ASTM D2866-11(2025) — total ash content of activated carbon.
- ISO 20468-1:2018 — general treatment-performance evaluation where ADSOVEX™ is used within a water-reuse system.
