## Technical Relationships — Validated

- Oil-filter architecture combines the filtration element with structural, sealing and flow-control components whose functions depend on the application. | status: approved
- Spin-on and cartridge designs package these functions differently even when both perform engine-lubricant filtration. | status: approved
- Layered cabin-filter media can be designed for a specific airflow direction, so installation orientation is a functional requirement rather than a cosmetic choice. | status: approved
- Engine lubrication, engine air intake and cabin-air filtration protect different exposure paths and should be managed as coordinated protection systems rather than unrelated service items. | status: approved
- Correct selection, installation and service condition are common requirements across these filtration systems. | status: approved
