## Technical Relationships — Validated

- Oil-filter protection depends on balancing particle capture efficiency, contaminant-holding capacity and lubricant flow rather than optimizing a single parameter. | status: approved
- A micron value does not define filtration performance unless it is paired with capture efficiency at that particle size. | status: approved
- Excessive pressure difference across the filter can trigger bypass operation so lubricant flow can continue around the restricted media. | status: approved
- During bypass operation, lubrication flow may continue while filtration through the main media is temporarily reduced or absent. | status: approved
- A bypass setting must be treated as application-specific rather than as a universal filter value. | status: approved
- Increasing contaminant loading consumes available media capacity and can raise flow restriction. | status: approved
- Media saturation can increase pressure differential and raise the likelihood of bypass operation in systems designed with a bypass function. | status: approved
- A restricted or incorrectly specified filter can be one contributor to abnormal lubrication pressure, but low oil pressure requires system-level diagnosis. | status: approved
- Diagnosis should separate filter restriction from lubricant, pump, engine-clearance and other lubrication-system causes. | status: approved
- Lower lubricant temperature generally increases viscosity, which can increase resistance to flow during cold-start conditions. | status: approved
- Cold-start flow demand can increase pressure differential across the filter and make correct application-specific flow and bypass characteristics important. | status: approved
- Engine-air-filter service life varies with dust exposure, operating environment and vehicle usage rather than mileage alone. | status: approved
- Higher airborne contaminant loading can accelerate media loading and increase intake restriction. | status: approved
- Engine-air-filter designs must provide particle capture while maintaining acceptable airflow and sealing within the intake housing. | status: approved
- Media construction and filter geometry affect contaminant capacity, restriction behavior and fitment. | status: approved
- Cabin-filter service interval is affected by environmental particulate loading, seasonal conditions and vehicle usage. | status: approved
- High contaminant exposure can shorten effective cabin-filter service life compared with operation in cleaner conditions. | status: approved
- Seasonal temperature and airborne-contamination changes can alter filtration loading, fluid-flow behavior and maintenance demand across multiple vehicle systems. | status: approved
- Seasonal maintenance should verify filter condition and application requirements rather than rely on one universal replacement interval. | status: approved
