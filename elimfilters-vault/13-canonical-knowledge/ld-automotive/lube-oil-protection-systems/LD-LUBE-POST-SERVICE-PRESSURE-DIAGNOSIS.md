## Technical Relationships — Validated

- Low or delayed oil pressure after service is not sufficient evidence that the replacement filter is restrictive or defective. | status: approved
- Loss of pump prime, low oil level, pickup-side problems, pressure-regulation faults, drain-back behavior and filter/application mismatch are distinct diagnostic branches. | status: approved
- Diagnosis should verify actual lubrication-system pressure and system condition before assigning causation to the filter. | status: approved

## Diagnostic Methods

- Verify oil level and obvious leakage.
- Confirm correct filter/application and sealing condition.
- Verify actual oil pressure with an appropriate diagnostic method when a warning persists.
- Evaluate pump-prime and pressure-control conditions according to the specific engine service procedure.

## Governance

- No generic priming procedure is authorized as universal.
- No numeric pressure threshold is canonicalized from legacy service literature.
- Heavy Duty service procedures and thresholds must not be inherited.
