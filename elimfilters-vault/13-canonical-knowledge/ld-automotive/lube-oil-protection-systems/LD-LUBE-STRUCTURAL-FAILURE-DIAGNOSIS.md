# Oil Filter Structural Failure Diagnosis

> **ELIMFILTERS CANONICAL KNOWLEDGE.** Approved for Light Duty Automotive use only.

## Knowledge Position

- Domain: LIGHT_DUTY_KNOWLEDGE_DOMAIN
- Industries: Automotive
- Systems: Lube/Oil Protection Systems
- Technologies: SYNTRAX™

## Failure Modes

- Center-tube or element structural collapse
- Seal displacement associated with abnormal loading
- Media deformation under excessive pressure differential

## Root Causes — Validated

- Structural damage can be a downstream symptom of abnormal system differential pressure rather than proof of a manufacturing defect. | status: approved
- Excessive restriction, cold/high-viscosity oil, contamination loading or malfunctioning pressure-control architecture can increase structural loading on a filter element. | status: approved
- Failure analysis must evaluate the lubrication system and operating condition before assigning root cause to the filter itself. | status: approved

## Diagnostic Methods

- Inspect the failed element and housing for deformation pattern and sealing condition.
- Review oil condition, service interval, cold-start history and evidence of excessive restriction.
- Verify bypass/regulating valve architecture and application suitability.

## Corrective Actions

- Correct the system or application condition that produced abnormal loading before installing a replacement element.
