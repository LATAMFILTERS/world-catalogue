## Root Causes

- Damaged or incorrectly seated seal
- Incorrect filter fitment
- Installation error
- Housing or sealing-surface damage

## Diagnostic Methods

- Inspect filter fitment, gasket position, sealing surface and housing condition

## Corrective Actions

- Correct the sealing or fitment condition and verify the system is leak-free after service
