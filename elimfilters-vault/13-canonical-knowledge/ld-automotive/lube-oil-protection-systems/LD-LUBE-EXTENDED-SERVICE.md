## Problems

## Root Causes

- Extended drain interval without matching filter capacity
- Thermal and chemical exposure over a longer service period

## Diagnostic Methods

- Compare filter service capability with the vehicle and lubricant service strategy

## Corrective Actions

- Use a filter whose validated service capability matches the intended maintenance interval
