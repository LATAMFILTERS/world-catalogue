# Replace Me

## Evidence type

Choose one: laboratory test, field validation, standard, supplier specification, engineering calculation, simulation, inspection record, or customer operating record.

## Scope

Define exactly what claim, entity, product, or architecture this evidence supports.

## Method

Describe test conditions, measurement method, sample size, operating environment, and controls.

## Result

Record measured or observed results without promotional interpretation.

## Limitations

Document uncertainty, exclusions, transferability limits, and any conditions under which the result should not be generalized.

## Verification

Record reviewer, review date, and whether the evidence is sufficient for public claims.
