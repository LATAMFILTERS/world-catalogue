## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This plan launches the first active ELIMFILTERS® distributor recruitment campaign. The goal is not to generate applications — it is to recruit two to four qualified, established industrial distributors in the first 90 days. Volume is irrelevant. Partner quality determines whether the program succeeds or fails.

**Campaign premise:** ELIMFILTERS® is a premium technical brand that rewards distributors who sell on performance. The recruitment campaign communicates this from first contact. The goal is not to attract every interested company — it is to attract the specific type of distributor who will succeed: technically capable, industrially established, commercially motivated by differentiation rather than price competition.

**Prerequisite gate:** Phase 1 website improvements (confirmed complete per DISTRIBUTOR_CONVERSION_PHASE1_REPORT.md — build 89/89 pages, 0 TypeScript errors) must be live before any outreach begins. Sending prospects to the application page before those changes are deployed wastes every contact.

## 1. FIRST THREE COUNTRIES TO TARGET

Priority sequencing is based on three factors: market size for anchor industries, distributor ecosystem maturity, and the geographic achievability of on-site activation visits.

---

### Country 1 — COLOMBIA

**Rationale:** Colombia is the single highest-priority first market.

- **Mining anchor:** Colombia is a major coal and gold producing nation. The Antioquia, Boyacá, and Cesar departments host dozens of active mining operations requiring heavy equipment filtration (excavators, haul trucks, drilling rigs). Filter demand is high-frequency, high-volume, and performance-sensitive.
- **Oil & Gas secondary anchor:** The Llanos and Caribbean coast regions have active petroleum operations managed by Ecopetrol and international operators. O&G maintenance programs are technically sophisticated and filtration-conscious.
- **Distributor ecosystem maturity:** Colombia has an established network of industrial parts distributors, particularly in Bogotá, Medellín, and Bucaramanga. These companies typically carry 3–8 brands and actively seek differentiated additions to their portfolio.
- **Commercial culture fit:** WhatsApp-first business communication, strong preference for direct relationships, emphasis on technical competence in supplier selection. This matches the ELIMFILTERS® support model.
- **No current ELIMFILTERS® presence to protect.** First entry.

**Target cities:** Bogotá (hub), Medellín (industrial/manufacturing), Bucaramanga (mining gateway to Santander), Barranquilla (port, marine, logistics).

**Tier:** Tier 1 — $6,000 USD opening order.

---

### Country 2 — CHILE

**Rationale:** Chile is the world's largest copper-producing country. The northern mining region (Antofagasta, Atacama) operates the largest concentration of heavy mining equipment anywhere in Latin America. Caterpillar 793 haul trucks, Komatsu PC8000 shovels, and Liebherr excavators run in hostile dust environments that place extraordinary demand on air, oil, and hydraulic filtration.

- **Mining anchor:** Codelco (world's largest copper producer), BHP Escondida, Anglo American Quellaveco, Antofagasta Minerals. These operations maintain multi-brand parts programs. The filtration purchasing decision is made by maintenance engineers who respond to technical arguments, not price.
- **Distributor quality:** Chilean industrial distributors serving the mining sector are among the most technically capable in LATAM. They understand Beta ratios, ISO cleanliness codes, and contamination failure modes because their mining customers require it.
- **Commercial discipline:** Chile has a more formalized business culture than other LATAM markets. Distributor agreements are taken seriously. Exclusivity is valued. This reduces the risk of an opportunistic distributor who applies without commercial intent.
- **Market size:** The concentration of large accounts in a small geography (Antofagasta region) means one distributor with mining relationships can achieve meaningful volume quickly.

**Target cities:** Santiago (commercial HQ for most mining distributors), Antofagasta (direct mining region access), Calama (Codelco proximity).

**Tier:** Tier 1 — $6,000 USD opening order.

---

### Country 3 — MEXICO

**Rationale:** Mexico is the largest industrial market in LATAM by absolute volume. It is the third country in sequence, not the first, because its size creates coordination complexity — wrong distributor selection in Mexico is a significant opportunity cost.

- **Agriculture anchor:** Mexico is one of the world's top 10 agricultural producers. The Bajío (Jalisco, Guanajuato, Michoacán), Sinaloa, and Sonora regions operate large mechanized agricultural fleets (tractors, combines, irrigation pumps). Filter replacement frequency in agriculture is high and technically accessible — agricultural equipment buyers are familiar with the filter replacement conversation.
- **Transportation secondary anchor:** Mexico operates one of Latin America's largest commercial truck fleets (Kenworth, Freightliner, International). Truck fleet operators in Monterrey, Guadalajara, and Mexico City are active filter buyers. The cross-reference tool (20,000+ OEM references) is the primary sales entry point for this segment.
- **Distributor complexity:** Mexico has hundreds of industrial parts distributors. Finding the right one requires careful targeting. The ideal Mexican distributor already carries a premium brand (Parker, Donaldson, Bosch) and is looking for a filtration line that helps them differentiate in mining, agriculture, or fleet accounts.
- **Market segmentation:** Mexico is too large for a single distributor at Version 1. The first Mexican distributor should be selected for their industry anchor, not their geographic breadth.

**Target cities (agriculture anchor):** Guadalajara, León, Culiacán, Hermosillo.
**Target cities (transport anchor):** Monterrey, Mexico City, Querétaro.

**Tier:** Tier 1 — $6,000 USD opening order.

## 2. FIRST THREE INDUSTRIES TO TARGET

Industry targeting determines which types of distributors to approach and which conversations to have first. The goal is to select industries where: (a) the technical argument is strongest, (b) filter replacement frequency is high, (c) end customers are technically sophisticated, and (d) the ELIMFILTERS® Knowledge Ecosystem provides the deepest sales enablement content.

---

### Industry 1 — MINING

**Why first:**

Mining is the highest-volume, highest-value, and most technically demanding filter application in LATAM. A haul truck in the Atacama runs its air filter in a dust concentration that would destroy a consumer-grade filter in days. The buyers — maintenance engineers and reliability managers at copper, coal, and gold operations — evaluate filtration by ISO cleanliness codes and Beta ratio data, not by price.

This is exactly the customer ELIMFILTERS® is designed to serve.

**Filter categories in demand:** Air intake (primary and safety elements), oil filtration (lube, hydraulic, transmission), fuel filtration (high-pressure injector protection), cabin air (operator safety, PM10 protection).

**Key cross-references:** Caterpillar, Komatsu, Liebherr, Sandvik, Atlas Copco mining equipment.

**Sales argument:** Contamination failure in mining costs $50,000–$500,000 per unplanned downtime event. A $45 filter that performs to spec is not a cost — it is risk insurance. The ELIMFILTERS® Knowledge System provides the contamination → failure mode → TCO argument in written form, available to any distributor's sales team immediately.

**Knowledge System assets available today:**
- Hydraulic System Contamination case study
- Particle Wear in Engines case study
- ISO 16889, ISO 4406 standard pages
- NANOFORCE, MACROCORE technology pages
- Mining industry content

---

### Industry 2 — AGRICULTURE

**Why second:**

Agriculture is the highest-volume filter category by unit count. Tractors, combines, sprayers, and irrigation pumps require air, oil, and fuel filter replacements every 250–500 hours. A cooperative with 200 tractors replaces filters 400–800 times per year. This is recurring, predictable revenue — the type that builds a distributor's confidence in a brand.

The buyer is less technically sophisticated than mining (the purchase decision is often made by an agronomist or a cooperative purchasing manager), which means the cross-reference tool is the dominant sales entry point. "What filter does a John Deere 8R tractor take?" is the primary question. The Part Search tool answers it instantly.

**Filter categories in demand:** Air filters (primary and secondary), oil filters, fuel filters. Hydraulic on large equipment.

**Key cross-references:** John Deere, CNH (Case/New Holland), AGCO (Fendt, Massey Ferguson), Kubota.

**Sales argument:** Every contaminated filter that passes through an irrigation pump or a tractor engine represents a maintenance event that costs more than the filter. The farmer's decision is simple: the filter that prevents the breakdown versus the one that doesn't.

**Geography:** This industry anchor is strongest in Mexico (Sinaloa, Sonora, Bajío), Colombia (coffee and palm oil regions, Cundinamarca), and Chile (Central Valley — grapes, avocados, blueberries for export).

---

### Industry 3 — TRANSPORTATION & FLEETS

**Why third:**

Commercial trucking is the broadest distribution opportunity in LATAM because every country has a large commercial vehicle fleet and a corresponding aftermarket parts distribution network. The filter buyer is a workshop owner, a fleet maintenance manager, or a parts store buyer — all familiar with the cross-reference process.

Trucking is also the category where the ELIMFILTERS® Part Search tool performs at maximum value: a workshop that discovers the tool and gets 10 accurate cross-references in a row becomes a loyal customer.

**Filter categories in demand:** Air filters (engine air intake, cabin), oil filters, fuel filters.

**Key cross-references:** Kenworth, Freightliner, International, Volvo, Scania, Mercedes-Benz Actros, MAN — all present across LATAM.

**Sales argument for the distributor:** Truck workshops are high-frequency customers. A single truck fleet with 50 vehicles changes filters every 20,000–30,000 km. That is a predictable reorder cycle a distributor can plan inventory around. ELIMFILTERS® cross-references make the sell-in conversation fast.

**Commercial note:** Transportation & Fleets is a volume game. Margins per unit are lower than mining. Include it as the third industry because it broadens the distributor's customer base and creates the volume that builds confidence in the ELIMFILTERS® line. It should not be the primary anchor for the first 90 days — it is the secondary conversion argument after mining or agriculture is established.

## 3. IDEAL DISTRIBUTOR PROFILE

This is the description of the single type of company ELIMFILTERS® is looking for. Every outreach message, every LinkedIn search filter, every industry association contact should be oriented toward finding this company.

---

### The Ideal ELIMFILTERS® Distributor

**Company structure:** An established industrial distributor with an active sales team of 3–15 people. Not a one-person broker or agent. Not a multinational with 500 employees that will give ELIMFILTERS® 0.5% of a sales rep's attention. A company where the right relationship means a dedicated salesperson who carries the brand consistently.

**Revenue range:** USD $500K–$5M annual revenue from industrial parts and related products. Enough operational infrastructure to handle the opening order and the inventory commitment. Small enough that ELIMFILTERS® represents a meaningful percentage of their portfolio — not a rounding error.

**Current product portfolio:** Carries 3–8 established industrial brands. Has experience selling at least one of: hydraulic components, lubricants, pneumatic systems, or filters (any brand). Is not exclusively a consumer auto parts retailer.

**Customer base:** Active accounts in at least one of the three target industries (Mining, Agriculture, Transportation & Fleets). "Active" means they have made a sale to that type of customer in the last 12 months — not aspirationally serving that sector.

**Sales capability:** At least one salesperson who currently visits industrial accounts directly. Cold calls on fleet managers, maintenance supervisors, or purchasing agents at farms, mines, or transport companies. Not purely order-taking from walk-in retail traffic.

**Technical readiness:** Not required to know filtration theory before onboarding. Required to be willing to learn it. The distributors who succeed with ELIMFILTERS® are the ones who go through the Knowledge System training and recognize that the contamination control argument is something their competitors cannot easily replicate.

**Relationship orientation:** Values a long-term supplier relationship over a short-term transaction. Is looking for a brand they can build into a meaningful revenue line over 12–24 months — not a brand to test for 30 days and drop.

**Disqualifying profile:** A company that leads the conversation with price ("what margin do I get?"), is not currently selling to industrial accounts, has been in business less than 2 years, or is primarily a retail auto parts store.

## 4. MINIMUM QUALIFICATION CRITERIA

These criteria are applied at Step 3 of the prequalification flow (per DISTRIBUTOR_PREQUALIFICATION_FLOW.md). All five must be satisfied. The commercial team evaluates borderline cases holistically — a company that clearly fails one criterion but excels in all others can proceed to a discovery call where the exception is discussed.

---

### Criterion 1 — Industrial Customer Base (Non-Negotiable)

**What is required:** The applicant must demonstrate an existing customer base in at least one of ELIMFILTERS®' 12 industrial sectors. Verified through company website, LinkedIn company profile, or stated customer references.

**What passes:** A distributor in Antofagasta whose website shows mining equipment photos, case studies from copper operations, or product pages showing Cat/Komatsu application fitments.

**What fails:** A retail auto parts store with no industrial customers. A consumer products company that has decided to "add industrial" to their portfolio. A startup with no established accounts.

---

### Criterion 2 — Technical Sales Capability

**What is required:** At least one salesperson on the applicant's team who currently sells technical industrial products to industrial accounts. Verified through LinkedIn (sales rep profiles with industrial backgrounds), the discovery call, or stated team credentials in the application.

**What passes:** A sales team member whose LinkedIn shows experience selling hydraulic components, lubricants, industrial tools, or filtration (any brand) to B2B industrial customers.

**What fails:** A team that exclusively manages walk-in retail traffic, phone order-taking, or consumer product sales.

---

### Criterion 3 — Minimum 2 Years in Operation

**What is required:** The company was registered and commercially active at least 2 years before the application date. Verified through website copyright dates, LinkedIn company founding year, or domain registration age.

**Rationale:** A new company that has been operating for 8 months has not demonstrated it can survive the first-year risk period. ELIMFILTERS® is not in a position to support a new company's survival — only to add a profitable line to an established one.

---

### Criterion 4 — Geographic Coverage of Declared Territory

**What is required:** The applicant must demonstrate commercial activity in the country they are claiming. A company claiming national coverage in Colombia must have minimum two active cities or a logistics infrastructure (warehouse, delivery fleet, distributor network) that plausibly covers their declared market.

**What passes:** A distributor in Bogotá with a branch in Medellín, or a distributor in Antofagasta with a sales rep who covers Calama and Copiapó.

**What fails:** A company in Bogotá claiming "all of Colombia" with no visible infrastructure, no branch presence, and no distributor network.

---

### Criterion 5 — Financial Capacity for Opening Order

**What is required:** The applicant can fund a $6,000 USD (Tier 1) or $3,500 USD (Tier 2) opening order at 50% prepayment ($3,000 or $1,750 USD) without requiring credit terms from ELIMFILTERS®.

**Assessment method:** Not a formal credit check. The 50% prepayment requirement is the filter. A company that cannot fund $3,000 in upfront inventory is not financially positioned to build a meaningful distributor relationship.

## 6. LINKEDIN STRATEGY

LinkedIn is the primary prospecting tool for the first eight weeks. The goal is not to close distributor partnerships on LinkedIn — it is to start conversations that lead to discovery calls.

---

### 6.1 Account Setup

**Before outreach begins:**

The ELIMFILTERS® LinkedIn company page must be active and present a credible industrial brand profile. The person conducting outreach must have a complete personal LinkedIn profile — name, photo, title ("Commercial Director" or "Regional Sales Manager — LATAM"), company affiliation (ELIMFILTERS® / Kleo Technologies), and a headline that communicates industrial filtration.

A blank LinkedIn profile sending messages about a distributor program will be ignored. A complete profile from someone who appears to be a legitimate commercial contact will be answered.

---

### 6.2 Search Strategy — Finding the Right Prospects

**LinkedIn Sales Navigator is strongly recommended** for this campaign. The free LinkedIn search has limited filters and limits on connection requests. Sales Navigator enables precision targeting.

**Primary search criteria:**

```
Geography: Colombia, Chile, Mexico (search each separately)
Industry: Industrial Machinery, Oil & Energy, Mining & Metals, 
          Farming, Transportation/Trucking/Railroad
Company size: 11–200 employees
Keywords: "distribuidor" OR "distributor" +

           OR "repuestos" OR "partes industriales"]
Job title of contact: "Gerente General" / "Director Comercial" / 
                       "Gerente de Ventas" / "Owner" / "Founder"
```

**Secondary search approach — by company type, not by keyword:**

Search for companies that currently distribute known technical brands: Parker, Donaldson, Fleetguard, Mann+Hummel, Baldwin, Bosch Rexroth, SKF, Gates, Rexnord. A distributor already carrying one of these brands has proven technical sales capability and is looking for additional lines. They are the highest-probability prospect.

---

### 6.3 Connection and Message Sequence

**This is a three-step sequence. Never lead with an offer.**

**Step 1 — Connection request (Day 0)**

No message with the connection request. Just connect. Connecting without a sales pitch is accepted at higher rates and feels less transactional.

*Exception: If the prospect is a 2nd-degree connection via a relevant mutual contact, name the mutual contact in the connection request message.*

---

**Step 2 — First message (Day 2–3 after connection accepted)**

Subject: None (LinkedIn messages have no subject line)

```
[Name],

Noticed your work at [Company Name] — distributing [brand/category] 
to [industry] accounts in [region] is exactly the type of operation 
we're looking to partner with for ELIMFILTERS® in [country].

We're seeking one authorized distributor in [country] — exclusive 
territory, technical support through WhatsApp, and a Part Search 
tool with 20,000+ OEM cross-references your team can use daily.

If you're open to a 20-minute conversation to see if there's a fit, 
I'd like to arrange that.

[Name]
ELIMFILTERS® Commercial Team
```

**Rules for this message:**
- Personalize for each prospect — company name, industry, and region must be specific
- Do not attach a brochure or PDF in the first message — it signals mass outreach
- Keep it under 150 words
- Send in Spanish when the prospect is from a Spanish-speaking country (Colombia, Chile, Mexico)
- Do not call this an "opportunity" — it is a conversation about a potential partnership

---

**Step 3 — Follow-up (Day 7–8, if no reply to Step 2)**

```
[Name],

Quick follow-up in case my earlier message got buried.

We're still looking for our authorized partner in [country/region] 
for industrial filtration. Given your work with [industry] accounts, 
I think the conversation would be worthwhile.

Happy to share our program overview if the timing is better now.

[Name]
```

**Rules:**
- One follow-up only. Two unanswered messages is the limit. A third message crosses into harassment territory and damages the brand.
- After two unanswered messages, archive the prospect and move to the next.

---

### 6.4 LinkedIn Content Strategy (Parallel to Outreach)

Outreach converts better when the recipient has seen recent ELIMFILTERS® content before receiving the message. Post 2–3 times per week on the ELIMFILTERS® company page during the campaign.

**Content themes that attract qualified distributors:**

1. **Contamination education posts** — "Why ISO 4406 matters for your mining customer's hydraulic system." Technical, credible, positions ELIMFILTERS® as a knowledge source not just a product supplier.

2. **Part Search demonstration** — "We just added OEM cross-references for [equipment model]." Practical. Shows the tool is actively maintained.

3. **Industry-specific contamination scenarios** — "What happens to an excavator's hydraulic pump when ISO cleanliness code exceeds 19/17/14." Targeted to mining maintenance engineers — who are the prospects' customers.

4. **Knowledge System content** — "Our full hydraulic contamination guide: [link]." Drives traffic to the Knowledge System, which builds credibility before the discovery call.

**What not to post:**
- Pricing announcements
- Generic "we're the best filtration brand" claims
- Testimonials that cannot be verified
- Product photos without technical context

### 7.2 How to Use Associations

**Approach 1 — Join as a member or affiliate supplier.** Most industry associations offer supplier membership categories. As a member, ELIMFILTERS® gains: directory listing, event access, and implicit endorsement. Cost varies ($200–$2,000 USD/year per association). Start with SONAMI (Chile) and ACM (Colombia) as first memberships.

**Approach 2 — Attend member events as a non-member guest.** Many associations allow non-members to attend specific events (annual conferences, technical seminars) as guests or sponsors. An ELIMFILTERS® representative attending a SONAMI mining conference in Antofagasta and having five direct conversations with local distributors is worth more than 100 LinkedIn messages.

**Approach 3 — Technical content contribution.** Some associations publish newsletters, technical bulletins, or have LinkedIn groups. Contributing a technical article ("Contamination Control Strategies for High-Altitude Mining Equipment") positions ELIMFILTERS® as a knowledge contributor, not a sales intrusion. This is the highest-credibility approach but requires the most content investment.

**Approach 4 — Association staff contact.** Association managers often know the member distributors who are actively looking to expand their portfolio. A direct call or message to the association's commercial or supplier relations contact ("We are an industrial filtration brand entering the [country] market — can you point us to members who distribute industrial parts to mining operations?") is often the fastest path to a warm referral.

## 8. TRADE SHOW STRATEGY

Trade shows are a high-density opportunity to meet qualified prospects face-to-face. However, they require advance planning, significant investment, and a minimum product demonstration capability. For the first 90-day campaign, trade shows are reconnaissance and relationship-building opportunities — not primary recruitment events.

---

### 8.1 Priority Trade Shows

**Expomina — Lima, Peru (Annual, typically August–September)**

The largest mining industry event in Latin America. Attended by equipment manufacturers, parts distributors, and mining operators from across the region. A Peru visit coincides with a potential Tier 1 market expansion conversation. Exhibitor investment: $3,000–$8,000 USD depending on booth size. **Attend as visitor in Year 1; exhibit in Year 2 if program is active in the region.**

**Exponor — Antofagasta, Chile (Biennial, odd years)**

The premier mining industry exhibition in Chile. Held in the Antofagasta desert — the heart of the copper mining region. Attended by Codelco, BHP, and Anglo American procurement teams and their preferred distributors. **If the Chile distributor is not yet signed, use Exponor as a targeted meeting event: schedule 10 direct meetings with pre-identified distributor prospects in Antofagasta before arrival.**

**AgroExpo Colombia — Bogotá (Biennial)**

Colombia's largest agricultural exhibition. Attended by agricultural equipment dealers, cooperatives, and distributors. Strong overlap with the agricultural anchor industry strategy for Colombia. Lower technical buyer concentration than mining shows — use for network building rather than technical sales conversations.

**FIMAQH — Buenos Aires, Argentina (Annual)**

Latin America's largest industrial machinery and parts show. Useful for network-building and assessment of the Argentina and Southern Cone distributor ecosystem for Phase 2 planning. Not a first-90-day priority.

---

### 8.2 Trade Show Execution Protocol

**90 days before any trade show attendance:**

1. Identify 15–20 exhibiting or attending companies that match the ideal distributor profile
2. Contact them on LinkedIn 3–4 weeks before the show: "We'll both be at [show] — I'd like to arrange 20 minutes to discuss the ELIMFILTERS® distributor program"
3. Confirm 6–10 scheduled meetings before departing for the show
4. Do not rely on booth traffic or floor introductions as the primary meeting source

**At the show:**

- Carry a one-page program summary (physical, co-brandable PDF print)
- Have the Part Search tool demonstrable on a tablet — "Let me show you what it does with your customer's part numbers" converts faster than any verbal explanation
- Prioritize meeting quality over quantity — 6 real conversations beat 30 business card exchanges

**After the show:**

- Follow up within 48 hours with every substantive conversation
- Do not send a mass email — personalize each follow-up to what was specifically discussed
- Move warm prospects directly into the prequalification flow

### 9.4 Decision Categories

**CLEAR PASS — Proceed to discovery call scheduling.**

Company website shows: active industrial customer base in a target industry, a sales team of 3+ people, physical presence in the declared territory, established company history (3+ years indicated), and at least one technical product category in their current portfolio.

**INCONCLUSIVE — Send Request for Additional Information (RFI).**

One or more qualifying signals are absent from the company website (no customer list, no product catalog, company is too new to evaluate clearly). Send a structured RFI email requesting: customer reference in their anchor industry, LinkedIn profiles of their 2–3 salespeople, a brief description of their territory coverage.

RFI template (email):

Response by [date — 5 business days]: [email address]

ELIMFILTERS® Commercial Team

Company is clearly outside the qualification criteria (retail consumer auto parts, no industrial customers, startup, outside LATAM without strategic rationale).

ELIMFILTERS® Commercial Team

No elaboration. No specific reasons given. Clear and respectful.

## 12. RISKS

---

### Risk 1 — CRITICAL: Wrong Distributor Selected to Hit the Number

**Description:** Campaign pressure produces a signed agreement with a company that does not meet the qualification criteria — wrong industry focus, no real industrial customers, insufficient sales team.

**Impact:** An unqualified distributor locks a territory for 12 months. The market is not developed. ELIMFILTERS® loses a year of opportunity in that country. The underperforming distributor relationship becomes a legal and commercial distraction.

**Likelihood:** Medium — the temptation to sign someone who "seems interested" and meets 3 of 5 criteria is real, especially in month 3 when the target is visible.

**Controls:**
- All 5 qualification criteria required — no exceptions without documented commercial team consensus
- Discovery call agenda includes probing questions that reveal the quality of industrial customer relationships, not just their existence
- 90-day target is 2–4 agreements, not a hard minimum — missing it is acceptable; signing a wrong distributor is not

---

### Risk 2 — HIGH: Program Terms Not Ready Before Outreach Generates Interest

**Description:** Discovery calls generate qualified interest but ELIMFILTERS® cannot send a formal program terms document because wholesale pricing structure is not finalized.

**Impact:** A motivated prospect goes from "interested" to "waiting" to "signed with a competitor." This is the most common failure mode in distributor recruitment — the internal decision timeline does not match the market's patience.

**Likelihood:** High — the Decision Workbook explicitly flags wholesale pricing and supply chain logistics as unresolved decisions required before distributor recruitment.

**Controls:**
- Resolve wholesale pricing structure before Week 3 outreach begins — this is a hard prerequisite
- If pricing is not resolved, delay outreach by 1 week rather than take discovery calls with no program terms to offer
- Program one-pager can describe the structure without revealing the margin — territory exclusivity, opening order amount, training program, support model, and 90-day milestones can all be communicated before the final pricing decision is made

---

### Risk 3 — HIGH: Formspree Auto-Reply Not Configured Before Inbound Leads Arrive

**Description:** DISTRIBUTOR_CONVERSION_PHASE1_REPORT.md confirms the auto-reply email template is ready but requires manual Formspree dashboard configuration. If this is not completed before outreach begins, inbound applications from LinkedIn-driven traffic arrive with no auto-confirmation.

**Impact:** A qualified distributor who submits after seeing ELIMFILTERS® on LinkedIn, receives no confirmation, and is left in the same "void" that the Phase 1 improvements were designed to fix.

**Controls:**
- Add Formspree auto-reply configuration to Week 1 checklist as a blocking item before outreach begins
- Test with a real email address and verify the confirmation email arrives within 2 minutes
- Verify field interpolation (`{{contactName}}`, `{{country}}`) works correctly on the current Formspree plan tier

---

### Risk 4 — HIGH: LinkedIn Outreach Quality Declines Under Volume Pressure

**Description:** Outreach messages become generic ("I'd like to connect about our distributor program") rather than personalized, because personalizing 30 messages per week feels slow.

**Impact:** Reply rate drops from 20% to 5–8%. The volume of discovery calls generated drops by 60%, and the entire funnel underperforms.

**Controls:**
- Each message must include at minimum: the recipient's company name, one specific observation about their business or industry, and the country (matching their location)
- Template messages are starting points — not final drafts
- Review reply rates weekly; if reply rate drops below 10%, audit the last 10 messages for personalization quality

---

### Risk 5 — MEDIUM: Discovery Call Converts Poorly Because Program Terms Are Unfamiliar

**Description:** The ELIMFILTERS® commercial team is not experienced at presenting a distributor program. Discovery calls meander, key value propositions are not communicated clearly, and prospects leave the call without a clear next step.

**Impact:** Qualified prospects do not submit applications because they lack sufficient program understanding.

**Controls:**
- Practice the discovery call agenda (Section 9.5) internally before the first real call
- The Part Search tool demonstration is the most powerful 3-minute segment of the call — always include it
- Every discovery call ends with a defined next step: either a date for receiving program terms, or a clear mutual decision that there is no fit

---

### Risk 6 — MEDIUM: Competitor Preemptive Distributor Signing

**Description:** A competing filtration brand (Donaldson, Fleetguard, Mann+Hummel) identifies the same ideal distributor profile and extends a distributor agreement before ELIMFILTERS® completes its process.

**Impact:** The best-qualified distributor in Antofagasta signs with a competitor while ELIMFILTERS® is still conducting its internal pricing discussion.

**Likelihood:** Moderate for the top-tier distributors — they are actively recruited by multiple brands.

**Controls:**
- Move qualified prospects through the pipeline quickly — no more than 5 business days from discovery call to program terms delivery
- The ELIMFILTERS® competitive advantage is not pricing — it is the combination of exclusive territory + technical support model + Knowledge System + Part Search. Lead with these in the discovery call, not with price.
- Do not try to match a competitor's margin offer — compete on the quality and uniqueness of the program

---

### Risk 7 — LOW: Cultural Execution Mismatch

**Description:** Outreach messages written in English to LATAM prospects, or in formal Spanish that does not match regional communication style (Colombian vs. Chilean vs. Mexican Spanish usage differs).

**Impact:** Messages read as obviously templated or foreign. Reply rate decreases.

**Controls:**
- All outreach messages sent in Spanish (or Portuguese for Brazil contacts)
- Have a native speaker from each target country review the message templates before deployment
- WhatsApp communication style in LATAM is more informal than email — calibrate accordingly
