// SEPARATION PROTOCOL

THE THREE-STAGE PROCESS

STAGE 1 / CENTRIFUGE

SEPARATION

Fuel spins through centrifuge. Large solids and water droplets—heavier than fuel—fall to collection bowl.

STAGE 2 / COALESCENCE

BEAD-UP

Small water droplets coalesce on conical baffle and cartridge filter surface, then fall to collection bowl.

STAGE 3 / FILTRATION

AQUABLOC®II CARTRIDGE

Proprietary waterproof filters remove contaminants down to 2 microns nominal, repelling water effectively.
