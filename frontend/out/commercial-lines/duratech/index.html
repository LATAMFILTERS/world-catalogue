Fleet Maintenance Standardisation Through Platform-Specific Kits

DURATECH™ is a fleet maintenance standardisation system that consolidates OEM-interchangeable filtration components into master kits. Each kit is platform-specific — one DURATECH™ kit covers a complete vehicle service: oil filter, fuel filter, air filter, and cabin filter. Designed for mixed-fleet operations in mining, construction, on-road transport, and agriculture.

Mixed-fleet operations face a systematic problem: each vehicle model requires a different combination of filter specifications. Without a kit system, maintenance teams must track individual elements across dozens of part numbers — creating inventory complexity, procurement overhead, and the persistent risk of wrong-element installation. Wrong elements installed at service are the primary cause of filtration-related equipment failures in fleet operations.

DURATECH™ eliminates this failure mode by converting filter inventory to a kit-based structure. Platform-specific kits contain only the correct elements for each vehicle model. A single DURATECH™ order per service cycle replaces multiple individual part orders — reducing procurement complexity and ensuring every service event uses verified OEM-interchangeable specifications.

FLEET OPERATIONS — ON-ROAD & OFF-ROAD

DURATECH™ standardises filtration across mixed-model fleets in any industry where multiple vehicle types share a maintenance program.

TRUCKS & ON-ROAD FLEETS

On-road commercial transport fleets with mixed makes and models — long-haul trucks, regional distribution, and last-mile delivery vehicles. Fleet operators managing 20+ vehicles across multiple makes face persistent wrong-element installation risk. DURATECH™ kit-per-platform structure eliminates this risk and reduces procurement to a single order per service event per vehicle model.

MINING & CONSTRUCTION

Off-road heavy equipment fleets — excavators, loaders, haul trucks, graders, and drill rigs operating across multiple equipment brands on the same site. Mining fleet maintenance teams typically manage 8–15 equipment models in parallel. DURATECH™ platform-specific kits convert this to a single kit reference per model, managed as standard inventory items rather than individual filter SKUs.

AGRICULTURE

Agricultural machinery fleets — tractors, harvesters, and self-propelled sprayers across seasonal maintenance cycles. Agricultural maintenance has defined service windows (pre-season, mid-season, harvest). DURATECH™ kits allow maintenance teams to pre-kit all required filtration before the service window, eliminating last-minute sourcing and ensuring correct elements are available for every machine in the fleet.
