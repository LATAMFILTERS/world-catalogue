ENGINEERED ADVANTAGES

The HYDROCORE™ Turbine Fuel Separator Series (900FH and 1000FH) combines 3-stage filtration: particle interception, water coalescing, and precision defense. The system achieves 99.8% water removal and is rated ISO 16332 with 30-micron absolute filtration for high-pressure common rail (HPCR) injection systems. HYDROCORE™ is designed for contaminated fuel environments in oil & gas, marine, and power generation applications where water ingress from fuel degradation or brine exposure is inevitable.
