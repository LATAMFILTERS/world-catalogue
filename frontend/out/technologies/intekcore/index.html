// TECHNICAL OVERVIEW

LIGHT-DUTY PRECISION. HEAVY-DUTY PROTECTION STANDARD.

Light-duty engines operate across the widest environmental range of any engine category — urban traffic, highway cruising, stop-and-go duty cycles, varying ambient temperatures and seasonal contamination profiles. INTEKCORE™ is engineered to handle this variability without protection compromise.

Extended surface geometry maximizes filtration area within compact housing dimensions, delivering service intervals that match or exceed OEM replacement recommendations while maintaining airflow resistance well below the threshold that degrades fuel economy and engine response.

The precision sealing system is the critical element that conventional replacement filters often compromise. INTEKCORE™ bypass-prevention architecture ensures zero unfiltered air migration at the housing interface — not at test conditions, but throughout the full service interval.

// PROTECTION ARCHITECTURE

THREE PROTECTION LAYERS FOR LIGHT-DUTY PRECISION.

LAYER 1
01

SURFACE EXPANSION ZONE

INTEKCORE™ extended pleat surface architecture maximizes the active filtration area within the constraints of OEM housing dimensions. Greater surface area reduces face velocity at any given airflow rate — which directly reduces pressure drop and extends the service interval by distributing contamination load across a larger capture surface. Surface expansion is calibrated to maintain rated airflow at the maximum allowable restriction threshold throughout the service interval.

LAYER 2
02

MULTI-LAYER PROTECTION MATRIX

The protection matrix combines two density zones — a coarse outer layer that handles the high-volume macro-particle fraction characteristic of urban and highway driving environments, and a precision inner layer that captures fine particles including brake dust, tire rubber particulate and road surface contamination. The two-zone gradient prevents the single-layer capacity limitation that causes early restriction in high-contamination urban cycles.

LAYER 3
03

BYPASS PREVENTION SEAL

The INTEKCORE™ precision sealing system addresses the failure mode that conventional air filter elements most frequently exhibit — bypass leakage at the housing interface. The multi-point seal geometry maintains positive contact with housing surfaces across the full temperature range from cold start to operating temperature, and across the compression relaxation cycle that occurs as sealing materials age. Zero bypass throughout the service interval, not just at installation.

WHERE INTEKCORE™ PROTECTS

INTEKCORE™ multi-layer defense is the precision air protection choice for light commercial and passenger vehicle fleets.

PASSENGER VEHICLES

Private and corporate fleet passenger cars operating across urban, suburban and highway cycles. INTEKCORE™ extended surface ensures OEM service interval adherence without airflow restriction that degrades fuel economy.

LIGHT COMMERCIAL

Vans, pickups and light delivery vehicles operating urban delivery cycles with frequent stop-and-go duty. The multi-layer matrix handles the elevated brake dust and urban particulate contamination load characteristic of delivery route operation.

AUTOMOTIVE FLEETS

High-utilization fleet operations where air filter service interval adherence directly impacts fuel cost management. INTEKCORE™ extended capacity reduces per-vehicle intervention frequency and fleet maintenance overhead.

Urban transit and intercity coach vehicles operating on scheduled routes where contamination profiles combine urban brake dust with highway road surface particulate. Precision bypass sealing is critical for emissions-managed engine systems.

LIGHT INDUSTRY

Forklifts and light industrial vehicles operating in enclosed or partially enclosed environments where particulate contamination includes industrial dust fractions alongside standard ambient particulate.
