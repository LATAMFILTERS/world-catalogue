// TECHNICAL OVERVIEW

THE HOUSING IS NOT A PASSIVE COMPONENT.

In heavy-duty truck and machinery applications, the filter housing defines the reliability boundary of the entire filtration system. A compromised housing — through corrosion, thread wear, bypass valve failure or seal degradation — neutralizes the protection value of the filter element it contains. INTEKCORE™ housing systems are engineered so the housing never becomes the failure point.

Constructed from high-grade materials rated for continuous high-pressure operation, INTEKCORE™ housings maintain structural integrity across the full thermal cycling range of heavy commercial and industrial engines — from cold starts in extreme climates to sustained high-load thermal conditions in mining and construction operations.

Universal mounting geometry and OEM-compatible thread standards make INTEKCORE™ housings direct-fit replacements across major truck and machinery platforms. No fabrication. No adapter plates. No serviceability compromise.

// PROTECTION ARCHITECTURE

THREE ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES FOR HOUSING RELIABILITY.

PRINCIPLE 1
01

HIGH-PRESSURE STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY

INTEKCORE™ housing bodies are rated for the peak pressure spikes that occur during cold-start conditions in heavy diesel applications — when oil viscosity creates instantaneous pressure surges that can stress inferior housing materials beyond their elastic limit. The housing wall geometry distributes burst load across the full structure rather than concentrating stress at the thread interface or bypass valve seat, eliminating the fracture failure mode that makes low-cost housings a liability in high-value asset operations.

HIGH
Burst pressure rating above standard housing specifications
PRINCIPLE 2
02

CORROSION-RESISTANT CONSTRUCTION

Trucks and machinery operating in agriculture, mining, construction and marine-adjacent environments expose filter housings to chemical contamination, humidity cycling, fertilizer residue, salt and abrasive particulate that accelerates corrosion on conventional steel housings. INTEKCORE™ material selection and surface treatment maintains housing integrity across multi-year service cycles — preventing the external degradation that leads to thread seizure, bypass valve sticking and catastrophic seal failure during element replacement.

100%
Seal integrity maintained across full service cycle
PRINCIPLE 3
03

PRECISION SEAL ARCHITECTURE

The INTEKCORE™ sealing system is engineered for heavy commercial torque cycles — the repeated high-torque installation and removal that characterizes fleet workshop maintenance. Seal groove geometry maintains O-ring compression within specification across the full range of technician-applied torque variation, preventing the under-seal bypass events that occur when housings are installed to different torque standards across a mixed crew maintenance environment.

0
Bypass events within rated torque installation range

// ASSET APPLICATIONS

WHERE INTEKCORE™ HOUSING SYSTEMS OPERATE

INTEKCORE™ housing systems are the structural foundation for filter protection on heavy trucks, off-highway machinery and industrial power units.

HEAVY TRUCKS

Long-haul and regional heavy commercial trucks operating on demanding freight cycles. INTEKCORE™ housings withstand the vibration, pressure cycling and temperature variation of continuous highway operation without developing the micro-leaks that degrade filter performance between service intervals.

CONSTRUCTION MACHINERY

Excavators, bulldozers, graders and crane systems operating in dusty, chemically aggressive environments. Housing corrosion resistance is critical when equipment is washed down with chemical cleaners or operates adjacent to concrete, lime or fertilizer contamination sources.

MINING EQUIPMENT

Off-highway extraction equipment running 24/7 in remote sites where housing failure forces extended downtime. INTEKCORE™ structural ratings ensure housing integrity across the extreme pressure and thermal cycles of continuous mining operation.

AGRICULTURE

Tractors, combines and harvesters exposed to fertilizer, pesticide and agricultural chemical contamination that corrodes standard housings. INTEKCORE™ corrosion-resistant construction extends housing service life across multi-season agricultural operation.

BUS & COACH FLEETS

Urban transit and long-distance coach fleets requiring high service frequency without housing degradation. INTEKCORE™ thread integrity ensures reliable element replacement across high-cycle workshop maintenance operations.

INDUSTRIAL POWER UNITS

Stationary and mobile industrial power generation and hydraulic systems where housing reliability directly determines operational continuity. INTEKCORE™ precision seal architecture prevents the bypass leakage that contaminates hydraulic circuits and engine oil systems.
