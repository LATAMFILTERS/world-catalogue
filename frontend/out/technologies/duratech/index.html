// OVERVIEW TÉCNICO

ONE SOURCE. EVERY SYSTEM. EVERY VEHICLE.

Fleet maintenance operations managing mixed-model vehicle populations face a consistent challenge: sourcing compatible filter elements from multiple suppliers for each vehicle variant, managing separate inventory lines, and coordinating service timing across different replacement intervals.

DURATECH™ master kit architecture consolidates all service-interval filter elements for a defined vehicle platform into a single sourcing event. Oil, fuel, air and cabin filters — all OEM-interchangeable, all coordinated to a unified service interval — in one kit.

Inventory standardization across a mixed-model fleet is the long-term operational benefit. DURATECH™ kit design philosophy reduces per-vehicle part number count, eliminates cross-contamination errors between similar-looking elements, and simplifies technician training for multi-make service operations.

// ARQUITECTURA DE PROTECCIÓN

THREE CONSOLIDATION PRINCIPLES FOR FLEET EFFICIENCY.

PRINCIPLE 1
01

MULTI-SYSTEM CONSOLIDATION

Each DURATECH™ master kit consolidates all filtration elements required for a complete vehicle service event into a single package. The kit composition is vehicle-specific — matched to the exact oil, fuel, air and cabin specifications of the target vehicle platform — eliminating the possibility of incorrect element selection that occurs when sourcing components individually from a general catalogue. One kit, one vehicle, one complete service.

PRINCIPLE 2
02

OEM INTERCHANGEABILITY

Every element in the DURATECH™ master kit is designed to direct OEM specification — same dimensions, same bypass valve settings, same sealing surfaces, same thread specifications. OEM interchangeability means no modification to vehicle systems, no adapter plates, no calibration adjustments. Technicians can service any platform in the fleet with DURATECH™ kits with the same confidence as OEM-supplied elements, at fleet purchasing economics.

100%
OEM dimensional and specification interchangeability
PRINCIPLE 3
03

STANDARDIZED INVENTORY

The DURATECH™ kit system converts fleet filter inventory from an unmanaged collection of individual part numbers to a structured kit-based inventory with predictable consumption rates. For fleet managers, this means reorder quantities based on vehicle count and service cycle frequency — not variable individual part consumption rates. Inventory holding costs fall, stockout risk falls, and administrative overhead for purchasing and parts management falls with them.
