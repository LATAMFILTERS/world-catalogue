// TECHNICAL OVERVIEW

HYDROPHOBIC DEFENSE. WATER CANNOT PASS.

Water contamination in fuel systems operates across three phases: free water that settles and can be drained, emulsified water suspended in micro-droplets that conventional filters cannot intercept, and dissolved water that forms free phase when temperature changes. Each phase causes a different failure mode — microbial growth, injector corrosion, and cavitation erosion respectively.

HYDROCORE™ hydrophobic technology addresses all three phases through a sequenced interception architecture where each protection layer is engineered for a specific water contamination state. The result is 99.8% water separation efficiency — validated across the full operating temperature and fuel flow range.

The HYDROCORE™ collection chamber operates as a sealed reservoir that permanently removes intercepted water from the fuel circuit. Integrated visual monitoring port allows service technicians to assess water accumulation without element removal.

// ASSET APPLICATIONS

WHERE HYDROCORE™ PROTECTS

HYDROCORE™ water separation technology protects any fuel system where water ingress threatens injection system integrity.

HEAVY TRANSPORT

Diesel fuel systems where bulk fuel from storage tanks arrives with water contamination from condensation cycles. HYDROCORE™ removes this contamination before it reaches sensitive Common Rail injectors.

POWER GENERATION

Stationary gensets with extended fuel storage periods where condensation accumulation in fuel tanks creates a chronic water contamination source. HYDROCORE™ continuous protection prevents the microbial growth that water accumulation promotes in stored fuel.

AGRICULTURE

Farm equipment fuel systems where outdoor fuel storage in diurnal temperature cycling environments creates regular water ingress through tank breathers. The collection chamber visual port allows field service personnel to assess water accumulation without equipment shutdown.

MARINE

Diesel marine engines where salt spray and humidity create elevated water contamination rates in fuel systems. HYDROCORE™ hydrophobic barrier prevents salt-water contamination of injection systems in marine service.

MINING

Remote site equipment fueled from bulk storage tanks subject to high humidity and temperature variation. HYDROCORE™ terminal protection ensures injection system integrity when fuel source quality cannot be guaranteed.

CONSTRUCTION

Construction site equipment fueled from mobile refueling units where water contamination risk is elevated by outdoor fuel handling and storage conditions.
