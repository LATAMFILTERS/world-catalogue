NOT JUST A FILTER. AN OIL CIRCUIT GUARDIAN.

Conventional oil filters rely on a single-density barrier to intercept contaminants. SYNTRAXTM deploys a four-layer protection matrix where each layer is calibrated to a specific contamination size class - from coarse metallic debris at the outer zone to sub-micron carbonaceous particles at the core barrier.

The AI-engineered gradient was developed by analyzing contamination profiles across 48 engine types under heavy-duty field conditions. The result is a protection matrix that matches real-world contamination distribution, not laboratory averages.

Turbocharger bearing failure is not a low-probability event - it is the predictable outcome of sustained oil contamination above the bearing film tolerance threshold. At operating speed, turbo shaft bearings run on oil films measured in 2-4 microns. Any abrasive particle above that threshold transiting the bearing circuit removes material on contact. The damage is irreversible and cumulative.

WHERE SYNTRAXTM PROTECTS

SYNTRAXTM protection matrix is validated for turbocharged and naturally aspirated engines across all duty cycles and oil grades.

HEAVY TRANSPORT

A turbocharger replacement on a long-haul diesel unit costs between $2,000 and $8,000 in parts alone, plus workshop hours and vehicle downtime. The failure is almost always traceable to oil contamination - abrasive particles that entered the bearing circuit during an earlier service interval and accumulated there. The contamination event precedes the failure event by thousands of kilometers.

POWER GENERATION

Gensets on base-load or standby duty run extended service intervals with no intermediate oil analysis. Oil contamination builds progressively as metallic wear particles and combustion by-products accumulate through the interval. By interval end, an unprotected engine has been operating on increasingly contaminated oil for months without any visible indication.

AGRICULTURE

Agricultural engines that sit dormant through winter accumulate moisture and oxidation products in the crankcase. The first startup after storage mobilizes these deposits into oil circulation in a single high-contamination surge. Without multi-layer protection at the outer entry zone, this startup spike reaches bearing surfaces directly.

MINING

Engine oil contamination rates in mining environments are three to five times higher than on-road equivalents due to ambient dust ingestion through crankcase ventilation and metal contamination from high-load component wear. Standard single-density oil filters reach restriction capacity prematurely in this duty cycle.

CONSTRUCTION

Excavators and graders run continuous multi-shift operations with no idle periods for oil circuit recovery. Contamination accumulated during each shift is still circulating when the next shift begins. Capacity loss in a single-density filter occurs faster in this duty cycle than in any laboratory test condition.

INDUSTRIAL

Industrial compressors and prime movers operate on tight bearing clearance tolerances required for efficiency and rotational precision. Any contamination that migrates to bearing surfaces creates measurable clearance growth - detectable only when vibration analysis confirms bearing degradation already well advanced.
