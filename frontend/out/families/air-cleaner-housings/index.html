Air Cleaner
Housings

Air cleaner housings provide the structural enclosure for primary and secondary air elements. INTEKCORE™ housings use precision radial seal geometry that eliminates bypass at the element-to-housing interface.

HD PREFIX EA1Air Intake Protection

Family engineering role.

INTEKCORE™ housing architecture uses corrosion-resistant construction and precision sealing surfaces. Radial seal geometry supports consistent sealing force around the element circumference and helps prevent unfiltered air ingress in field-assembled intake systems.

Technical validation.

This family connects the protected air intake system, the primary housing technology, product identification, and ISO 5011 intake-air validation into a single engineering path.
