Fuel Water Separators

Fuel water separators remove free, emulsified, and dissolved water from diesel fuel in high-volume heavy-duty fuel systems. The HYDROCORE™ series provides advanced water separation for fuel systems in power generation, large-scale mining, and marine applications.

HYDROCORE™ water separation architecture: Stage 1 — primary separation removes bulk free water and coarse particles; Stage 2 — graduated coalescence merges emulsified droplets for gravity separation; Stage 3 — hydrophobic barrier intercepts remaining dissolved water.
