Secondary / Safety
Air Elements

Secondary / safety air elements are installed downstream of the primary element as the final safety barrier. They protect the engine if the primary element is damaged, improperly seated, overloaded, or removed during service.

HD PREFIX EA1LD PREFIX EA3Air Intake Protection

Family engineering role.

Secondary / safety elements use high-efficiency fine-fibre construction designed to capture residual contamination that passes through a compromised primary element. Their role is protective redundancy, not routine dust loading.

Technical validation.

This family connects the protected intake system, secondary safety filtration, product identification, and ISO 5011 validation into a single engineering path.
