Primary Air

HD: EA1XXXXLD: EA3XXXX

// PURPOSE

Primary air filtration elements protect the combustion chamber from particulate ingestion. They are the main contamination barrier in the intake system, installed upstream of the engine air intake. Primary elements carry the full contaminant loading and are the primary service-interval component in the air intake system.

// ENGINEERING

Primary air elements use MACROCORE™ progressive density gradient construction. Outer pleat zones capture coarse particles (>25 µm); inner zones intercept fine and sub-micron contamination. The graduated density distributes contaminant loading across the full media depth, extending service intervals beyond single-density cellulose elements.
