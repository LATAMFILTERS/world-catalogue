Which technologies define ELIMFILTERS fuel cleanliness?

SYNTEPORE™, HYDROCORE™, and TURBOCORE™.

What is the difference between HYDROCORE™ and TURBOCORE™?

HYDROCORE™ is the fuel-water separation architecture for separator elements and assemblies, while TURBOCORE™ is dedicated to Turbine Series FH and FG systems.

Fuel Cleanliness Protection

Secondary Fuel: Secondary fuel filters provide final-stage particulate control immediately upstream of the high-pressure pump and injectors.

Secondary Fuel engineering principle: SYNTEPORE™ fine filtration controls the critical particle population that threatens precision injection clearances.

Secondary Fuel control strategy: Select secondary fuel by protected system, duty class, applicable standard, flow or airflow requirement, and service condition.

Secondary Fuel operational impact: Secondary Fuel supports the contamination-control objective of the fuel cleanliness domain.

Secondary Fuel domain summary: Fuel-cleanliness protection combines staged particulate removal, free and emulsified water separation, coalescence, collection, drainage, and bulk-fuel conditioning.

SYNTEPORE™ controls particulate contamination in primary and secondary diesel-fuel stages.

HYDROCORE™ separates free and emulsified water through staged coalescence, gravity collection, and hydrophobic control.

TURBOCORE™ is dedicated to Turbine Series FH and FG fuel-conditioning systems.

Secondary Fuel is associated with the following engineering standards: ASTM D6304, ISO 12937.

Secondary Fuel addresses or is exposed to the following failure modes: Particle Wear, Diesel Water Contamination.
