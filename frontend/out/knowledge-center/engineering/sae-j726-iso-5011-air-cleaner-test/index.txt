The two metrics are not interchangeable. However, for comparisons where Beta-ratio-like data is needed for air filters, ISO 5011 can be supplemented with APC particle counting downstream of the element during the gravimetric test — generating particle count data at specific size thresholds (e.g., ≥5 µm, ≥10 µm) as the element loads. This combined methodology gives both overall gravimetric efficiency (from mass balance) and particle count efficiency (from APC) from the same test run. Some OEMs specify minimum particle count efficiency at ≥5 µm ≥99.9% in addition to overall gravimetric efficiency ≥99.9% for air filter elements on turbocharged engines where fine particles passing through the filter have proportionally higher impact on turbocharger bearing wear.4:T41d,SAE J726 assembled-system testing captures ingestion events not visible in ISO 5011 element-only testing: (1) Housing seal bypass — a leaking element-to-housing seal passes unfiltered dust directly to the engine; element-only testing in a perfect test housing shows zero bypass, while assembled housing testing with the production seal arrangement may show 0.1–1% bypass at the seal — revealing real-world protection levels; (2) Pre-cleaner efficiency integration — SAE J726 assembled testing measures the fraction of total incoming dust that the pre-cleaner captures before the primary element, and the fraction that passes through the pre-cleaner and loads the element; (3) Housing re-entrainment — in poorly designed housings, high-velocity areas near the outlet can re-entrain partially captured particles from upstream housing walls — visible only in assembled system testing.
