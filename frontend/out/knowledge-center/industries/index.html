04 / INDUSTRY APPLICATIONS

Industrial Application Profiles

Industry-specific contamination profiles, equipment exposure levels, and filtration requirements for heavy equipment and industrial operations. Contamination exposure directly determines filtration system design, service intervals, and asset protection strategy.

⛏️EXTREME EXPOSURE

Mining

Silica dust, rock fines, explosive gases, and water contamination in extreme environments.

🏗️HIGH EXPOSURE

Construction

Concrete dust, soil, and hydraulic circuit contamination on mobile earthmoving equipment.

🌾HIGH EXPOSURE

Agriculture

Soil, grain dust, and organic matter causing air intake and hydraulic system wear.

🚛MODERATE EXPOSURE

Truck Fleets

Urban diesel soot, highway dust, and extended drain interval requirements for long-haul applications.

⚓MODERATE EXPOSURE

Marine

Salt water ingress, marine diesel contamination, and IMO compliance for offshore operations.

🛢️HIGH EXPOSURE

Oil & Gas

Sand, H₂S, drilling mud contamination in upstream and midstream oil and gas assets.

🏭MODERATE EXPOSURE

Manufacturing

Metalworking fluid contamination, compressor intake filtration, and hydraulic press protection.

⚡MODERATE EXPOSURE

Power Generation

Turbine air intake filtration, generator lube systems, and continuous operation requirements.

🚂MODERATE EXPOSURE

Railway

Diesel soot, brake dust, and extended service interval requirements for locomotive and rail fleet operations.

🗑️HIGH EXPOSURE

Waste & Municipal

Extreme duty cycles, organic decomposition dust, and hydraulic system demands in refuse collection and compaction equipment.

CONTAMINATION EXPOSURE CLASSIFICATION

Exposure levels reflect typical operating conditions for ambient particulate, process contamination, and fluid ingress severity. Extreme exposure (mining, silica-heavy operations) requires daily air restriction checks, cabin HEPA filtration, and reduced hydraulic service intervals. High exposure (agriculture, construction) requires site-specific service intervals rather than OEM calendar-based schedules.
