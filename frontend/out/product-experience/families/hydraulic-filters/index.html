Filtration Role in Hydraulic Protection

Hydraulic filters maintain ISO 4406 cleanliness targets in hydraulic circuits to protect proportional valves, servo valves, and high-pressure pumps with clearance tolerances of 1–25 µm.

Media Architecture and Construction Principles

NANOFORCE™ achieves β₁₀(c) ≥200 for standard hydraulic applications and β₄(c) ≥1000 for precision servo valve circuits. Collapse resistance ≥3,000 kPa prevents structural failure under pressure surges. Available in pressure line, return line, and kidney loop configurations.

Primary Equipment Applications

01Construction excavator, wheel loader, and crane hydraulic circuits
02Mining dump truck and drill rig hydraulic systems
03Industrial hydraulic presses and machine tools
04Marine deck machinery hydraulic systems
05Agricultural tractor and harvester hydraulic circuits

Physical Construction and Materials

Multi-layer synthetic nanofiber media with structural collapse protection. Pressure line elements: rated to 420 bar. Return line elements: low ΔP design. Kidney loop elements: high dirt capacity. End caps welded or bonded with polyurethane. Seal materials: NBR standard, FKM for phosphate ester fluids.

Filtration Technologies Applied

NANOFORCE™

Hydraulic System Protection

NANOFORCE™ achieves β₁₀(c) ≥200 for standard hydraulic applications and β₄(c) ≥1000 for precision servo valve protection. The element collapse resistance of ≥3,000 kPa prevents structural failure under pressure surges. Available in pressure line (rated to 420 bar system pressure), return line, and offline kidney loop configurations. Target ISO 4406 cleanliness of 16/14/11 is achievable with proper system design combining NANOFORCE™ elements with breather protection and commissioning flush procedures.
