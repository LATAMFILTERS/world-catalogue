Canonical Answers

What is Fuel Cleanliness Protection?

Fuel Cleanliness Protection defends high-pressure injection systems by controlling particulate contamination and water before fuel reaches precision pumps and injectors.

How does Fuel Cleanliness Protection work?

SYNTEPORE™ controls particulate contamination, HYDROCORE™ provides fuel-water separation, and TURBOCORE™ supports Turbine FH and FG fuel-separation systems.

What does Fuel Cleanliness Protection protect?

Consistent fuel cleanliness protection reduces contamination exposure and supports asset reliability across the service interval.

Which technologies define ELIMFILTERS fuel cleanliness?

SYNTEPORE™, HYDROCORE™, and TURBOCORE™.

What is the difference between HYDROCORE™ and TURBOCORE™?

HYDROCORE™ is the fuel-water separation architecture for separator elements and assemblies, while TURBOCORE™ is dedicated to Turbine Series FH and FG systems.

Which standards are associated with Fuel Cleanliness Protection?

ASTM D6304, ISO 12937, ISO 16332

Which failure modes are connected to Fuel Cleanliness Protection?

Particle Wear, Diesel Water Contamination

Which industries use Fuel Cleanliness Protection?

Mining, Agriculture, Power Generation, Marine, Oil & Gas, Construction, Truck Fleets

Fuel Cleanliness Protection engineering principle: SYNTEPORE™ controls particulate contamination, HYDROCORE™ provides fuel-water separation, and TURBOCORE™ supports Turbine FH and FG fuel-separation systems.

Fuel Cleanliness Protection control strategy: Coordinate the technologies and product families assigned to Fuel Cleanliness Protection around the protected asset, contamination boundary, applicable standard, and operating duty cycle.

Fuel Cleanliness Protection operational impact: Consistent fuel cleanliness protection reduces contamination exposure and supports asset reliability across the service interval.

Fuel Cleanliness Protection domain summary: Fuel-cleanliness protection combines staged particulate removal, free and emulsified water separation, coalescence, collection, drainage, and bulk-fuel conditioning.

SYNTEPORE™ controls particulate contamination in primary and secondary diesel-fuel stages.

HYDROCORE™ separates free and emulsified water through staged coalescence, gravity collection, and hydrophobic control.

TURBOCORE™ is dedicated to Turbine Series FH and FG fuel-conditioning systems.

Fuel Cleanliness Protection is connected to the following ELIMFILTERS technologies: SYNTEPORE™, HYDROCORE™, TURBOCORE™.

Fuel Cleanliness Protection is connected to the following product families: Primary Fuel, Secondary Fuel, Fuel Water Separators.

Fuel Cleanliness Protection is associated with the following engineering standards: ASTM D6304, ISO 12937, ISO 16332.

Fuel Cleanliness Protection addresses or is exposed to the following failure modes: Particle Wear, Diesel Water Contamination.

Fuel Cleanliness Protection is applied across the following industries: Mining, Agriculture, Power Generation, Marine, Oil & Gas, Construction, Truck Fleets.
