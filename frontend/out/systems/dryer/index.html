Air Dryer Systems

The ELIMFILTERS® DRYCORE™ Desiccant Air Dryer System achieves zero dew-point performance through molecular sieve desiccant technology combined with an integrated coalescing pre-stage for bulk water and oil mist removal. DRYCORE™ extends service life 45% beyond standard desiccant air dryer elements and prevents pneumatic system corrosion in manufacturing, power generation, and railway applications where compressed air purity is critical for control valve and brake system operation.
