Marine Filter Systems

The ELIMFILTERS® MARINECLEAN™ Filter System is naval-grade alloy construction engineered for salt-resistant performance in permanent brine and H2S-contaminated atmospheres. MARINECLEAN™ removes 99.9% water and sediment from marine diesel fuel and is IMO-certified for commercial and offshore vessel operation. Where standard filters corrode within 2–3 months offshore, MARINECLEAN™ maintains integrity across continuous salt-mist and subsea hydrogen sulfide exposure.
