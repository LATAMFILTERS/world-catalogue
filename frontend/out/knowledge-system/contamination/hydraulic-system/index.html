CANONICAL ENGINEERING CONTEXT

Hydraulic System Contamination

Hydraulic system contamination is the presence of solid particles, water, wear debris, or other foreign material in a hydraulic circuit operating with precision clearances.

Engineering Principle

Particles circulate through pumps, valves, actuators, and servo controls, where they produce abrasion, surface fatigue, stiction, and accelerated seal wear.

Control Strategy

Set cleanliness targets around the most sensitive component, monitor fluid condition, and select filtration by Beta ratio, flow demand, pressure rating, collapse strength, and duty cycle.

Operational Impact

Progressive contamination increases leakage, reduces control precision, raises heat generation, and increases exposure to pump, valve, and actuator failure.

What is Hydraulic System Contamination?

What does Hydraulic System Contamination protect?

How should hydraulic cleanliness targets be selected?

Targets should be selected around the most contamination-sensitive component, then verified against flow, pressure, temperature, duty cycle, and the applicable ISO cleanliness and filter-performance standards.
