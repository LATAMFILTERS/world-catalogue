// TECHNICAL REFERENCE

COMMON QUESTIONS

What is the best air filter for mining equipment?

MACROCORE™ and NANOFORCE™ achieve 99.9% particulate capture for mining air intake, meeting SAE J1539 and ISO 5011. Selection depends on engine displacement and dust load.

How often should industrial fuel filters be changed?

ELIMFILTERS® recommends 500–1,000 hour intervals for heavy diesel engines, 250–500 hours in high-contamination environments. AQUAGUARD™ extends intervals through 99.8% water separation.

What ISO cleanliness code should a hydraulic system target?

Most industrial hydraulic systems require ISO 17/15/12 to protect proportional valve spools. Critical systems may specify ISO 15/13/10. Target measured cleanliness codes, not product brand.

Why does contamination cause engine failure?

Particles wear bearing surfaces, restrict injectors, and degrade seals. Uncontrolled contamination reduces bearing life from 15,000+ hours to 2,000–3,000 hours.
