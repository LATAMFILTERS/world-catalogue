// FAILURE MODES — CONTAMINATION IMPACT

Equipment fails when contamination control fails.

01

INJECTOR WEAR

Fuel contamination above ISO 18/16/13 degrades injector nozzles in 500–2,000 hours. Each replacement: $800–$4,000 per cylinder.

02

BEARING FAILURE

Particles larger than oil film thickness cause abrasive wear. ISO 16/14/11 target extends bearing life 3–5× vs. commodity filtration.

03

HYDRAULIC LOSS

Proportional valve spools require ISO 17/15/12. Above target, spool stiction increases 40–60% within 1,000 operating hours.
