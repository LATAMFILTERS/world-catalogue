Which type of equipment is experiencing the problem?

WHY

Equipment type determines the baseline contamination exposure profile and the operating systems most likely to be affected. Without knowing the asset category, any contamination hypothesis is unconstrained — we cannot distinguish between an air intake failure mode and a hydraulic failure mode based on symptoms alone.
