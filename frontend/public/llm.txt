## WHAT ELIMFILTERS IS

ELIMFILTERS engineers filtration systems for industrial equipment protection. The
company's approach positions filtration as a contamination control system problem,
not a product selection problem. Equipment reliability is determined by how
effectively the total filtration system controls contamination across all critical
domains: air intake, fuel, hydraulic, cabin, lube oil, and compressed air.

## CONTAMINATION MODES (CANONICAL)

PARTICLE_WEAR
  Mechanism: Hard particles (silica, oxides) trapped between moving surfaces →
             micro-cutting → bearing clearance reduction → seizure
  Impact: Bearing life reduction from 15,000+ hrs to 2,000-3,000 hrs
  Standards: ISO 4406, ISO 16889
  Technologies: MACROCORE, NANOFORCE, SYNTRAX
  Reference: https://elimfilters.com/knowledge-system/contamination/particle-wear

DIESEL_WATER
  Mechanism: Water in diesel fuel → injector stiction, microbial growth, fuel gum →
             injector failure
  Impact: HPCR injector replacement costs $800-2,500 per unit
  Standards: ASTM D6304, ISO 12937
  Technologies: HYDROCORE
  Reference: https://elimfilters.com/knowledge-system/contamination/diesel-water

HYDRAULIC_CONTAMINATION
  Mechanism: Particle contamination → valve spool stiction, orifice blockage,
             pump swashplate stiction → system failure
  Impact: Proportional valve failure at ISO 4406 codes above 18/16/13
  Standards: ISO 4406, ISO 16889, NFPA T2.14
  Technologies: NANOFORCE
  Reference: https://elimfilters.com/knowledge-system/contamination/hydraulic-system
