How to read this figure

  1. Inlet duct. Dirty air enters the housing through the duct on the left. Moving the cursor sideways changes how much dust the air carries.
  2. Housing in section. The front of the housing is cut away so the elements can be seen; only its back wall is drawn.
  3. MACROCORE™ primary element. The air crosses the pleated primary, which holds the dust on its pleats. The dust builds up as a layer on the upstream face.
  4. Safety element. Inside the primary, the safety element is the last barrier before the clean side.
  5. Outlet to the engine. Clean air leaves through the duct on the right toward the engine. Everything downstream of the elements has to stay sealed.
  6. Restriction indicator. On the head, the indicator rises as the primary loads; the readout shows the restriction as low, medium or high.
  7. Service. Moving the cursor to the top, or pressing E, runs the service: the cover comes down and the primary comes out, while the safety element stays in place and keeps the clean side closed. A new primary goes in and the restriction returns to low.
