# COMMERCIAL CONVERSION AUDIT
## ELIMFILTERS® — Distributor Acquisition Perspective
**Date:** 2026-06-04 · **Scope:** Full website commercial review

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The ELIMFILTERS® website presents an exceptionally strong technical identity. The brand is positioned with authority: ISO standards, quantified performance metrics, deep knowledge infrastructure, and a premium visual language. From a pure credibility standpoint, the site is more sophisticated than most industrial filtration competitors.

From a distributor acquisition standpoint, the commercial layer is incomplete. The site builds desire and establishes technical legitimacy, but stops before converting that interest into a committed distribution relationship. A motivated prospect who finds the distributor application page will submit a form and then hear silence — with no confirmation, no process, no expectations set, and no program economics communicated.

**Final Recommendation: B — Commercial flow requires improvement before active distributor recruitment.**

## 3. WHAT INFORMATION IS MISSING

The following information is expected by any qualified distributor evaluating a new brand partnership. None of it is present anywhere on the site.

### Commercial Program Terms

- **Wholesale pricing structure** — No margin framework, no discount tiers, no suggested retail pricing. A distributor cannot estimate unit economics before applying.
- **Minimum order quantities (MOQ)** — Not stated anywhere.
- **Exclusivity terms** — No indication of whether territories are exclusive, shared, or open.
- **Territory availability** — Which regions are already covered? Which are open? Is there a territory map?
- **Initial inventory requirement** — What does a new distributor need to invest to begin?

### Process Clarity

- **Application timeline** — The application page itself states no expected response time. (The contact page FAQ mentions "5 business days," but this is not visible from the application page.)
- **Post-approval onboarding** — No description of what happens after an application is approved. No mention of training structure, inventory ordering, first shipment, or account setup.
- **Who reviews the application** — No named contact, no commercial team identification.

### Product Information

- **No downloadable product catalog** — A distributor cannot review SKU counts, product ranges, or specifications before applying.
- **No product data sheets** — No downloadable technical specifications, no filter dimension tables, no cross-reference guides.
- **No SKU count** — "20,000+ OEM cross-references" is mentioned as a brand statistic but not as a distributor sales tool.

### Company Credibility

- **No leadership profiles** — No founders, no commercial team, no management bios.
- **No founding story or history** — "25+ years" is mentioned but no founding year, milestones, or growth narrative.
- **No existing distributor network information** — How many distributors? In which countries? A distributor evaluating exclusivity cannot assess territory saturation.
- **No certifications displayed** — ISO certification numbers, laboratory accreditation, or third-party test reports are not shown.
- **No case studies** — No documented customer deployments, no operational outcomes, no testimonials from end users.
- **No distributor testimonials** — No existing partners speaking to the experience of distributing ELIMFILTERS®.

## 7. CONVERSION OBSTACLES

### Critical (deal-breakers for qualified prospects)

1. **No program economics.** A distributor evaluating brand partnerships compares margin potential. Without any indication of wholesale pricing tiers or expected margins, ELIMFILTERS® cannot be compared against competing brands. This is the single largest conversion obstacle.

2. **No post-submission confirmation email.** A serious distributor submitting a formal application expects an immediate automated acknowledgment. Receiving nothing after 5 seconds (when the success message disappears) creates doubt about whether the application was received.

3. **No process communication.** The distributor application page explains nothing about the review process. Competing distributor programs typically show: "Step 1 → Application | Step 2 → Review Call | Step 3 → Approval | Step 4 → Onboarding." ELIMFILTERS® shows none of this.

### Significant (reduce conversion rate substantially)

4. **Low distributor CTA visibility.** The primary distributor CTA exists only in a rotating home section (5-second window, competes with Part Search CTA), in the footer under "Support," and indirectly on the Contact page. No technology page, no industry page, no knowledge system page, no warranty page carries a distributor CTA.

5. **Kleo Technologies identity gap.** The footer states "© 2015–2026 Kleo Technologies" — not ELIMFILTERS® LLC. A distributor doing basic due diligence will search for "Kleo Technologies" and find a different brand identity. The relationship between Kleo Technologies and ELIMFILTERS® is never explained on the site. This can appear either as a holding company structure (acceptable but unexplained) or as a brand with a hidden parent (creates doubt).

6. **No product catalog.** A distributor cannot assess range coverage, SKU count, or fill-rate potential without a product catalog. They cannot estimate territory sales potential or present the brand to their customers without spec sheets.

7. **Email-only contact.** For a distributor ready to move quickly, having only an email address creates friction. No direct phone line, no WhatsApp, no scheduled call booking tool.

### Moderate (friction points)

8. **Venezuela LATAM base.** The LATAM operations center in Barquisimeto, Venezuela is stated on the contact page. For distributors in Colombia, Brazil, Mexico, or Central America evaluating supply chain reliability, this may raise questions about logistics, currency, sanctions compliance, or operational continuity.

9. **Missing application field: industry focus.** The distributor form asks for current product lines and service area but does not ask which industries the distributor serves. This slows the matching process internally and signals that ELIMFILTERS® has not yet optimized its distributor qualification criteria.

10. **`employees` field defined in form state but not rendered.** The form state includes an `employees` field that is never shown to the user. This is a minor code inconsistency with no visible impact, but reflects that the distributor form may not have gone through a full qualification review.

11. **Form submission infrastructure.** The contact form uses formsubmit.co; the distributor form uses Formspree. Two different third-party services, both with free-tier rate limits. There is no indication of a CRM integration, no lead tracking, and no backup if either service fails.

## 9. IS THE CURRENT FLOW SUFFICIENT FOR DISTRIBUTOR ACQUISITION?

**No.** The current flow is a passive application intake, not an active distributor acquisition system.

The website successfully creates:
- Technical credibility
- Brand premium positioning
- A reason to believe the product is superior

The website does not yet deliver:
- A compelling commercial case for becoming a distributor
- The information needed to make a commitment decision
- A process that moves the prospect through stages toward activation
- Any indication that ELIMFILTERS® is actively seeking and evaluating distributors at scale

The current path for a distributor is: find the site → absorb technical content → find the application page → submit a form → wait indefinitely with no confirmation.

This is sufficient for receiving inbound interest from prospects who are already motivated. It is not sufficient for actively recruiting qualified distributors in target territories.

## STRENGTHS

1. **Unmatched technical depth.** The Knowledge System, 9 protection architectures, ISO standards integration, and quantified metrics give ELIMFILTERS® more technical authority than any major competitor's website. This is a genuine and defensible differentiation.

2. **Premium brand identity.** The visual language (dark/yellow, precision typography, motion design) positions ELIMFILTERS® as a premium brand, not a commodity supplier. This is a major commercial advantage: distributors selling premium protect margin.

3. **Part Search tool as a distributor selling weapon.** `part-search.elimfilters.com` is live and functional. This is a ready-made sales enablement tool for distributors that most competitor programs don't offer. It is not mentioned as a distributor benefit anywhere.

4. **Warranty program clarity.** The warranty page clearly documents non-prorated coverage, engine protection, and 24H response time. This is a strong distributor selling point and is documented well enough to be used in sales conversations.

5. **11-language support.** The site auto-detects and serves 11 languages. For a LATAM or European distributor, this means the end-customer they sell to can use the site in their native language. This is a genuine operational advantage that no competitor prominently advertises.

6. **20,000+ OEM cross-references.** This is the most commercially powerful statistic on the site for a distributor: it means broad coverage, easy part number lookups, and reduced friction in replacing incumbent brands.

7. **Application form captures qualified data.** The distributor application form (legal name, years in business, current product lines, service area) is well-designed for qualification purposes.

## WEAKNESSES

1. **Zero social proof.** No testimonials, no case studies, no named customers, no documented outcomes. Every performance claim (99.9% efficiency, +45% engine life, 25+ years) is self-declared with no third-party validation.

2. **No commercial program documentation.** The distributor application page lists six benefits but provides no actionable program terms. A prospect cannot evaluate the commercial opportunity before submitting.

3. **Post-submission black hole.** No confirmation email, no stated process, no timeline, no next steps. This is the most damaging single gap in the conversion flow.

4. **Distributor CTA buried and transient.** The primary distributor CTA competes with an unrelated CTA (Part Search) in a 5-second rotating panel. Footer placement requires the prospect to scroll to the bottom of the home page.

5. **Company identity unclear.** "ELIMFILTERS® LLC" vs. "Kleo Technologies" is never reconciled on the site. For a distributor doing legal due diligence, this gap is material.

6. **No downloadable resources.** No catalog, no data sheets, no cross-reference guides. A distributor cannot evaluate or present the brand to their customers before establishing an account.

7. **No phone contact.** Email-only creates friction for high-intent prospects and international distributors in time zones where email response latency creates delays.

8. **Venezuela LATAM operations as a potential liability.** The stated LATAM operations center location may require explanation for distributors evaluating supply chain, currency risk, and regulatory compliance.

## RISKS

1. **Lead loss with no visibility.** Qualified distributors may submit applications and hear nothing back promptly. With no CRM integration and two different form backends, there is no guarantee of follow-up, prioritization, or lead tracking. Each lost qualified distributor represents a multi-year revenue opportunity.

2. **Competitor displacement.** Donaldson, Fleetguard, Mann, and Baldwin all operate mature distributor programs with published program guides, online dealer portals, pricing tools, and regional sales managers. A distributor evaluating options will receive a detailed commercial package from competitors and an application form from ELIMFILTERS®.

3. **Credibility challenged by anonymity.** The "25+ years" claim, German engineering reference, and asset protection narrative are strong but unanchored. A distributor who cannot find the founders, the manufacturing base, or existing customer references may conclude the brand is newer or smaller than presented.

4. **Kleo Technologies discovery.** A distributor doing due diligence will search the company name, find "Kleo Technologies" in the footer, and potentially research that entity instead. If the relationship is not explained, this creates confusion that can abort the conversion process.

5. **Venezuela logistics hesitation.** LATAM distributors in Colombia, Brazil, Ecuador, or Chile evaluating ELIMFILTERS® as a supplier will ask whether the supply chain runs through Venezuela. If it does, they will raise questions about logistics reliability, currency conversion, and trade compliance. If it does not, the listed address may still raise the question unnecessarily.

6. **Unreliable form backends.** Formspree free tier and formsubmit.co both have submission rate limits. If volume increases during an active distributor recruitment campaign, form submissions may be throttled or lost.

## OPPORTUNITIES

1. **Knowledge System as the distributor's sales toolkit.** No competitor offers a distributor a knowledge system like this. The 30 technical pages, ISO standards documentation, and contamination case studies are tools a distributor sales team can use with customers. This should be explicitly positioned as a distributor commercial advantage.

2. **Part Search tool as a territory management asset.** The Part Search platform at `part-search.elimfilters.com` enables a distributor to support any customer lookup in real time. This deserves a dedicated mention in the distributor program: "Your customers can search 20,000+ OEM cross-references from any device."

3. **Multi-language site as a LATAM distributor advantage.** A LATAM distributor selling to Portuguese or Spanish-speaking operators can send them to the ELIMFILTERS® website in their own language. This is an unusually strong localization advantage that should be communicated.

4. **Premium-sell positioning reduces distributor price pressure.** The technical authority of the brand enables distributors to sell on performance, not price. This is the most important commercial message ELIMFILTERS® can deliver to a prospective distributor: "You will never compete on price with us. You will compete on contamination control ROI." This message is implicit in the site but never stated to a distributor audience.

5. **Warranty program as a distributor closing tool.** Engine protection coverage as part of the warranty is extremely rare in aftermarket filtration. This is a closing argument in distributor sales conversations ("your customers' engines are covered if anything goes wrong") that is not being exploited in the distributor recruitment flow.

6. **12-industry coverage creates cross-sell depth.** A distributor in the mining sector who adds ELIMFILTERS® gains access to 11 other industries with the same product family. Most filtration distributors serve 2–3 industries. This is a multi-vertical revenue opportunity that should be quantified and communicated on the distributor page.

---

## FINAL RECOMMENDATION

**B — Commercial flow requires improvement before active distributor recruitment.**

The technical and brand foundation is exceptionally strong. ELIMFILTERS® can credibly compete against established industrial filtration brands and win on technical authority.

However, launching active distributor recruitment with the current commercial flow will produce two outcomes: low conversion rates from qualified prospects, and poor post-submission follow-through due to the absence of a defined intake and response process.

The minimum viable improvements required before active recruitment:

1. Add a response confirmation email after distributor application submission
2. Add the review timeline and "what happens next" to the distributor application page
3. Publish a distributor program one-pager (not a price list — just program structure, territory concept, benefits, and process)
4. Resolve the Kleo Technologies / ELIMFILTERS® LLC relationship publicly
5. Place a distributor CTA on at least three additional pages (Technologies, Industries, Warranty)

These are presentation and process changes, not structural changes. The product, technical infrastructure, and brand are ready. The commercial intake layer is not.
